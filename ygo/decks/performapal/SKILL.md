- **Core Mechanic: Pendulum Scale Engine**

- Set scale 1 + scale 8 (天使女士 58938528 + 粗鲁先生 21949879, or 琴键猴 17330916 + 异色眼贴身男仆 82661461) to Pendulum Summon Levels 2-7 in one action, then convert the bodies into links, fusions and Xyz
- 粗鲁先生 21949879 scale effect adds any 异色眼 card from deck to hand, but only while 天使女士 58938528 is on the other scale and own field has no monsters or only Pendulum monsters
- The engine is a self-destruction loop: 灵摆魔术家 47075569 on special summon destroys up to 2 own cards to add that many 娱乐伙伴 from deck (one of each name); 粗鲁先生 21949879 destroyed sets a 娱乐伙伴 Pendulum from deck into a scale; 宙读之魔术士 76794549 in hand special summons itself when own card is destroyed and adds a copy of the destroyed monster from deck
- 银金公主 24094258 on link summon adds a Pendulum from deck face-up to the extra deck; its ignition effect destroys own card to add a face-up Pendulum from extra to hand; it draws 1 whenever a scale leaves the field
- Grave-to-scale recursion: 天使女士 58938528 places itself into a scale from the grave while an 异色眼 card or 粗鲁先生 21949879 is on field; 异色眼管家 56677752 special summons itself from hand or grave by destroying own 娱乐伙伴 or 异色眼 card; 异色眼祭司 4836680 scale effect adds a 娱乐伙伴 or 异色眼 card from grave to hand then destroys itself
- Fusion finish: 异色眼融解者 46136942 fuses from hand and field as a scale, or from field including scale cards as a monster, into 异色眼风雷龙 53262004 (异色眼 monster + Pendulum monster) or 霸王眷龙 凶饿毒 43387895 (2 DARK Pendulum monsters, or tribute 2 face-up DARK Pendulums)

- **One-Card Combo: 骷髅杂技小丑 40318957**

- Step 1: normal summon 骷髅杂技小丑 40318957 and search 琴键猴 17330916 from deck
- Step 2: activate 琴键猴 17330916 as a scale (scale 1), then its scale effect adds a Level 4 or lower 娱乐伙伴, typically 灵摆魔术家 47075569 or 粗鲁先生 21949879
- Step 3: set 粗鲁先生 21949879 as the other scale (scale 8), Pendulum Summon 骷髅杂技小丑 40318957 and the searched Level 4 娱乐伙伴
- Step 4: 灵摆魔术家 47075569 on special summon destroys up to 2 own cards and adds the same number of 娱乐伙伴 from deck, for example 天使女士 58938528 and 异色眼贴身男仆 82661461
- Step 5: link 2 Pendulums into 银金公主 24094258, add a Pendulum face-up from deck to extra, then destroy a scale to add a face-up Pendulum from extra to hand (银金公主 24094258 also draws when the scale leaves)
- Step 6: set 天使女士 58938528 as a scale, then 粗鲁先生 21949879 scale effect adds 异色眼同调士 82224646 or 异色眼融解者 46136942
- Step 7: summon 异色眼贴身男仆 82661461 to dump 天使女士 58938528 or 异色眼管家 56677752 to the grave for recursion; 异色眼管家 56677752 special summons itself by destroying an own 娱乐伙伴 or 异色眼 card
- Step 8: 异色眼融解者 46136942 monster effect fuses itself with a Pendulum (field or scale) into 异色眼风雷龙 53262004
- Requires 琴键猴 17330916 in deck, which is why builds run it at 3; the line collapses if 灰流丽 14558127 hits Step 1 or 2

- The 骷髅杂技小丑 40318957 normal summon decides the game; hold 灰流丽 14558127 for the opponent's 骷髅杂技小丑 40318957 or 琴键猴 17330916 search and 小丑与锁鸟 94145021 to end their search chain
- Whoever resolves 银金公主 24094258 with the 灵摆魔术家 47075569 pop loop first keeps the resource lead; keep 墓穴的指名者 24224830 for the opponent's hand traps
- Both decks recur through scales and grave (天使女士 58938528, 异色眼管家 56677752, 粗鲁先生 21949879), so remove the opponent's scale zones with 群豪世界-百识公国 75952542 and banish their grave with 深渊的潜伏者 21044178 or D.D.乌鸦 24508238
- 异色眼风雷龙 53262004 forces the opponent to shuffle a face-up Pendulum from their extra deck to negate; deny them face-up Pendulums by keeping your own 银金公主 24094258 live and their scales broken

- **Common Mistakes**

- Pendulum Summoning 威风妖怪 31991800 or 51073802 while 骷髅杂技小丑 40318957 scale is up: its restriction only allows 娱乐伙伴, 魔术师 Pendulum, or 异色眼 Pendulum Summons
- Using 粗鲁先生 21949879 scale search at the wrong time: it needs 天使女士 58938528 on the other scale and a field with no monsters or only Pendulum monsters
- Triggering 轨迹之魔术师 22125101 search without Pendulum Summoning afterward: until a Pendulum Summon succeeds this turn your monster effects cannot be activated and scale effects are negated
- Destroying both scales with 灵摆魔术家 47075569 or 银金公主 24094258, leaving no Pendulum Summon available; always keep at least one scale
- Forgetting 异色眼风雷龙 53262004 negation needs a face-up Pendulum in the extra deck to shuffle back; keep one loaded via 银金公主 24094258
- Using the extra-deck-summoned 异色眼同调士 82224646 as Synchro material banishes it; use the hand-summoned copy for the Synchro line
- 异色眼管家 56677752 destroys an own 娱乐伙伴 or 异色眼 card to summon itself; do not target the only remaining scale or a card you cannot afford to lose
- 天使女士 58938528 scale effect discards a Pendulum monster from hand; do not empty the hand of Pendulums before the opponent's battle phase
- 五虹之魔术师 19619755 scale 12 disables attacks and monster effects while you control no face-down Spell or Trap cards; only set it with face-downs ready
- Over-extending into 增殖的G 23434538 or 欢聚友伴·茸茸长尾山雀 42141493; the deck special summons in bulk every turn and a resolved Maxx C line hands the opponent many draws
- 宙读之魔术士 76794549 needs an own card destroyed to trigger; plan the self-destruction (灵摆魔术家 47075569 pop) before you need its search
