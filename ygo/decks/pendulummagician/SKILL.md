# 魔术师 (Pendulum Magician) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- DARK Spellcaster pendulum archetype built around 魔术师 scale 1 and scale 8 monsters, splashing 娱乐伙伴 and 异色眼 cards in every real build
- Core scale 1 monsters: 星读之魔术师 94415058, 宙读之魔术士 76794549, 星霜之魔术师-宙读之魔术士 48171151, 星读之魔术师-星占之魔术士 1186447, 娱乐伙伴 琴键猴 17330916
- Core scale 8 monsters: 时读之魔术师 20409757, 刻读之魔术士 12289247, 娱乐伙伴 骷髅杂技小丑 40318957, 娱乐伙伴 异色眼贴身男仆 82661461
- Key spells: 灵摆呼唤 53208660, 时空之灵摆读阵 1344018, 星霜之灵摆读阵 74850403, 黑魔导的幕帘 41350417, 蒂迈欧之眼光 22283204
- Extra deck engine: 轨迹之魔术师 22125101, 刚炼装勇士·银金公主 24094258, 奇迹之魔导剑士 92812851, 星刻之魔术师 47349116

- 灵摆召唤支持 in the task likely refers to 灵摆呼唤 53208660, the deck's dedicated scale-fetch spell

- **Core Mechanic: Pendulum Scale Engine**

- Set one low scale (1) and one high scale (8) in the pendulum zones, then pendulum summon every Level 2-7 monster from hand in one action
- Pendulum summon count equals scale difference plus one, so scale 1 and scale 8 summon up to 8 monsters, this is the swarm engine
- 时读之魔术师 20409757 and 星读之魔术师 94415058 both change their scale to 4 when the other pendulum zone card is not a 魔术师 or 异色眼 card, keep them paired with a 魔术师 or 异色眼 to hold the 1-8 spread
- 娱乐伙伴 琴键猴 17330916 changes its scale to 4 when no other 娱乐伙伴 is in the pendulum zone, keep another 娱乐伙伴 like 骷髅杂技小丑 40318957 in the other zone to hold it at scale 1
- Scale-setting pend effects destroy themselves and fetch a replacement, these self-destructions trigger the hand effects of 宙读之魔术士 76794549 and 刻读之魔术士 12289247
- 星读之魔术师-星占之魔术士 1186447 pendulum effect negates an opponent spell effect when you control a 魔术师 pendulum or 异色眼 monster, then destroys itself, a free spell negate
- Destroyed pendulum monsters go face-up to the extra deck instead of the grave, so every destroyed scale and monster is a future pendulum summon target
- 骷髅杂技小丑 40318957 in the pendulum zone restricts your pendulum summons to 娱乐伙伴, 魔术师 pendulum, and 异色眼 monsters only

- **One-Card Combo: 骷髅杂技小丑**

- Starter: 娱乐伙伴 骷髅杂技小丑 40318957 normal summoned with no other cards, it searches any 娱乐伙伴, 魔术师 pendulum, or 异色眼 monster
- Step 1: normal summon 骷髅杂技小丑 40318957 and search 星读之魔术师-星占之魔术士 1186447
- Step 2: activate 星占之魔术士 1186447 effect one, discard one other 魔术师, 娱乐伙伴, or 异色眼 monster, special summon it from hand
- Step 3: its effect two adds a 2500 ATK pendulum monster from deck, take 四天之龙 异色眼灵摆龙 75787708
- Step 4: set 星占之魔术士 1186447 as scale 1 and 时读之魔术师 20409757 as scale 8
- Step 5: pendulum summon 四天之龙 异色眼灵摆龙 75787708, it gains 300 ATK per scale difference and doubles battle damage when pendulum summoned
- Step 6: 四天之龙 scale effect destroys itself to add a 1500 or lower ATK pendulum monster from deck, but unless you pendulum summon this turn you cannot activate field monster effects, so make sure the turn ends with a pendulum summon to lift the restriction
- Step 7: link 星占之魔术士 1186447 and one pendulum monster into 轨迹之魔术师 22125101 in the extra monster zone
- Step 8: pay 1200 LP and search any pendulum monster from deck, this searches 宙读之魔术士 76794549 or 星霜之魔术师-宙读之魔术士 48171151
- Step 9: unless you pendulum summon this turn, your monster effects and pendulum zone card effects are negated, so if you have not pendulum summoned yet, do it immediately after the search to lift the lock
- Step 10: link two pendulum monsters into 刚炼装勇士·银金公主 24094258, add one pendulum monster from deck to the extra deck face-up, then destroy one card you control to add a face-up pendulum from the extra deck to hand
- Step 11: link into 奇迹之魔导剑士 92812851, it adds a face-up pendulum monster from the extra deck to hand on link summon
- Step 12: 星刻之魔术师 47349116 searches any DARK Spellcaster from deck, grave, or face-up extra deck, use it to grab 黑魔术师 46986414 for the fusion line
- Step 13: 黑魔导的幕帘 41350417 special summons 黑魔术师 46986414 from deck and fetches 蒂迈欧之眼光 22283204, which fuses 超魔导龙骑士-真红眼龙骑士 37818794 using only that one material
- Halt point: the combo dies to 灰流丽 14558127 on the 骷髅杂技小丑 search, the 星占之魔术士 1186447 search, or the 轨迹之魔术师 22125101 search

- **Extender: 宙读之魔术士 76794549**

- When any of your cards is destroyed by battle or effect, special summon it from hand, then add one copy of a monster destroyed this turn from deck to hand
- Its scale effect destroys itself to place or summon 星读之魔术师 94415058 from hand or deck, and the self-destruction refunds the special summon trigger
- Effect two banishes one 灵摆龙, 超量龙, 同调龙, and 融合龙 monster plus itself to fusion summon 霸王龙 扎克 13331639

- **Extender: 刻读之魔术士 12289247**

- When any of your cards is destroyed by battle or effect, special summon it from hand, then special summon one monster from hand
- Its scale effect destroys itself to place or summon 时读之魔术师 20409757 from hand or deck
- Same 霸王龙 扎克 13331639 fusion line as 宙读之魔术士 76794549, useful only in the 霸王 builds

- **Extender: 星霜之魔术师-宙读之魔术士 48171151**

- When a face-up monster card you control is destroyed by battle or effect, special summon it from hand, then revive one of the destroyed monsters from the face-up extra deck or grave
- Effect two is a quick effect during either main phase that tributes itself to fusion summon a Dragon fusion using field or face-up extra deck monsters returned to deck, this makes 异色眼风雷龙 53262004 mid-combo
- Its scale effect destroys itself to place another scale 1 pendulum monster from hand or deck in the pendulum zone, the placed card has its pendulum effect negated this turn

- **Extender: 娱乐伙伴 琴键猴 17330916**

- 娱乐伙伴 天使女士 58938528 discards itself and another 娱乐伙伴 monster to draw two, and sets itself to the pendulum zone from grave when an 异色眼 card is on field

- **Extender: 灵摆呼唤 53208660**

- Discard one card, add two different 魔术师 pendulum monsters from deck to hand, the standard two-scale setup card
- Cannot be activated in a turn where you already activated a 魔术师 pendulum monster pendulum effect, the custom activity counter in c53208660.lua enforces this, so activate it before using scale effects
- After resolution your 魔术师 pendulum zone cards cannot be destroyed by effects until the end of the opponent next turn, which also shuts off your own 时空之灵摆读阵 1344018 self-destruction triggers that turn

- **Extender: 时空之灵摆读阵 1344018 and 星霜之灵摆读阵 74850403**

- 时空之灵摆读阵 1344018 field spell makes your Spellcasters untargetable by trap effects, and its quick effect destroys one of your 魔术师 pendulum cards plus one opponent card, the self-destruction feeds the hand triggers of 宙读之魔术士 76794549 and 刻读之魔术士 12289247
- 星霜之灵摆读阵 74850403 field spell makes your Spellcasters untargetable by spell effects, and searches a 魔术师 pendulum monster whenever a face-up 魔术师 pendulum card leaves your field or pendulum zone
- Play only one of the two field spells, they share the same protection role and the second copy is dead

- The player who resolves 骷髅杂技小丑 40318957 or 灵摆呼唤 53208660 first controls the scale spread, whoever pendulum summons more Level 7 monsters first wins the race

- 星读之魔术师-星占之魔术士 1186447 pendulum effect negates an opponent spell, use it against their 灵摆呼唤 53208660

- **Common Mistakes**

- 灵摆呼唤 53208660 is illegal if a 魔术师 pendulum monster pendulum effect was activated earlier in the turn, sequence the search spells before any scale effect
- 轨迹之魔术师 22125101 search locks monster effects and pendulum zone effects until you pendulum summon, always pendulum summon right after the search
- 四天之龙 异色眼灵摆龙 75787708 scale search blocks field monster effects unless you pendulum summon, do not use its search before the pendulum summon
- 骷髅杂技小丑 40318957 in the pendulum zone limits pendulum summons to 娱乐伙伴, 魔术师 pendulum, and 异色眼 only, no stray non-archetype monsters
- 星读之魔术师 94415058 and 时读之魔术师 20409757 drop to scale 4 when the other pendulum zone is not 魔术师 or 异色眼, never pair them with an unrelated scale
- Pendulum monsters destroyed from field or pendulum zone go to the face-up extra deck, do not send them to grave with cost effects and lose the resource
- 调弦之魔术师 73941492 cannot be special summoned from the extra deck and is banished when it leaves the field after being summoned from deck, it is a one-shot synchro enabler only
- 星刻之魔术师 47349116 requires exactly two Level 4 魔术师 pendulum monsters as material, it is not a generic Rank 4
- 黑魔导的幕帘 41350417 gives the opponent a free DARK Spellcaster too, only activate it when the trade is favorable or the fusion is lethal
- 蒂迈欧之眼光 22283204 fusion is banished at the end of the next turn, do not rely on 超魔导龙骑士-真红眼龙骑士 37818794 surviving into the long game
- 星霜之魔术师-宙读之魔术士 48171151 quick fusion returns materials to deck, check the deck count before activating so you do not deck out
- Play under 增殖的G 23434538 and 欢聚友伴 hand traps with a two or three special summon line into 异色眼风雷龙 53262004 instead of the full swarm
