# 救祓少女 (Exosister) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- Main deck monsters: 马尔法 37343995, 埃莉丝 16474916, 斯特拉 43863925, 伊雷娜 79858629, 索菲娅 5352328, all Level 4 Light with 800 DEF
- Extra deck: 米迦埃莉丝 42741437, 卡尔麦尔 77675029, 卡斯皮特尔 78135071, 阿索菲勒 41524885, 吉卜利娜 5530780, 圣母颂歌 59242457
- Key spells: 和平问候 77913594, 圣母悼歌 52335937, 连祷 197042, 共赴何方 77891946
- Searcher: 斋狭依姬 9627299 searches any Level 4 Light or Dark monster with 800 ATK or DEF, which includes every Exosister main monster

- **Core Mechanic: Overlay Trigger**

- Every main deck Exosister monster has a second effect that Xyz summons any Exosister Xyz monster on top of itself
- The trigger fires only when a card leaves a graveyard and the opponent caused it, verified in scripts as rp equals opponent, so it is a reactive counter engine not a self-combo engine
- Each monster has its own count limit so multiple different main deck monsters on field can each overlay once during the same opponent grave play
- The overlay summon uses the monster as material and works from the field, not the hand

- **One-Card Combo: 马尔法**

- Starter: 马尔法 37343995 in hand, no other cards needed
- Step 1: activate 马尔法 effect one on empty field or with only Xyz monsters, special summon itself and 埃莉丝 16474916 from deck
- Step 2: Xyz both into 米迦埃莉丝 42741437, detach one material to search 圣母悼歌 52335937 which is a continuous spell that extends this turn
- Step 3: activate 圣母悼歌 effect one, add 斯特拉 43863925 and 索菲娅 5352328 from deck to hand, discard one card
- Step 4: normal summon 斯特拉, activate its effect one to special summon 索菲娅 from hand
- Step 5: activate 索菲娅 effect one to draw one card, gaining extra hand advantage
- Step 6: Xyz 斯特拉 and 索菲娅 into 卡尔麦尔 77675029, its effect one sets a 救祓少女 spell or trap at end phase
- Step 7: Xyz 米迦埃莉丝 and 卡尔麦尔 into 圣母颂歌 59242457
- Step 8: end phase, 卡尔麦尔 lingering set effect places 连祷 197042 or 共赴何方 77891946 from deck

- **End Field One-Card**

- 圣母颂歌 59242457 with double attack, one quick banish, and one chain dodge
- One set trap from 卡尔麦尔, one 圣母悼歌 face up, plus one extra card in hand from 索菲娅 draw
- Halt point: Ash Blossom on 马尔法 effect one leaves one monster, Ash on 米迦埃莉丝 search or 圣母悼歌 stops extension

- **Extender: 和平问候 77913594**

- Activate in main phase, pay 800 LP, add any 救祓少女 card from deck to hand
- If the added card is a monster and a monster that lists its name is on field or grave, also special summon the added monster
- Example: add 马尔法 and special summon it when 埃莉丝 is on field or grave because 马尔法 lists 埃莉丝

- **Extender: 斋狭依姬 9627299**

- Effect one gives an extra normal summon when an 800 stat monster is on field, effect two searches any Level 4 Light or Dark 800 stat monster
- Not a one-card starter because it needs an 800 stat monster already on field, use as the second card after 马尔法 or 埃莉丝

- **Mirror Match: 救祓少女 vs 救祓少女**

- Never touch a graveyard with your effects in the mirror, any card leaving a grave triggered by you activates the opponent main deck monsters overlay into Xyz for free
- Avoid 落胤与圣女 destroy effect targeting grave-sourced plays and 霆王的闪光 banish of grave-relevant cards unless the overlay trade is favorable
- Prioritize removing the opponent main deck monsters before they overlay, their second effects are the engine, no main monster means no overlay
- 圣母悼歌 third effect banishes a monster sent to the opponent grave, use it to strip their overlay materials and search targets
- Keep your own main deck Exosister monsters on field as reactive overlay threats during the opponent turn

- **Playing Under 增殖的G and 欢聚友伴**

- Do not play the full combo while 增殖的G or 欢聚友伴 茸茸长尾山雀 is active, every extra special summon hands the opponent a draw
- Compromise line under G: 马尔法 into 米迦埃莉丝 only, use the search to set up backrow, keep special summons to two or three
- 次元吸引者 91800273 can be chained to G to shut down draws and grave synergy in the same turn, consider it before extending

- **Common Mistakes**

- Do not search a trap like 连祷 or 共赴何方 as the mid-combo search, traps cannot activate the turn they are set or searched
- Prefer 圣母悼歌 or 和平问候 as the 米迦埃莉丝 search because both extend the same turn
- 斯特拉 special summons from hand, so ensure the target monster is in hand before activating its effect
- 马尔法 effect one requires empty field or only Xyz monsters, do not normal summon first
- 卡尔麦尔 end phase set effect is a lingering effect that resolves even after 卡尔麦尔 becomes Xyz material
- 圣母颂歌 requires two Rank 4 Exosister Xyz monsters as material, stack 米迦埃莉丝 and 卡尔麦尔
- Normal summon 索菲娅 only for its draw, a 100 attack monster is a weak beater, prefer summoning or setting 埃莉丝 or 斯特拉 as a body
- 落胤与圣女 extra deck cost order matters: send 白界龙 87746184 first because its end phase effect searches another 落胤与圣女, send 黑龙之艾克莉西娅 78397661 second because its grave effect recycles 白界龙
