# 帝 (Monarch) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- Tribute summon deck that runs an empty extra deck by design: every squire trigger and the key locks forbid extra deck summons, and the payoff cards punish opponents who rely on the extra deck
- Modern pure build (deck/250426帝王): squire engine 天帝从骑 爱迪娅 95457011, 冥帝从骑 哀多斯 59463312, 源帝从骑 特塞拉 67584223 plus bosses 天帝 埃忒耳 96570609, 冥帝 厄瑞玻斯 23064604, 冥帝王 哀多斯 31596518
- Bosses are LIGHT or DARK: 天帝 埃忒耳 96570609 is LIGHT Fairy, 冥帝 厄瑞玻斯 23064604 is DARK Zombie, 冥帝王 哀多斯 31596518 is LIGHT Fairy, all 2800/1000 or 2400/1000
- Attribute monarch toolbox searched by 帝王的策略 63899196: 冰帝 美比乌斯 4929256, 邪帝 盖乌斯 9748752, 炎帝 泰斯塔罗斯 26205777, 雷帝 扎博尔格 51945556, 地帝 格兰玛格 60229110, 风帝 莱扎 73125233
- Floodgate fiends as one-tribute summons: 虚无魔人 47084486 stops all special summons, 威光魔人 33746252 stops all monster effect activations, both level 6
- Spell package: 帝王的深怨 33609262, 泛神的帝王 22842126, 叛逆的帝王 9283801, 帝王的策略 63899196, 真帝王领域 84171830, 帝王的烈旋 79844764, 魔法卡「灵魂交错」15735108, no main deck hand traps, generic side cards 增殖的G 23434538 and 墓穴的指名者 24224830
- DB quirk: 水陆的帝王 11250655 is a level 5 WATER vanilla carrying the 帝王 setcode, pure builds skip it; 210925帝王 is a spell-heavy variant playing every 帝王-named card

- **Core Mechanic: Tribute Engine**

- Squire engine: 天帝从骑 爱迪娅 95457011 on summon special summons one 800/1000 monster from deck; 冥帝从骑 哀多斯 59463312 grants one extra tribute summon the turn it is summoned; 源帝从骑 特塞拉 67584223 reveals a 帝王 spell or trap to summon itself then performs a tribute summon
- Every squire summon effect resolves with "cannot special summon from the extra deck this turn", so the engine commits the deck to no extra deck
- Bosses with one-tribute summon proc by releasing one of your tribute-summoned monsters: 天帝 埃忒耳 96570609, 冥帝 厄瑞玻斯 23064604, 爆炎帝 泰斯塔罗斯 69230391, tribute summons chain into each other in one turn
- 天帝 埃忒耳 96570609 on tribute summon sends two different 帝王 spells or traps from hand or deck to graveyard, then special summons one 2400 or higher ATK 1000 DEF monster from deck that returns to hand at the end phase
- 天帝 埃忒耳 96570609 quick effect during the opponent main phase banishes one 帝王 spell or trap from graveyard to tribute summon itself from hand, the deck main interrupt
- 冥帝 厄瑞玻斯 23064604 on tribute summon sends two 帝王 spells or traps to graveyard and shuffles one opponent card from hand random, field, or graveyard into the deck; its graveyard effect discards one 帝王 spell or trap to recycle a 2400 or higher 1000 DEF monster
- No-extra floodgates: 真帝王领域 84171830 forbids opponent extra deck summons while you control a tribute-summoned monster and they control none, boosts your tribute-summoned monsters 800 ATK in damage calculation, and once per turn drops a 2800/1000 monster in hand two levels for a one-tribute summon
- 帝王的熔击 48716527 continuous trap negates every face-up monster that is not tribute summoned while you control a tribute-summoned monster with an empty extra deck, self-destroys at your end phase if the monster is gone

- **One-Card Combo: 天帝从骑 爱迪娅 95457011**

- Step 1: normal summon 天帝从骑 爱迪娅 95457011, effect one special summons 冥帝从骑 哀多斯 59463312 from deck in defense
- Step 2: 冥帝从骑 哀多斯 59463312 effect one grants one extra tribute summon this turn
- Step 3: tribute both squires to tribute summon 天帝 埃忒耳 96570609 from hand
- Step 4: 天帝 埃忒耳 96570609 effect one sends two different 帝王 spells or traps such as 帝王的深怨 33609262 and 泛神的帝王 22842126 from hand or deck to graveyard and special summons 冥帝 厄瑞玻斯 23064604 from deck
- Step 5: 冥帝从骑 哀多斯 59463312 graveyard effect banishes itself to special summon 天帝从骑 爱迪娅 95457011 back from graveyard in defense
- Step 6: with the extra tribute summon, tribute 天帝从骑 爱迪娅 95457011 alone to tribute summon 冥帝王 哀多斯 31596518, a level 6 monster that needs only one tribute
- Step 7: 冥帝王 哀多斯 31596518 effect one searches 真帝王领域 84171830 or 帝王的熔击 48716527 from deck or graveyard and set it
- Step 8: 天帝从骑 爱迪娅 95457011 graveyard effect retrieves one banished 帝王 spell or trap such as 泛神的帝王 22842126 after its graveyard effect

- **End Field**

- Base line leaves 天帝 埃忒耳 96570609, 冥帝 厄瑞玻斯 23064604, and 冥帝王 哀多斯 31596518 on field with 真帝王领域 84171830 set, the special summoned 冥帝 厄瑞玻斯 23064604 returns to hand at the end phase
- 真帝王领域 84171830 locks opponent extra deck summons while 帝王的熔击 48716527 set negates all non-tribute-summoned monsters, two to four 帝王 spells or traps sit in graveyard
- Lockdown variant: spend the extra tribute summon on 虚无魔人 47084486 or 威光魔人 33746252 instead of 冥帝王 哀多斯 31596518 to forbid all special summons or all monster effect activations
- 天帝 埃忒耳 96570609 can special summon 威光魔人 33746252 directly from deck as its summon target for an instant effect-activation lock, but it ends your own monster effect activations so grab it only as the last summon
- Halt point: 增殖的G draws three cards across this line but the line special summons only three times so 原始生命态 尼比鲁 27204311 cannot trigger

- **Extender: 源帝从骑 特塞拉 67584223**

- Reveal one 帝王 spell or trap from hand to special summon itself, then its field effect tribute summons one monster, when it hits the graveyard it special summons another 800/1000 monster from deck
- One-card tribute summon engine when a spare 帝王 spell or trap exists in hand such as 帝王的深怨 33609262 before activation
- Graveyard trigger locks extra deck summons for the turn, acceptable because the deck runs no extra deck

- **Extender: 帝王的深怨 33609262 + 泛神的帝王 22842126**

- 帝王的深怨 33609262 reveals a 2400/1000 or 2800/1000 monster in hand to add any 帝王 spell or trap except itself from deck
- 泛神的帝王 22842126 discards one 帝王 spell or trap to draw two cards, its graveyard effect banishes itself to reveal three 帝王 spells or traps and the opponent picks one to add
- Search 帝王的深怨 33609262 for backrow, then 泛神的帝王 22842126 for draw power, then 叛逆的帝王 9283801 for monsters

- **Extender: 叛逆的帝王 9283801**

- Discard one card to reveal three monsters with 2400 or higher or 800 ATK and 1000 DEF from deck, the opponent picks one to hand and the rest go to graveyard
- Graveyard effect banishes itself to special summon one 800/1000 monster from hand for the next tribute summon
- Loads 冥帝从骑 哀多斯 59463312 and 天帝从骑 爱迪娅 95457011 into the graveyard for their recovery effects

- **Extender: 帝王的策略 63899196**

- On activation sends one 帝王 spell or trap from deck to graveyard to fuel 天帝 埃忒耳 96570609 quick cost and 泛神的帝王 22842126 graveyard effect
- When banished, target an opponent face-up monster, add a 2400/1000 monarch of the same attribute from deck or graveyard, then may tribute summon that monarch
- Attribute control enables it: 帝王的轰毅 5795980 graveyard effect changes all face-up monsters to one declared attribute and 冥帝王 哀多斯 31596518 effect two changes one monster attribute

- 真源的帝王 54241725 quick effect shuffles two 帝王 spells or traps from graveyard to deck to draw one, graveyard effect banishes one 帝王 spell or trap to special summon itself as a 1000/2400 monster
- 帝王的牲祭 72237166 is a level 2 monster that returns to hand whenever it is tributed for a tribute summon, endless tribute fodder

- **Halt Points**

- Ash Blossom on 天帝从骑 爱迪娅 95457011 effect one or 天帝 埃忒耳 96570609 effect one kills the special summons and the two card dump, the common choke points
- 效果遮蒙者 or 无限泡影 on 源帝从骑 特塞拉 67584223 effect two cancels the tribute summon, on 冥帝王 哀多斯 31596518 effect one cancels the search
- 墓穴的指名者 24224830 on 冥帝从骑 哀多斯 59463312 or 天帝从骑 爱迪娅 95457011 in the graveyard kills recursion, banish effects remove the squires before they resolve
- 鹰身女妖的羽毛扫 18144506 and 宇宙旋风 8267140 on 真帝王领域 84171830 and 帝王的熔击 48716527 dismantle the lock, any removal of your tribute-summoned monster also ends 帝王的熔击 48716527

- The deck special summons only two to three times per line so 增殖的G draws few cards and 原始生命态 尼比鲁 27204311 usually cannot trigger

- **Common Mistakes**

- Do not special summon from the extra deck in this deck, 真帝王领域 84171830, 帝王的熔击 48716527, 帝王的开岩 61466310, and every squire trigger forbid or punish it
- 帝王的烈旋 79844764 in this database only lets one tribute summon use an opponent monster as tribute, it does not grant an extra tribute summon
- 天帝 埃忒耳 96570609 effect one special summoned monster returns to hand at the end phase, plan to tribute it away before then if it should stay in the graveyard
- 魔法卡「灵魂交错」15735108 effect two lets the opponent use your monsters as tributes if this card leaves the field, do not set it into backrow removal
- 帝王的轰毅 5795980 costs a face-up level 5 or higher normal summoned monster, a monster special summoned by 天帝 埃忒耳 96570609 does not qualify as cost
- 威光魔人 33746252 stops both players from activating monster effects, resolve 天帝 埃忒耳 96570609 and 冥帝王 哀多斯 31596518 chains before summoning it
- 始源的帝王 8522996 locks your special summons to its declared attribute, declare LIGHT when the squire engine still needs to summon
- 再临的帝王 98552723 revives with negated effects, use it for the double tribute value not for the revived monster summon effect
- 冥帝 厄瑞玻斯 23064604 and 天帝 埃忒耳 96570609 on-summon effects have no once-per-turn limit in the scripts, tribute summoning the same boss twice fires the effect twice
