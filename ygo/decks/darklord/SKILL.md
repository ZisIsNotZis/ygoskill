# 堕天使 (Darklord) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- LIGHT/DARK Fairy archetype, setcode 0xef, big Level 6-11 monsters with heavy graveyard recursion and repeatable spell effects, reference near-pure build 201031堕天使
- Engine monsters: 苏泊比亚 11260714 (Lv8), 莫斯提马 25339070 (Lv7), 伊希塔布 52840267 (Lv10), 安度西亚斯 51728779 (Lv6), 内尔伽勒 18168997 (Lv8), 特斯卡特利波卡 88234365 (Lv9), 杰拉特 40921744 (Lv8), 阿斯蒙蒂斯 85771019 (Lv8), 迪赛尔 55690251 (Lv10), 乌科巴克 92807548 (Lv3), 骼骼他 84031359 (Lv6), 杰胡提 10426067 (Lv4), 埃地·阿拉耶 47664723 (Lv5)

- **Core Mechanic: 苏泊比亚 Grave Revival Engine**

- 苏泊比亚 11260714 revives 1 other Fairy from your grave when it is special summoned from the grave, so every revival source chains into a second body
- Grave is the fuel: 乌科巴克 92807548 mills 1 堕天使 card on summon, 愚蠢的副葬 35726888 mills any spell or trap, 抵价购物 38120068 and 暗之诱惑 1475311 discard, 名推理 58577036 and 手札抹杀 72892473 dump the hand
- Revival sources: 戒坛 14517422, 堕天使降临 58787301, 死者苏生 83764718, 黎明之堕天使 路西菲尔 4167084 quick effect, 黄昏之堕天使 路西法 10136446 copied effects
- 苏泊比亚 11260714 needs another Fairy in the grave, so mill or discard a second Darklord before reviving it, the ideal order is 苏泊比亚 first then any other Fairy

- **Core Mechanic: 1000-LP Copy Family**

- 莫斯提马 25339070, 伊希塔布 52840267, 安度西亚斯 51728779, 特斯卡特利波卡 88234365, 内尔伽勒 18168997 and 黄昏之堕天使 路西法 10136446 pay 1000 LP as quick effects to apply the activation effect of 1 堕天使 spell or trap from the grave, then shuffle that card into the deck, each once per turn and usable on the opponent's turn
- This recycles 追放 87112784 search, 戒坛 14517422 revival, 佚乐之堕天使 99941223 fusion, 堕天使降临 58787301 revival, 神属的堕天使 48152161 negation and 魅惑的堕天使 87990236 snatch from the grave
- 内尔伽勒 18168997 also gives every Fairy you control piercing damage
- The copied card returns to the deck, so it is spent until re-milled, do not copy 追放 87112784 expecting to reuse it

- **One-Card Combo: 杰胡提 10426067**

- Step 1: normal summon 杰胡提 10426067 and activate, special summon any other 堕天使 from deck in defense, take 骼骼他 84031359, this locks you to Fairy-only special summons until end of turn
- Step 2: 骼骼他 84031359 on summon creates 2 堕天使衍生物 84031360 tokens, Level 6 Fairy Dark 0/0
- Step 3: link 杰胡提 10426067 and 骼骼他 84031359 into 失乐之堕天使 35306215, 骼骼他 in grave searches 佚乐之堕天使 99941223
- Step 4: 失乐 35306215 discards 1 card and mills 苏泊比亚 11260714 from deck
- Step 5: activate 佚乐之堕天使 99941223, banish 苏泊比亚 11260714, 骼骼他 84031359 and 杰胡提 10426067 from the grave to fusion summon 黎明之堕天使 路西菲尔 4167084, 佚乐 raises its attack by 1000 to 5000
- End field: 失乐 35306215, 黎明之堕天使 路西菲尔 4167084 and 2 tokens, 黎明 makes all your Fairies untargetable and quick-revives 1 Fairy per turn for 1000 LP
- Halt points: Ash Blossom on 杰胡提 10426067, on 骼骼他 84031359 search or on 佚乐之堕天使 99941223, 增殖的G 23434538 anywhere in the chain, Nibiru after 5 summons

- **End Field**

- 失乐之堕天使 35306215 keeps the grave engine running, 黎明之堕天使 路西菲尔 4167084 protects every Fairy from targeting and revives one per turn, 特斯卡特利波卡 88234365 in hand saves a monster from destruction
- 黄昏之堕天使 路西法 10136446 fusion summon sets 1 堕天使 spell and 1 堕天使 trap from deck, typically 神属的堕天使 48152161 and 魅惑的堕天使 87990236, and its own copy effect reuses them from the grave
- Tribute-summoned 路西菲尔 25451652 with 2 to 3 Darklords from hand or deck is a 3000 beatstick untargetable while another 堕天使 is on field, its ignition effect mills 堕天使 equal to 堕天使 on both fields and gains 500 LP per milled 堕天使
- 召命之神弓-阿波罗萨 4280258 is the negate boss, link it from Fairies before any non-Fairy link, then 访问码语者 86066372 or 刺刀枪管龙 85289965 finishes after its negates are spent
- 闭锁世界的冥神 98127546 steals a threat by using it as material through an opponent link monster

- **Extenders**

- 追放 87112784 searches any 堕天使 card, the primary one-card setup for 戒坛 14517422, 佚乐之堕天使 99941223 or 苏泊比亚 11260714
- 失乐之堕天使 35306215 discards 1 to add a 堕天使 monster or mill one, and while it is on field a 2-tribute Fairy such as 路西菲尔 25451652 can be advance summoned by banishing 2 grave monsters instead of tributing
- 享乐之堕天使 55289183 quick effect tribute summons a Fairy from hand during either main phase, summon 路西菲尔 25451652 on the opponent's turn for a surprise Darklord swarm
- 伊希塔布 52840267 discards itself and 1 堕天使 from hand to draw 2, 莫斯提马 25339070 discards 2 堕天使 from hand to special summon itself, 安度西亚斯 51728779 discards itself and 1 堕天使 to add 1 堕天使 from grave to hand
- 悦乐之堕天使 82773292 on summon gives the opponent 1 堕天使 in defense and adds a different-level 堕天使 from deck to hand, feed the opponent a Level 8 monster then 堕天使降临 58787301 revives up to 2 Level 8 Darklords from your grave for half your LP
- 佚乐之堕天使 99941223 can banish the opponent's face-up 堕天使 as fusion material when fusing a 堕天使 fusion, fuse away the monster that 悦乐之堕天使 82773292 gave them
- 三位圣统者 26866984 quick effect releases 1 to 3 Fairies including the opponent's face-up Fairies to summon itself, release 3 to destroy 1 card and draw 2
- 堕入黑暗的天使 93023136 sends 1 Fairy from hand or field to grave to special summon any 堕天使 from hand, deck or banished ignoring summon conditions, the only way to cheat out 路西菲尔 25451652 or 迪赛尔 55690251 without tribute summoning
- 森蚺 70369116 copies 超融合 48130397 from deck to fuse an opponent monster with your Darklord into 凶饿毒融合龙 41209827 or 捕食植物 犀角龙 69946549, use its attribute change first if the opponent monster is not Dark
- 杰胡提 10426067 grave effect banishes itself to add a 堕天使 card or 禁忌的 quick-play from grave to hand while a face-up Dark Fairy fusion is on field

- **Mirror Match**

- The opponent's 佚乐之堕天使 99941223 can banish your face-up 堕天使 as fusion material, never leave 苏泊比亚 11260714 or 内尔伽勒 18168997 face up unprotected
- 悦乐之堕天使 82773292 feeding a 堕天使 to the opponent backfires in the mirror, it gives them free fusion material and a 堕天使降临 58787301 level anchor
- 三位圣统者 26866984 releases the opponent's face-up Fairies, any face-up 堕天使 on their field is a free tribute for them
- 神属的堕天使 48152161 negates their 黎明之堕天使 路西菲尔 4167084 or 黄昏之堕天使 路西法 10136446 for a turn and gains LP equal to its attack
- The first 黎明之堕天使 路西菲尔 4167084 to stick wins the targeting war, back it with 特斯卡特利波卡 88234365 in hand and 内尔伽勒 18168997 piercing
- 路西菲尔 25451652 is untargetable only while another 堕天使 is on field, 背德的堕天使 50501121 destroys it after stripping the field

- **Common Mistakes**

- 佚乐之堕天使 99941223 banishes its materials, so 苏泊比亚 11260714 does not trigger and the grave is consumed, count banish fodder before fusing
- The copy family returns the copied spell or trap to the deck, a copied 追放 87112784 is spent until re-milled
- 路西菲尔 25451652 and 迪赛尔 55690251 cannot be special summoned, 戒坛 14517422, 死者苏生 83764718 and 苏泊比亚 11260714 cannot bring them back, only tribute summoning or 堕入黑暗的天使 93023136 works
- 骼骼他 84031359 is once per turn and its token effect fires only on its first special summon, its grave search fires whenever it hits the grave including as link or fusion material
- 杰胡提 10426067 and 骼骼他 84031359 lock you to Fairy-only special summons, do non-Fairy plays such as 森蚺 70369116 or 访问码语者 86066372 first
- 森蚺 70369116 copy effect forbids all special summons for the rest of the turn, copy 超融合 48130397 only as the final summon
- 苏泊比亚 11260714 needs another Fairy in the grave, do not activate 戒坛 14517422 with an empty grave
- 悦乐之堕天使 82773292 gives the opponent a monster that can be tributed or fused against you and locks your non-Fairy monster effects after use
- 堕天使降临 58787301 costs half your LP, check for lethal before activating
- 抵价购物 38120068 discards only Level 8 monsters, 苏泊比亚 11260714, 内尔伽勒 18168997, 杰拉特 40921744 and 阿斯蒙蒂斯 85771019 qualify but 特斯卡特利波卡 88234365 is Level 9
- 莫斯提马 25339070 discards 2 堕天使 as cost, keep one 特斯卡特利波卡 88234365 or 追放 87112784 in hand
- 路西菲尔 25451652 mills equal to every face-up 堕天使 on both fields, the opponent's 悦乐-fed monster increases the mill
- Under 增殖的G 23434538 stop after 失乐之堕天使 35306215 and one 苏泊比亚 11260714 chain, the token line hands over many draws

- **Build Quirks**

- This environment's cards.cdb marks every fusion spell with setcode 0x46, which is what 森蚺 70369116 can send from deck, 超融合 48130397 is the usual target and is not needed in the main deck
- 骼骼他 84031359 and 杰胡提 10426067 can also search 禁忌的 quick-play spells (setcode 0x11d) such as 禁忌的一滴 24299458 and 禁忌的圣冠 98829635
- 杰胡提 10426067 grave effect requires a face-up Dark Fairy fusion on field, it only works after 黎明之堕天使 路西菲尔 4167084 or 黄昏之堕天使 路西法 10136446 is out
- The 201031 near-pure build runs 名推理 58577036 and 手札抹杀 72892473 as mass mill, every milled 堕天使 is live grave fuel for the copy family
- 堕天使衍生物 84031360 tokens are Dark Fairy, they stay useful as 佚乐之堕天使 99941223 material next turn
- 三位圣统者 26866984 leaves the field banished, use it as release material before it would be destroyed
