- **Core Mechanic: Venus LP into Shine Balls**

- 创造之代行者 维纳斯 64734921 activates from the field, pays 500 LP, and special summons 神圣球体 39552864 from deck or hand; no once-per-turn restriction, so three activations produce three Shine Balls for 1500 LP
- 神圣球体 39552864 is the universal material: Level 2 LIGHT Fairy works for Rank 2 Xyz 圣光之宣告者 1249315, Level 4 Synchro 虹光之宣告者 79606837, and Fairy Link plays 虚光之宣告者 46935289 and 代行者的近卫 穆恩 90290572
- 宣告者的神巫 92919429 on normal or special summon sends one Fairy from deck or extra deck to graveyard and gains that monster's level until end of turn, then when released it special summons one Level 2 or lower Fairy from hand or deck
- 神秘之代行者 厄斯 91188343 on normal summon adds one 代行者 from deck, or 主宰者·许珀里翁 55794644 instead when 天空的圣域 56433456 is on the field; it is a Level 2 Tuner so 厄斯 plus Shine Ball makes 虹光之宣告者 79606837
- 命之代行者 尼普顿 38529357 discards itself to special summon one 代行者 from hand or graveyard, or one 许珀里翁 monster instead when Sanctuary is on field or in graveyard; when banished it adds 天空的圣域 56433456 from deck
- 破坏之代行者 维纳斯 99054885 banishes 创造之代行者 维纳斯 64734921 from graveyard to summon itself, then pays LP in 500 multiples to revive that many 神圣球体 39552864 from graveyard or banished zone, and those Shine Balls return to the bottom of the deck when they leave the field
- 失落的圣域 90312154 counts as 天空的圣域 56433456 while face-up on field or in graveyard (change-code effect), so it turns on 神罚 81066751, 尼普顿 38529357's Hyperion upgrade, 圣水 26684111's LP recovery, and the Hyperion double-use conditions
- 虹光之宣告者 79606837's first effect redirects monsters sent from hand or deck to the graveyard to the banished zone for both players, so graveyard setups must be done before it is summoned

- **One-Card Combo: 创造之代行者 维纳斯**

- Activate 创造之代行者 维纳斯 64734921, pay 500 LP, special summon 神圣球体 39552864 from deck
- Repeat twice for two more 神圣球体 39552864, total cost 1500 LP and three Level 2 Fairies
- Xyz two 神圣球体 39552864 into 圣光之宣告者 1249315, detach one material to add one monster from your graveyard to hand, then return one card from hand to deck
- Link 创造之代行者 维纳斯 64734921 plus one 神圣球体 39552864 into 虚光之宣告者 46935289 (two non-token monsters with the same attribute and race, both LIGHT Fairy), giving a spell/trap negate that discards one Fairy from hand
- End field: 圣光之宣告者 1249315 plus 虚光之宣告者 46935289 plus one 神圣球体 39552864, one extra card in hand, and the normal summon still available for 神秘之代行者 厄斯 91188343 or 宣告者的神巫 92919429
- With 宣告者的神巫 92919429 as the second card: dump 地外生命 40177746, let 地外生命 banish up to two LIGHT Fairies from field or graveyard to add that many Level 2 or lower LIGHT Fairies from deck, then 朱光之宣告者 17266660 plus 神圣球体 39552864 into 虹光之宣告者 79606837

- **Halt Points**

- 灰流丽 14558127 stops 创造之代行者 维纳斯 64734921's deck summon, 宣告者的神巫 92919429's dump, 地外生命 40177746's search, and 天空的圣水 26684111
- 增殖的G 23434538 or 欢聚友伴·茸茸长尾山雀 42141493 punish every special summon from deck and extra deck, so stop after Venus plus one Shine Ball and play the Sanctuary control game instead
- 无限泡影 10045474 on 创造之代行者 维纳斯 64734921 or on 虹光之宣告者 79606837 disables the whole engine or negate, and 墓穴的指名者 24224830 or 抹杀之指名者 65681983 answer the hand traps
- Removing 神圣球体 39552864 or 创造之代行者 维纳斯 64734921 before it links or Xyzs starves the Herald plays, so protect the Shine Ball board
- 虹光之宣告者 79606837 must stay face-up for its macro effect, so targeted negation on it turns off both its first and second effects
- 宣告者的神巫 92919429 is the recursion point, destroy or banish it instead of letting it be released, because its release trigger special summons from deck

- **Mirror Match**

- 朱光之宣告者 17266660 decides the mirror because it negates monster effects from the hand, so whoever holds it first wins the Herald war
- 虹光之宣告者 79606837's macro effect banishes monsters sent from hand or deck to graveyard, so the player who summons it first denies the opponent's 地外生命 40177746 and 宣告者的神巫 92919429 graveyard recursion
- 神罚 81066751 needs a face-up 天空的圣域 56433456 or 失落的圣域 90312154, so setting 失落的圣域 90312154 first activates your counter trap and the Hyperion double-use effects
- Save 无限泡影 10045474 for the opponent's 创造之代行者 维纳斯 64734921 to stop the Shine Ball engine before it snowballs
- 深渊之神兽 狄斯·帕忒耳 27572350 answers banished monsters and negates monster effects, strong against the opponent's macro and banish-heavy lines
- 超雷龙-雷龙 15291624 blocks the opponent's searches from 神秘之代行者 厄斯 91188343, 天空的圣水 26684111, and 宣告者的神巫 92919429

- **Common Mistakes**

- Forgetting 失落的圣域 90312154 is treated as 天空的圣域 56433456 on field or in graveyard, so 神罚 81066751, 尼普顿 38529357's Hyperion upgrade, and the Hyperion double-use effects all work off it
- Summoning 虹光之宣告者 79606837 before graveyard setup, because its macro effect banishes monsters sent from hand or deck to graveyard and wastes 地外生命 40177746 and 神圣球体 39552864 value
- Releasing the monster summoned by 命之代行者 尼普顿 38529357 for 三位圣统者 26866984 or 宣告者的神巫 92919429, since it cannot be tributed until the opponent's end phase
- Overpaying 创造之代行者 维纳斯 64734921 with three 500 LP activations into burn range or hand traps like 增殖的G 23434538; one or two Shine Balls is often enough
- Forgetting 破坏之代行者 维纳斯 99054885's revived 神圣球体 39552864 return to the bottom of the deck when they leave the field, so they do not recycle into the graveyard
- 宣告者的神巫 92919429 can dump from the extra deck too, so send 虹光之宣告者 79606837 or 圣光之宣告者 1249315 when the main deck Fairy is not useful
- 晓天使 肯比 12500059's release effect locks special summons to Fairy-only for the rest of the turn, activate it last or plan the combo around the lock
- 神罚 81066751 cannot activate without a face-up 天空的圣域 56433456 or 失落的圣域 90312154, check the field before setting it
- 深渊之神兽 狄斯·帕忒耳 27572350 requires non-Tuner Dragon monsters, so build it through 加速同调星尘龙 30983281's extra synchro effect or dedicated Dragon materials, not through Fairy monsters
