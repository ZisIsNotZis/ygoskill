# 自奏圣乐 (Orcust) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- Core engine monsters are all DARK Machine and work from the graveyard by banishing themselves: 嬉游曲恶魔 57835716 (banish, Special Summon any Orcust from deck), 谐谑曲骷髅 21441617 (banish, Special Summon an Orcust from GY), 梦幻崩影 4055337 (banish, send 1 DARK Machine from deck to GY and buff a monster)
- Field spell 通天塔 90351981 turns every 自奏圣乐-named link monster on the field and every Orcust monster in the GY into quick effects usable on the opponent turn, and recycles itself from GY by discarding 1 card if it was not sent there this turn
- Signature one-card starter: 宵星之骑士 吉尔苏 69811710, Normal Summon sends 1 Orcust or World Legacy card from deck to GY, and while it is the only monster it creates 2 星遗物衍生物 69811711 tokens (one on each field)

- Extra deck: 伽拉忒亚 30741503, 伽拉忒亚i 48835607, 管弦乐琴机械乐团 3134857, 朗吉尔苏 76145142, 卡农曲大炮 94046012, 丁吉尔苏 93854893, 双穹之骑士 阿斯特拉姆 21887175, I:P百变莱娜 65741786, 访问码语者 86066372, 拓扑零日衔尾蛇 66403530, 天霆号阿宙斯 90448279, No.84 增痛蛛 26556950

- **Core Mechanic: Graveyard Banish Engine**

- All recursion lives in the GY: 嬉游曲恶魔 57835716 pulls from the deck, 谐谑曲骷髅 21441617 pulls from the GY, 梦幻崩影 4055337 refills the GY from the deck
- Every one of those effects locks you to DARK-only special summons until the end of the turn, so all combo pieces must be DARK and the LIGHT plays are off limits in the same turn
- 通天塔 90351981 makes the whole loop reactive: on the opponent turn, 嬉游曲恶魔 can summon an Orcust to link away, 伽拉忒亚 30741503 and 伽拉忒亚i 48835607 search and set mid-combat, and 梦幻崩影 4055337 dumps during the damage step
- 伽拉忒亚 30741503 and 朗吉尔苏 76145142 shuffle banished Machines back into the deck (Galatea then sets 1 Orcust Spell/Trap from deck, Longirsu then sends 1 co-linked opponent monster to GY), so the banished pool is recycled instead of exhausted
- 之阶 703897 is the counter-trap layer, usable while an Orcust link is on field, and its GY effect banishes itself to add a DARK Machine while locking to DARK Machine summons
- 丁吉尔苏 93854893 is a Rank 8 Xyz that can instead be overlaid directly onto your Orcust link monster, sending 1 opponent card to GY or attaching a banished Machine, with material-detach destruction replacement

- **One-Card Combo: 宵星之骑士 吉尔苏**

- Starter: 吉尔苏 69811710 in hand, nothing else needed (one discard required later for 伽拉忒亚i)
- Step 1: Normal Summon 吉尔苏, its effect sends 嬉游曲恶魔 57835716 from deck to GY
- Step 2: 吉尔苏 ignition effect (no other monsters on your field): make 1 星遗物衍生物 69811711 on your field and 1 on the opponent field
- Step 3: 嬉游曲恶魔 GY effect: banish itself, Special Summon 谐谑曲骷髅 21441617 from deck, DARK lock applies
- Step 4: Link 吉尔苏 and 谐谑曲骷髅 into 伽拉忒亚 30741503, 谐谑曲骷髅 goes to the GY
- Step 5: 伽拉忒亚 effect: shuffle the banished 嬉游曲恶魔 into the deck and set 之阶 703897 face-down from deck
- Step 6: Link 伽拉忒亚 into 伽拉忒亚i 48835607 (material: 1 Orcust or World Legacy link monster), 伽拉忒亚 goes to the GY
- Step 7: 伽拉忒亚i effect: discard 1 card, add 通天塔 90351981 from deck to hand and activate it as the field spell
- Step 8: 谐谑曲骷髅 GY quick effect (通天塔 active): banish itself, Special Summon 伽拉忒亚 back from the GY
- Step 9: fresh 伽拉忒亚: shuffle 1 banished Machine into the deck and set a second Orcust Spell/Trap face-down (主音 26845680 or 释音 47171541)
- Step 10: overlay 伽拉忒亚 into 丁吉尔苏 93854893, its effect sends 1 opponent card to the GY

- **Common Mistakes**

- Do not activate 谐谑曲骷髅 21441617 before another Orcust is in the GY: it revives from the GY and needs a target, the usual setup is linking away 伽拉忒亚 30741503 or dumping a second Orcust with 梦幻崩影 4055337 or 主音 26845680
- Every Orcust GY effect and 星遗物-『星杖』 93920420 locks special summons to DARK until the end of the turn: no 苍穹之机界骑士 20537097, 双穹之骑士 阿斯特拉姆 21887175, 天霆号阿宙斯 90448279, or 梦幻崩影·凤凰 2857636 in the same turn
- 梦幻崩影 4055337 needs a face-up monster to target, if 暗黑战士格雷法 14536035 special summoned itself onto an empty field the dump effect is dead until a body exists
- 之阶 703897 requires an Orcust link monster on field, setting it behind no 伽拉忒亚 30741503 leaves it unusable
- 通天塔 90351981 is a field spell and dies to any opponent field spell, keep 警卫路障山巨人 13117073 (link summon: discard 1, end phase add a Continuous or Field Spell from GY) or the GY recycle ready
- 伽拉忒亚i 48835607 must be linked using an Orcust or World Legacy link monster, 废铁回收员 4334811 and 数学家 41386308 cannot be its material
- Only one 丁吉尔苏 93854893 special summon per turn and only one 伽拉忒亚i 48835607 link summon per turn
- Do not overlay 伽拉忒亚 30741503 into 丁吉尔苏 93854893 before using 谐谑曲骷髅 21441617: as Xyz material 伽拉忒亚 is not in the GY for the revive
- 吉尔苏 69811710's token effect gives the opponent a free 0 ATK/DEF token they can use as link material, only make it when the extra body or column matters
