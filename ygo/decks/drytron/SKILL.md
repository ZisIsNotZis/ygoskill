# 龙辉巧 (Drytron) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- 龙辉巧 (Drytron) is a LIGHT Machine ritual combo deck built around the 六个 special-summon monsters (δ/β/γ/α/ν2/星彩) that spiral forward by tributing each other and revive from hand or grave while searching ritual cards, then cheat out the 宣告者 (Herald) ritual negate wall via 流星辉巧群
- Core six engines: 龙辉巧-天厨一δ 22420202, 龙辉巧-天棓三β 33543890, 龙辉巧-天棓四γ 60037599, 龙辉巧-上弼ζ 96026108, 龙辉巧-右枢α 97148796, 龙辉巧-扶筐增二 58793369 (the field spell 扶筐增二 is the name-schism: same string used for both the monster 扶筐增二 and this field spell, read datas race to tell them apart)
- Ritual spell core: 流星辉巧群 22398665 and its upgraded 流星极辉巧群 24166324 both ritual summon by tributing Machine monsters from hand or field whose total ATK reaches the target's ATK, ignoring the normal "exact level" ritual requirement
- Search and recursion support: 极超之龙辉巧 94187078 (deck special summon), 龙辉巧流星群 84965420 (counter negate-recyclable), 星彩之龙辉巧 57970721 (spot destroy), 食光之龙辉巧 21576077 (grave recycle + buff), 天斗辉巧极 89771220 (contact special summon 天极辉舰-熊斗龙巧)
- Ritual targets: 虚光之宣告者 46935289, 神光之宣告者 44665365, 崇光之宣告者 48546368 (the negate bosses), 深渊的宣告者 94016752, 虹光之宣告者 79606837 (cheap first negate), 三叉龙之影灵衣 52068432 in some builds
- Generic ritual support: 宣告者的神巫 92919429 mills/spawns ritual fodder, 仪式的准备 13048472 searches 流星辉巧群 plus a ritual monster, 宣告者的神托 79306385 and 宣告者的预言 27383110 legacy variants
- Sample pure builds in deck/220115龙辉巧, deck/221015龙辉巧, deck/240427龙辉巧; the engine also splashes 北极天熊 (Beargram) for 天极辉舰-熊斗龙巧 and 现世与冥界的逆转/巨石遗物 variants

- **Core Mechanic: Tribute-Swarm Ritual Machine**

- All six Drytron monsters cannot be Normal Summoned and can only be Special Summoned by a Drytron effect, but each once per turn can release another Drytron or a ritual monster you control to Special Summon itself from hand or GY in Defense; γ and δ and ζ then each chain a bonus search (γ revives another 2000-ATK Drytron, ζ adds a ritual spell, α adds a ritual monster, δ adds/draws, β returns a banished Drytron)
- The release engine has a tight lock: after any of these special-summon effects activates this turn, you cannot Special Summon except monsters that cannot be Normal Summoned, which is exactly the Drytron monsters and ritual monsters, so the deck is machine-only as soon as the chain starts
- 流星辉巧群 22398665 ①: ritual summon using Machine monsters from hand or field, tributing until their total ATK is higher than the target's ATK, so high-ATK Drytron monsters and 大天使 克里斯提亚 59509952-type fodder pay for the summon; its ② in GY lowers a Drytron's ATK by 1000 to add itself back, enabling repeat rituals each turn
- 流星极辉巧群 24166324 ① recovers up to 2 banished Drytron cards by releasing a Drytron you control, and ② is the same ATK-based ritual using Machine monsters while revealing a 流星辉巧群 from hand, the extended ritual line that does not consume the 陨石 original
- 极超之龙辉巧 94187078: from hand or field, Special Summon a Drytron monster from deck (destroyed in the end phase) and locks you to non-normal-summonable monsters for the turn, a one-card body-plus-chain starter
- 龙辉巧流星群 84965420: when an opponent summons while you control a ritual monster, negate it and return to deck, printed once per turn and its grave recycle keeps it live across turns
- The 宣告者 negate bosses use Fairy hand monsters (朱光 17266660, 绿光 21074344, 紫光 94689635) to discard and negate opponent effects, and their 神巫 make the hand cheat: 宣告者的神巫 92919429 mills a ritual monster from deck/extra then special summons a 宣告者; the Drytron engine's new special summons after the 神巫 keep the Fairy chain live

- **One-Card Combo: 极超之龙辉巧 94187078**

- Starter: 极超之龙辉巧 94187078 alone in hand, or any Drytron plus 极超 to begin the spiral
- Step 1: activate 极超, Special Summon 龙辉巧-上弼ζ 96026108 from deck, the non-normal-summonable lock now applies for the turn
- Step 2: ζ effect ① releases itself or another Monster, is used to Special Summon δ or β from hand/GY and to meet the 极超 end-phase destruction
- Step 3: chain 龙辉巧-天厨一δ 22420202, its effect releases ζ, Special Summons itself from hand in Defense, then reveals a ritual monster or ritual spell from hand to draw 1
- Step 4: δ or the next Drytron adds 流星辉巧群 22398665 or a ritual monster via 龙辉巧-右枢α 97148796 or 上弼ζ's search
- Step 5: activate 流星辉巧群 22398665, tribute the Drytron bodies (their ATK pays for the ritual), ritual summon 虚光之宣告者 46935289 or 神光之宣告者 44665365
- Step 6: 流星辉巧群 ② in GY lowers the Herald by 1000 to add itself back, so the ritual can repeat next turn
- One hand-trap start: 宣告者的神巫 92919429 + any Drytron after it — 神巫 mills 崇光之宣告者 48546368, then 极超 or δ summons 虚光 to build the negate wall faster
- Verify exact ritual math against the target's ATK; 流星辉巧群 tributes until total ATK exceeds the target, so a 虚光之宣告者 46935289 (3000 ATK) needs 3000+ total Machine ATK

- **End Field**

- 虚光之宣告者 46935289 plus 神光之宣告者 44665365 or 崇光之宣告者 48546368 with Fairy hand fodder (朱光 17266660 / 绿光 21074344 / 紫光 94689635), 6+ negates standing behind a machine-ritual board
- 宣告者的神巫 92919429 on field or in hand for the follow-up, plus 龙辉巧流星群 84965420 set to negate summons
- 龙辉巧-扶筐增二μβ' 1174075 in the extra deck: on Xyz summon mills a Drytron from deck, its ② lets you use its Xyz materials as ritual tributes, and ③ once per turn detaches a material to negate an opponent Spell/Trap activation, the machine lock's co-negate
- 天极辉舰-熊斗龙巧 via 天斗辉巧极 89771220 as a tall machine body alternative
- Halt points: 灰流丽 14558127 on 极超之龙辉巧 94187078, on 流星辉巧群 or on 宣告者的神巫; 增殖的G 23434538 punishing the special-summon spiral; effect negation on the release-search triggers stops the chain before the Herald lands

- **Extenders**

- 龙辉巧-天棓三β 33543890: its trigger returns a banished Drytron to the grave, recovering the ritual spell or an earlier engine piece
- 龙辉巧-天厨一δ 22420202 live reveal-draw gives the deck extra fuel past its own summons, and its send-to-grave loops feed 食光之龙辉巧 21576077 and 天斗辉巧极 89771220
- 星彩之龙辉巧 57970721: quick destroy in Main Phase, target your Drytron and an opponent monster, the Drytron gains nothing but the opponent card is destroyed
- 食光之龙辉巧 21576077 ① recycles a Drytron from grave to hand, and ② banishes itself to buff a Drytron by 2000 ATK, the OTK pump for a Delta/β attack
- 龙辉巧-上弼ζ 96026108 adds a ritual spell; 龙辉巧-右枢α 97148796 adds a ritual monster; 极超之龙辉巧 94187078 is the extra normal-summon-free deck body
- 巨龙 深渊的宣告者 94016752 as a second Herald line and 大天使 克里斯提亚 59509952 as a special-summon floodgate splash (both legal under the non-normal-summonable lock)

- **Halt Points**

- 灰流丽 14558127 stops 极超之龙辉巧 94187078 (deck summon), 流星辉巧群 activation, and 宣告者的神巫 92919429 milling, each a distinct choke point
- 增殖的G 23434538: the Drytron spiral special summons five or more times, the engine player must stop early or hand the game
- 次元吸引者 91800273 and D.D. 乌鸦 24508238 remove grave Drytron monsters, cutting 流星辉巧群's ritual targets and the ① revive-recursion
- 无限泡影 10045474 or 效果遮蒙者 97268402 on the release-effect trigger kills the chain at its source; negate the 龙辉巧-天厨一δ or 右枢α search before the ritual spell is found
- 王家长眠之谷 blocks the ATK-based ritual targets that must come from hand or grave, and the 神巫 can no longer mill
- 禁忌的一滴 24299458 or 无限泡影 10045474 strip the 宣告者 negate pile because the Heralds have no built-in protection once their hand is exhausted

- **Mirror Match: 龙辉巧 vs 龙辉巧**

- The first player to resolve 极超之龙辉巧 94187078 and land 虚光之宣告者 46935289 usually locks the other side out, so the opening hand-trap race is the whole game
- 灰流丽 14558127 on the opponent 流星辉巧群 is the decisive interrupt; 墓穴的指名者 24224830 on the opponent 神巫 or their grave Drytron stops their recursion
- Whoever keeps 龙辉巧流星群 84965420 live on the opponent's turn dominates, its recycle means the negate never runs out
- The machine lock means neither player can drop non-machine extra or tribute monsters after the chain starts, so the mirror stays within Drytron/ritual space
- 星彩之龙辉巧 57970721 removes the opponent's Herald mid-chain, but costs a Drytron you have already sunk

- **Common Mistakes**

- Activating the release-① effects in the wrong order: after the first one fires you are locked to non-normal-summonable monsters, so make your non-machine plays (e.g. any Link or Xyz that must precede the machine lock) before the spiral begins
- Tributing the field spell 扶筐增二 58793369 thinking it is a Monster (same Chinese name as 龙辉巧-扶筐增二δ/β variants) — check race and card type; the field spell and the Xyz 扶筐增二μβ' 1174075 do different jobs
- Forgetting the 流星辉巧群 ① ATK-based tribute: it needs total Machine ATK above the target, not the target's Level, so a 崇光之宣告者 48546368 summon costs more fodders than a level-check would suggest
- 流星辉巧群 and 极超 each lock you to non-normal-summonable monsters for the turn, and that restriction interacts with your Herald's need for Fairy hand-reveals — read the lock before starting the ritual
- 极超之龙辉巧 94187078's special summoned Drytron is destroyed in the end phase, plan for the body not to survive
- 龙辉巧-ν2 22435424 special summons itself from grave only while a Drytron card is face-up and is banished when it leaves the field, do not treat it as a permanent body
- 宣告者的神巫 92919429 special summons only a monster that can be Normal Summoned; pairing it with the Drytron engine requires sequencing the summon before the non-normal-summonable lock
- Over-extending into 增殖的G 23434538 or 原始生命态尼比鲁 27204311, the spiral exceeds five summons, keep the special-summon count under the balloon threshold when 尼比鲁 is live
- The Herald negates discard any Fairy from hand; without hand Fairies the 宣告者 wall is dead, keep 朱光/绿光/紫光 or 神巫 fodder available
