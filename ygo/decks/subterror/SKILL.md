- **Core Mechanic: Flip Loop**

- Behemoth template, shared by all eight 地中族邪界兽: ① when a face-up monster you control becomes face-down while you control no face-up monsters, special summon this from hand face-up defense; ② main phase, flip this face-down once per turn; ③ flip effect

- 地中族导师 16428514 flip effect searches any 地中族 card except itself; ② flips any face-up monster on either side of the field plus itself face-down, so it is both an engine piece and removal for a key opponent monster, and becomes a quick effect on the opponent's turn only while another face-up 地中族 card is on your field
- 地中族妖魔 74762582 is the hand-trap: discard it from hand or field to negate any opponent's activated spell, trap, or monster effect, targeting a face-up 地中族 monster you control, then flip that monster face-down; its ② flips a face-up monster you control and specials a 地中族 from hand or grave face-up or face-down defense
