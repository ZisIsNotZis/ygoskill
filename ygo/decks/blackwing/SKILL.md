# 黑羽 (Blackwing) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- 黑羽 is a DARK Winged-Beast swarm deck (archetype setcode 0x33) that normal-summons repeatedly, searches with 黑旋风 91351370, and climbs into DARK Synchro bosses 黑翼龙 9012916 and 黑翼强袭龙 73218989
- Modern pure builds (deck/240727黑羽, deck/230114黑羽, deck/220716黑羽) run 3x 毒风之西蒙 81470373, 3x 幻耀之苏德里 70465810, 3x 无赖之伐他 71187462, 2-3x 岚沙之夏马 8571567, 2x 雪击之奇努克 34976176, 1-2x 突风之颪 73652465, 1-2x 沙尘之哈麦丹 77152542, 1x 下弦之光弓鸟 54594017, 2x 黑羽之旋风 7602800, 3x 黑旋风 91351370
- Classic engine pieces that still appear: 疾风之盖尔 2009101, 黑枪之布拉斯特 49003716, 精锐之泽费洛斯 14785765, 南风之奥斯特 17465972, 大旆之伐由 72714392
- Extra deck core: 黑翼龙 9012916, 黑翼强袭龙 73218989, 魔风之玻瑞阿斯 10602628, 星影之苦剑鸟 95040215, 孤高之银风鸦 33236860, 重装铠翼鸦 54082269, 骤雨之雷切刀鸟 16051717, 神立之鬼丸刀鸟 80773359, 泪雨之千鸟刀鸟 23338098, 五月雨之骚速刀鸟 53389254

- Generic synchro toolbox: 琰魔龙 红莲魔·渊 9753964, 琰魔龙王 红莲魔·厄 62242678, 混沌之双翼 22850702, 鲜花女男爵 84815190, 装弹枪管狞猛龙 27548199, 加速同调士 37675907, 磷回之三弦猫 46057733, 深渊之神兽 狄斯·帕忒耳 27572350, S:P小夜骑士 29301450

- **Core Mechanic: 黑旋风 Search Engine**

- 黑旋风 91351370 is a Continuous Spell with no once-per-turn limit: whenever you Normal Summon a 黑羽 monster, add 1 黑羽 monster with strictly lower printed ATK from deck to hand
- 毒风之西蒙 81470373 is the one-card 黑旋风 engine: with an empty field, banish 1 other 黑羽 from hand, place 黑旋风 from deck face-up, then Normal Summon Simoon without tribute (Lv6 1600); the placed 黑旋风 self-destructs at the end phase for 1000 damage and the turn is locked to DARK Extra-Deck summons
- 幻耀之苏德里 70465810 searches on Normal Summon any card whose text mentions 黑翼龙 (9012916): 黑羽之旋风 7602800, 岚沙之夏马 8571567, 南风之奥斯特 17465972, 雪击之奇努克 34976176, 黑羽-双影 69973414, 无赖之伐他 71187462, 黑翼强袭龙 73218989, 黑鸟断道翼 80254726, 黑翼高洁 96367119
- 岚沙之夏马 8571567 sends itself from hand or field to grave to place 黑羽之旋风 7602800 face-up from deck; its grave effect banishes itself to add a 黑羽 from grave to hand when 黑翼龙 or a 黑羽 Synchro is special summoned, costing 700 damage
- 星影之苦剑鸟 95040215 (Lv6 generic Synchro) burns 800 and weakens a monster on summon, and while face-up grants one extra 黑羽 Normal Summon per turn, enabling a second 黑旋风 search in the same turn

- **Core Mechanic: 黑羽之旋风 Recursion and 黑羽 Counters**

- 黑羽之旋风 7602800 is a Continuous Spell: once per turn, when you Special Summon a DARK Synchro from the Extra Deck, Special Summon 1 黑羽 or 黑翼龙 9012916 from grave or banished with lower ATK than it; separately, once per turn it removes 1 黑羽 counter instead of a DARK monster you control being destroyed
- 黑翼龙 9012916 (Lv8 2800 DARK Dragon Synchro, generic tuner plus non-tuner) converts all effect damage you would take into 黑羽 counters on itself, loses 700 ATK per counter, and its activation removes all counters to cut an opponent monster by 700 per counter and burn the difference
- 黑翼强袭龙 73218989 (Lv10 3200) synchro-summons using a Synchro monster as the tuner material plus non-tuners, or special summons from the Extra Deck by banishing 1 Synchro Tuner and 1 黑翼龙 9012916 from field or grave; every opponent monster effect puts 1 黑羽 counter on it and burns 700, and on the opponent's turn it can tribute itself with 4 or more counters to destroy all cards on the field
- 魔风之玻瑞阿斯 10602628 (Lv6 Synchro Tuner, DARK tuner material) is the ladder key: on Synchro Summon it mills 1 黑羽 from deck and changes its own level to the milled card's level, becoming the tuner half of any level climb
- 重装铠翼鸦 54082269 (Lv10, 黑羽 tuner plus non-tuner) is immune to other card effects, places 1 wedge counter on an opponent monster per effect activation, steals one wedge-countered monster per turn, and destroys all wedge-countered monsters at the end phase

- **One-Card Starter and Standard Combo**

- There is no true one-card full combo in this card pool; the one-card starters are 幻耀之苏德里 70465810 alone (Normal Summon, search 黑羽之旋风 7602800, tribute itself for a 幻耀衍生物 Lv2 tuner token, take 700) and 毒风之西蒙 81470373 (requires 1 other 黑羽 in hand to banish; sets 黑旋风 91351370, Normal Summons Simoon, searches via 黑旋风)
- Standard two-card combo 幻耀之苏德里 70465810 plus 无赖之伐他 71187462 ends on 黑翼强袭龙 73218989 plus 黑羽之旋风 7602800:
- Step 1: Normal Summon 苏德里, search 黑羽之旋风
- Step 2: Special Summon 伐他 (Suddri is a face-up 黑羽), activate 黑羽之旋风
- Step 3: 伐他 effect sends 伐他 plus 6 levels of non-tuner 黑羽 from deck (苏德里 Lv4 plus 雪击之奇努克 34976176 Lv2) to grave and Special Summons 黑翼龙 9012916 from the Extra Deck; after this the turn allows only DARK Extra-Deck summons
- Step 4: 黑羽之旋风 recursion brings back 奇努克 (100 ATK); 苏德里 tributes 奇努克 to make 幻耀衍生物 (Lv2 tuner token) and takes 700 damage
- Step 5: 幻耀衍生物 plus 苏德里 Synchro into 魔风之玻瑞阿斯 10602628, which mills 突风之颪 73652465 and becomes Lv1
- Step 6: 玻瑞阿斯 as the Synchro Tuner plus 黑翼龙 Synchro into 黑翼强袭龙 73218989
- 西蒙 81470373 variant: set 黑旋风 91351370, Normal Summon Simoon (Lv6), search 突风之颪 73652465, Synchro Simoon plus 突风之颪 into 骤雨之雷切刀鸟 16051717, then extend next turn, accepting the end phase 黑旋风 self-destruct for 1000 damage

- **End Field**

- Standard end from the two-card combo: 黑翼强袭龙 73218989 plus 黑羽之旋风 7602800, with 雪击之奇努克 34976176 in hand as a quick negation for the opponent's turn (requires a face-up DARK Synchro, which 黑翼强袭龙 provides)
- 黑翼强袭龙 burns 700 and gains a 黑羽 counter on every opponent monster effect, then nukes the entire field for 4 counters on the opponent's turn; 黑羽之旋风 removes counters to keep the board alive
- Extended end options: 琰魔龙 红莲魔·渊 9753964 (玻瑞阿斯 as Lv1 plus 黑翼龙 9012916, quick negation each turn), 装弹枪管狞猛龙 27548199 (equips 智慧林鸮 36429703 from grave for one negation), 鲜花女男爵 84815190, 混沌之双翼 22850702

- Generic tuner extenders in the builds: 强袭同调士 77202120, 喷气同调士 9742784, 泣神的石像 5763020; opponent-turn climbing via 加速同调士 37675907 and 磷回之三弦猫 46057733

- **Halt Points**

- Stop the engine on the 幻耀之苏德里 70465810 search or the 黑旋风 91351370 search after a Normal Summon; both add from deck to hand and are negated by 灰流丽 14558127
- 无赖之伐他 71187462 mills from deck, so 灰流丽 14558127 stops the 黑翼龙 9012916 cheat-summon; 魔风之玻瑞阿斯 10602628 deck-mill is also Ashable
- 毒风之西蒙 81470373 and 岚沙之夏马 8571567 setting the two 旋风 spells from deck is not Ashable, verified in c81470373.lua and c8571567.lua which carry no deck search or deck-to-grave category
- 墓穴的指名者 24224830 on 苏德里, 伐他, 夏马, or 奇努克 in grave kills recursion and the negation; 无限泡影 10045474 on 黑羽之旋风 7602800 or on 苏德里 before its tribute effect
- 增殖的G 23434538 and 欢聚友伴·茸茸长尾山雀 42141493 force the deck to stop after one or two summons: make 黑翼龙 9012916 alone or search and pass, never run the full line under them
- 次元吸引者 91800273 removes the 黑羽之旋风 7602800 grave-and-banished recursion targets, so the engine stalls

- **Mirror Match**

- Both sides run the same search engine, so the game is decided by who resolves 毒风之西蒙 81470373 or 幻耀之苏德里 70465810 first and by 黑羽之旋风 7602800 recursion pressure; keep 雪击之奇努克 34976176 in hand for the opponent's Synchro turn
- 下弦之光弓鸟 54594017 destroys a face-up card once per duel, so use it to remove the opponent's 黑羽之旋风 7602800 or 黑旋风 91351370 and break their engine
- 疾风之盖尔 2009101 halves a monster, so use it on 黑翼强袭龙 73218989 before it accumulates 4 counters and nukes the board
- 重装铠翼鸦 54082269 is immune and steals wedge-countered monsters, so remove it with 琰魔龙 红莲魔·渊 9753964 negation or 下弦之光弓鸟 54594017 destroy before it steals your boss

- **Common Mistakes**

- 幻耀衍生物 is not a 黑羽 monster, so it cannot enable the 伐他, 哈麦丹, or 突风之颪 self-summons and cannot be a 黑羽 material for 重装铠翼鸦 54082269 or 孤高之银风鸦 33236860
- 苏德里 70465810 and 伐他 71187462 lock Extra-Deck summons to DARK Synchro for the turn, so do not plan Xyz or Link plays after them and do not summon 智慧林鸮 36429703 first
- 伐他 mills only non-tuner 黑羽, so 夏马 8571567, 突风之颪 73652465, 盖尔 2009101, and 奥斯特 17465972 cannot be its mill material
- 黑旋风 91351370 searches printed ATK strictly lower, so it cannot add 苏德里 70465810 (1400) after summoning 盖尔 2009101 (1300) or any equal-ATK card
- 黑羽之旋风 7602800 triggers only on Synchro summons that come from the Extra Deck, not on grave revives, and the recursion target must have lower ATK than the summoned Synchro
- 西蒙 81470373 destroys its placed 黑旋风 91351370 at the end phase for 1000 damage and locks the turn to DARK Extra-Deck summons, so close the game or accept the burn
- 黑翼龙 9012916 loses 700 ATK per 黑羽 counter, so self-damage sources like 苏德里 700 and 夏马 700 weaken it; remove counters with its burn effect before they stack
- 星影之苦剑鸟 95040215 grants an extra 黑羽 Normal Summon, so summon 苏德里 70465810 through it for a double 黑旋风 search in one turn
- 黑羽-双影 69973414 is a Trap in this card database, so set it or activate it from hand with 2 or more face-up 黑羽; its special summon is treated as a Synchro Summon
- 精锐之泽费洛斯 14785765 works once per duel and costs 400 damage; 刻夜之佐达 7459919 locks Extra-Deck summons to Synchro while face-up, so order your summons before using it
