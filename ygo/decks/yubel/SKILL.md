- **Core Mechanic: 破坏连锁**

- 于贝尔 78371393 destroyed by any effect except its own end-phase self-destroy → special summon 于贝尔-被憎恶的骑士 4779091 from hand, deck or grave
- 于贝尔-被憎恶的骑士 4779091 face-up leaves the field by any means including being used as link material → special summon 于贝尔-极度悲伤的魔龙 31764700 from hand, deck or grave, but a banished 魔龙 cannot be recovered because the search is hand, deck and grave only
- 于贝尔精灵 90829280 destroyed → special summon 于贝尔 78371393 from hand, deck, grave or banished; 精灵 also special summons itself from hand when the opponent declares an attack and searches any 于贝尔-named spell or trap (噩梦之苦痛 65261141, 永远的至爱 87532344) to hand or face-down on its own special summon
- Attack-position burn: 于贝尔 78371393 and 骑士 4779091 burn the opponent for the attacker's ATK before damage calculation when attacked; 魔龙 31764700 and 守护者 47172959 burn the attacker's ATK and destroy or banish the battled monster after the damage step
- 噩梦之苦痛 65261141 turns the wall into a lock: while you control a 于贝尔-set monster the opponent's attackable monsters must attack it, and battle damage from your 于贝尔-set monsters' battles is taken by the opponent instead, so attacking into 骑士 4779091 costs the attacker its own ATK
- 骑士 4779091 wipes every other monster on both fields at your end phase, and destroying your own 于贝尔 78371393 or 精灵 90829280 with it climbs the chain for free
- 噩梦之玉座 93729896 recursion: whenever a face-up 于贝尔-set monster on your field leaves by an effect, add a 于贝尔-set monster of one level higher or lower (10↔11↔12) from deck, grave or banished to hand and optionally special summon it ignoring summon conditions
- 献祭魔界莲 62318994: tribute itself to special summon a 于贝尔-set monster from deck, but only condition-free ones such as 于贝尔 78371393 or 精灵 90829280, never 骑士 4779091 or 魔龙 31764700
- 献祭魔界莲 62318994 during the opponent's turn: tribute it while you control a 于贝尔-set monster to change any monster effect into destroy 1 于贝尔-set monster on the field, a self-trigger that climbs your own chain
- 献祭魔界莲 62318994 in grave at your end phase with a face-up 于贝尔 78371393 returns to hand or re-summons itself, giving the deck endless fodder
- 地狱墓场蠕动者 24215921: quick special summon from hand while you control a face-up Fiend, then optionally destroy a 于贝尔-set monster you control to climb the chain; banish it from grave to special summon any other 0 ATK 0 DEF Fiend from hand or grave
- 于贝尔幻影 80453041 is a contact fusion wall (于贝尔-set monster plus 0 ATK 0 DEF Fiend shuffled back from hand, field or grave) that cannot be used as fusion material; its quick effect only works when the opponent owns a 于贝尔-set card and forces them to destroy it, so outside the mirror it is just an indestructible body

- Remove their monsters by banishing instead, for example S：P小夜骑士 29301450, and never point 无光之影 阿-宝·阿·库 4731783's destroy at their 于贝尔
