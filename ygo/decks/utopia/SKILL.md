# 霍普/我我我/怒怒怒 (Utopia / Onomatopoeia) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- True identity: the Onomatopoeia (拟声) engine deck nicknamed 福音 — 福音 08512558 swarms 我我我/怒怒怒/隆隆隆/刷拉拉 monsters from hand into Rank-4 Xyz, then ladders 霍普 84013237 into 霍普·翻倍 62517849 (Double-Wide) or 电光皇 56832966 for the 翻倍机会 94770493 one-shot OTK
- Four archetype families, all treated as 拟声: 我我我 (Gagaga), 怒怒怒 (Dododo), 隆隆隆 (Gogogo), 刷拉拉 (Zubaba); 福音 08512558 counts as all four so it is engine, material and extender at once
- Canonical build 210626希望我我我: 福音 08512558 x3, 拟声连携 06595475 x3, 拟声选择 85119159 x3, 刷拉拉番长-我我我外套 23720856 x3, 怒怒怒矮人-隆隆隆手套 59724555 x3, 希望皇 星光霍普 69852487 x3, 异热同心从者-升华贤者 04647954 x3, 我我我魔术师 26082117, 我我我妹妹 00387282, 我我我少女 03606728, 怒怒怒破坏者 57996334, 我我我书记 44250812
- Level spread: 福音/魔术师/手套/番长 are level 4, 我我我妹妹 00387282 level 2, 我我我少女 03606728 level 3, so 福音 plus one onomat in hand already makes a Rank-4 Xyz; 妹妹 plus a level-4 我我我 makes a level-6 pair for 彼端超霍普 21521304
- Most engine monsters are Warrior (福音/妹妹/少女/手套/番长/破坏者/书记/升华贤者/星光霍普), so 增援 32807846 is the generic searcher; only 我我我魔术师 26082117 and 我我我我魔术师 86331741 are Spellcasters

- **Core Mechanic: 福音 swarm and the Xyz-only lock**

- 福音 08512558 once per turn: special summon any number of 拟声 monsters from hand, at most one of each family (刷拉拉/我我我/隆隆隆/怒怒怒), all in defense; after resolving, no extra deck summons except Xyz until end of turn
- 拟声连携 06595475 discards 1 card to add up to two onomat monsters from deck, max one per family; 拟声选择 85119159 adds 1 拟声 card on activation and later sets every monster's level to match one onomat on your field
- 我我我妹妹 00387282 searches 1 我我我 spell or trap on normal summon (我我我学园的紧急联络网 21715135, 我我我抽卡 21831848), and its second effect turns itself and a target 我我我 into a level-6 pair
- 我我我少女 03606728 copies 我我我魔术师 26082117's level; when used as material with only 我我我 materials, the Xyz gains an on-summon effect that sets one opponent special summoned monster's attack to 0
- 我我我魔术师 26082117 changes its own level freely from 1 to 8, enabling Rank 5 plays such as 胜光 87911394
- Because 福音, 刷拉拉番长-我我我外套 23720856 and 我我我学园的紧急联络网 21715135 all lock the extra deck to Xyz, plan the whole ladder before activating the swarm

- **One-Card Combo: 福音 plus one 拟声 in hand**

- Normal summon 福音 08512558, special summon 我我我魔术师 26082117 from hand, Xyz both into 我我我侍 91499077 (Rank 4, 1900 attack)
- Detach 福音: 我我我侍 91499077 attacks twice this battle phase (1900+1900) and its second effect flips it to defense and redirects an opponent attack onto itself
- The same opening alternatively Xyzs straight into 霍普 84013237, a 2500 beater that negates one attack per material detached
- With 翻倍机会 94770493 in hand the same two cards become the OTK line; with 我我我枪手 12014404 the swarm trades for 800 burn or attack buffs

- **End Field**

- OTK board: 福音+魔术师 become 霍普·翻倍 62517849 directly, detach 1 to add 翻倍机会 94770493 and special summon 霍普 84013237 on top with doubled 5000 attack, note the summoned 霍普 cannot attack directly
- Finish line: the 5000-attack 霍普 declares attack on a monster, use 霍普's own attack-negate (detach 1) to disable its own attack, chain 翻倍机会 94770493 so it attacks again with 10000 attack in the damage step, one-shot
- 电光皇 route: overlay 霍普 84013237 into 电光皇 56832966, detach 2 in damage calculation for 5000 attack with a no-activation lock during its battles; 电光皇 itself cannot be Xyz material
- Control board: make two same-rank Xyz such as 我我我我魔术师 86331741 and 霍普 84013237, then 未来皇 65305468 on top and 未来龙皇 26973555 over it: 3000 attack, indestructible, quick negate one monster effect and steal that monster
- Grind board: 超升阶魔法-乌托邦之力 67517351 ranks 霍普 into 霍普德拉戈纳 95134948, which each turn detaches 2 to re-summon a Number 1-100 Xyz from the extra deck that can attack directly

- **Mirror Match: Onomat vs Onomat**

- Both players open the same 福音 08512558 line, so whoever resolves 未来龙皇 26973555 first controls the game; do not overcommit while the opponent still holds 翻倍机会 94770493
- The attack-negating 霍普 84013237 and the attack-redirecting 我我我侍 91499077 are the mirror's defense; keep at least one material on 霍普 or it destroys itself when attacked
- The 10000-attack 霍普 from 翻倍机会 94770493 ignores the mirror's small monsters, so the first resolved OTK usually wins the match
- 拟声蜥蜴 55088578 special summons itself from hand or grave while any onomat is on field and recycles your own Xyz from grave to the extra deck, refreshing 电光皇 56832966 and 霍普 84013237 for a second push

- **Common Mistakes**

- Search before you swarm: 福音 08512558 only special summons from the hand, so resolve 拟声连携 06595475 or 我我我妹妹 00387282 first to put the monsters you need in hand
- After 福音, 刷拉拉番长-我我我外套 23720856 or 我我我学园的紧急联络网 21715135, the extra deck is Xyz-only for the turn: do not attempt link or synchro plays
- 福音 summons in defense position, so the summoned monsters cannot attack; go straight into the Xyz summon
- 福音 takes at most one monster per family: with 我我我魔术师 26082117 and 我我我少女 03606728 both in hand it still summons only one 我我我
- 翻倍机会 94770493 requires an attack that was actually disabled; only 霍普 84013237's own attack-negate sets it up reliably in this deck
- Do not overlay 电光皇 56832966 under anything: it cannot be Xyz material, and its 5000-attack quick effect consumes two materials including the 霍普 beneath it
- 我我我妹妹 00387282's level effect changes both itself and the target to the sum: a level-2 妹妹 plus a level-4 target makes a level-6 pair for 彼端超霍普 21521304, not a Rank-4
- 怒怒怒破坏者 57996334 is a going-second tool: its hand special summon needs an empty field with an opponent monster, and its grave revive only triggers on a tribute summon
- 异热同心从者-希望贤者 31123642 locks the turn after resolving: non-Number monsters cannot attack, so summon and equip the 异热同心武器 before committing to the battle phase
- 霍普 84013237 with no materials destroys itself when attacked, so ladder it away or keep a material on it during the opponent's turn
