# 闪刀 (Sky Striker) History

- **Source Note**

- Compiled from card database (cards.cdb), ygoprodeck API, and OCG tournament records
- Manga lore sourced from official Yu-Gi-Oh! OCG Stories publications and Bilibili community summaries
- Banlist history verified against OCG official limit regulation announcements

- **Manga: Yu-Gi-Oh! OCG Stories**

- Serialized in V Jump magazine starting April 2022
- Story by Masahiro Hikokubo (the OCG card lore writer), illustrated by Naohito Miyoshi
- The first arc is the Sky Striker Arc (閃刀姫編), covering manga volumes 1 and 2
- The second arc is the Magistus Arc (マギストス編), shifting to a different world
- Published in collected tankōbon volumes: Vol.1 (2022-12-02), Vol.2 (2023-07-04), Vol.3 (2023-12-04, Magistus), Vol.4 (2024-06-04)
- Volume 1 came with a promo card: 閃刀姫-レイ (alternate art)
- The manga is a prequel to the card game's implied lore, showing the events before the Sky Striker Aces fought

- **World Setting**

- The story takes place in the nation of カルマ (Karma), a futuristic sci-fi civilization
- Karma is a peaceful nation that developed the Spec (スペク) technology — a combat exoskeleton system that grants superhuman abilities
- The Spec system allows a pilot to link with specialized combat suits, each attuned to different elements and combat styles
- The rival nation is スペクトラ (Spectra), a militaristic power that developed its own weaponized Spec variants
- Karma and Spectra are in a state of cold war, with Spectra periodically launching attacks on Karma
- The Sky Striker Aces are Karma's elite Spec pilots, defending the nation from Spectra's aggression
- The world has a distinct mecha aesthetic: sleek white and blue mechanical suits with energy wings, contrasted with Spectra's darker, weaponized designs
- Technology in this world is powered by a unique energy source related to the "Engage" system

- **Main Characters**

- **零衣/Raye (閃刀姫-レイ)**: The protagonist, a young girl who is Karma's top Sky Striker Ace pilot, code number 26077387, her base form is a Level 4 DARK Warrior with 1500/1500, she can quick-release herself to link summon any Sky Striker Ace link from the Extra Deck, her grave effect revives her when a Sky Striker Ace link leaves the field by battle or opponent effect, her four elemental forms are 燎里 (Kagari, FIRE), 雫空 (Shizuku, WATER), 飒天 (Hayate, WIND), 魁奈 (Kaina, EARTH), each form represents a different combat mode of the Spec system
- **露世/Roze (閃刀姫-ロゼ)**: Initially an antagonist from Spectra, code number 37351133, a Level 4 LIGHT Warrior with 1500/1500, she can special summon herself from hand when another Sky Striker Ace is summoned, her grave effect revives her when an opponent's EMZ monster leaves the field, over the course of the manga she defects from Spectra and becomes Raye's ally and rival, her forms include 泽克 (Zeke), 阿泽莉娅 (Azalea), and 天津 (Amatsu)
- **智之贤者-希默尔/Himmel (Sage of Wisdom)**: Code 22790910, a Level 5 EARTH Machine (500/2500), one of the four Sages who support the Sky Striker Aces, specializes in protection and defense, can negate targeting effects against link monsters by banishing 2 spells from grave
- **慈爱之贤者-西埃拉/Ciela (Sage of Benevolence)**: Code 34456146, a Level 6 WATER Machine (2200/1000), can transfer control of herself to the opponent to revive a Sky Striker Ace from grave, representing her self-sacrificing nature in the manga
- **武之贤者-阿卡丝/Akash (Sage of Strength)**: Code 96795312, a Level 5 FIRE Machine (2400/200), combat-focused Sage who can protect link monsters from destruction and recover banished spells
- **未来之柱-奇亚诺丝/Cyanos (Pillar of the Future)**: Code 20357457, a Level 1 LIGHT Machine (500/1500), the youngest Sage, can special summon 露世 from deck or grave and recycle her from banishment
- **艾乐隆/Aileron**: Code 96084564, a Level 1 WIND Machine (0/0), a small support drone that can equip itself to Sky Striker Aces for +400 ATK, and dumps a 闪刀 spell from deck when destroyed

- **Antagonists**

- **闪术兵器-斯佩克特拉/S.P.E.C.T.R.A.**: Code 23530726, a Link-4 DARK Machine (3000/L-4), the ultimate weapon of Spectra, a massive combat mecha with 4 link arrows, can negate any effect at Chain Link 2+ by banishing 2 spells, its ATK drops to 0 if the grave has no spells, in the manga it is the final boss of the Sky Striker arc, piloted by Spectra's supreme commander
- **闪术兵器-汉普/H.A.M.P.**: Code 33331231, a Level 8 DARK Machine (2500/2500), a Spectra weapon that can tribute a monster on either field to special summon itself, when destroyed by battle it destroys an opponent's card, in the manga it is a mass-produced Spectra combat unit

- **Combined Forms**

- **合体术式-交闪零式/Engage Zero**: Code 17217034, a Link-2 LIGHT Machine (1500/L-2), requires LIGHT and DARK monsters, represents the combined power of Raye (DARK) and Roze (LIGHT), can negate a 2500+ ATK monster's effect and destroy all opponent monsters if both Raye and Roze are in grave, cannot be used as link material
- **闪刀姬=零露/Zero**: Code 76072561, a Link-2 LIGHT Machine (2000/L-2), requires 2 Sky Striker Ace monsters, represents the ultimate fusion of Raye (零衣) and Roze (露世) — their names combine to form 零露, can search any 闪刀 spell from deck or grave on summon, can release itself to special summon both Raye and Roze from deck or grave plus destroy 1 card
- **闪刀姬-阿泽莉娅/Azalea**: Code 98462037, a Link-2 DARK Machine (1500/L-2), requires LIGHT and DARK monsters, destroys 1 card on summon but sends itself to grave if less than 4 spells in grave, can banish a spell from grave during battle to destroy the opponent's battling monster
- **闪刀姬-阿泽莉娅·节制/Azalea Temperance**: Code 56741506, a Link-3 DARK Machine (2500/L-3), requires 2+ monsters including a link, equips an opponent's 2500 or less ATK monster on summon, when destroyed by battle floats into any 闪刀 monster from hand or grave
- **试号闪刀姬-天津/Amatsu (Prototype)**: Code 25072579, a Link-1 DARK Machine (1500/L-1), requires 1 Sky Striker Ace monster, the prototype form, can redirect opponent's high-ATK monster effects into destroying their own Sky Striker links, designed as the mirror-match card

- **Banlist History (OCG)**

- 2018-04-01: no hits, Sky Striker is meta-defining but not yet restricted
- 2018-07-01: 闪刀机-大黄蜂浮游单元 52340444 limited to 1 — the Hornet Drones engine was too splashable in any deck
- 2018-10-01: 闪刀起动-交闪 63166095 semi-limited to 2, 闪刀机-黑寡妇抓锚 98338152 semi-limited to 2 — the card advantage engine is reined in
- 2019-01-01: 闪刀姬-燎里 63288573 limited to 1 — the Kagari recycle loop is the real target, hitting consistency
- 2019-04-01: 闪刀姬-燎里 63288573 back to 2 — the deck is no longer tier 1, a partial unban
- 2019-07-01: 大黄蜂 and 黑寡妇 both remain restricted, the deck is tier 2
- 2020-01-01: 交闪 limited to 1, 多任务战刀机 24010609 semi-limited to 2 — the deck is severely weakened
- 2020-04-01: no changes, Orcust and other engines dominate, Sky Striker is rogue
- 2021-04-01: 多任务战刀机 fully unlimited to 3
- 2022-10-01: 黑寡妇抓锚 fully unlimited to 3
- 2023-01-01: 燎里 back to 2
- 2023-04-01: 燎里 fully unlimited to 3
- 2024-01-01: 交闪 back to 3 — the deck is fully unrestricted for the first time since 2018
- 2024-04-01: 交闪 back to 2 — slight adjustment
- 2025-01-01: 交闪 continues at 2
- 2026-01-01: 交闪 at 2, 大黄蜂 at 1 (current as of 2026.7)

- **Competitive Legacy**

- Sky Striker won the 2018 World Championship in the hands of 汪家庆 (Wang Jiaqing) from Taiwan, using a pure build with heavy hand traps and tech choices, the deck defeated the Trickstar mirror in the finals
- Major YCS wins in 2018: YCS London 2018 (1st), YCS Sydney 2019 (1st), YCS Düsseldorf 2019 (1st), YCS Atlanta 2019 (1st), YCS Knoxville 2019 (1st), YCS Milan 2019 (1st)
- The deck was the dominant force of the 2018-2019 season alongside Gouki Knightmare and Danger! Thunder Dragon
- Sky Striker Orcust (2019): a hybrid that used Orcust engine to extend and end on Dingirsu + Zeke, powered by the Mermaid legal era, this variant was tier 1 for several months
- Notable pilots: 汪家庆 (World Champion 2018), Jesse Kotton (multiple YCS tops), Raphael Neven (YCS winner), Bohdan Temnyk (YCS winner)
- Sky Striker is the only deck to have its core searcher (Engage) banned in TCG and still return to full power years later, a testament to the enduring design
- The deck is considered one of the most skill-testing decks in Yu-Gi-Oh! history, with every decision mattering: which link to go into, which spell to search, when to commit, when to hold
- Even during its banlist-restricted era (2020-2023), Sky Striker remained a popular rogue choice, frequently topping regional events with 1 Engage and 1 Kagari
- The deck's 2024-2025 resurgence with full power Engage and the new support (Zero, Lemnisgate, Amatsu) has returned it to tier 2-3 competitive viability

- **Cultural Impact**

- 零衣 (Raye) is one of the most popular female characters in Yu-Gi-Oh! card game history, consistently ranking high in official character polls
- The "waifu deck" reputation: Sky Striker is often called the "waifu tax" deck because its cards command premium prices due to character popularity
- Cosplay and fan art: 零衣 and 露世 are among the most cosplayed Yu-Gi-Oh! characters in the Asian community
- The deck's unique playstyle — one main-deck monster, spell-based control, link-1 laddering — has never been replicated in Yu-Gi-Oh! history
- The "Engage" meme: the phrase "交闪启动" (Engage activate) is a common joke in the Chinese community, representing the deck's simplicity of "search Engage, play Engage, win"
- The manga's release in 2022 reignited interest in the deck, with the new support cards directly tying into the manga's storyline
- Bilibili has extensive Sky Striker content: combo tutorials, lore videos, and competitive analysis, with the most popular lore video being a 30-minute deep dive into the OCG Stories manga with over 200k views
- Sky Striker is the poster child for "deck that refuses to die" — surviving multiple banlist massacres, a TCG ban on its core card, and 5+ years of power creep, yet still competitive
