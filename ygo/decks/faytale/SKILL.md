# 妖精传姬 (Fairy Tail) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- TRUE identity: 妖精传姬 is Fairy Tail, setcode 0x1DB (475) — a Spellcaster engine, not 旧神 or a 圣月 archetype itself
- Classic core (2017-2019 era): 白雪 55623480, 辉夜 86937530 — generic 1850-ATK Spellcaster beatdown/disruption, still run in the modern build
- Modern full support (2025-2026): 玛奇莉勒 19144622, 辛德瑞拉 52022648, 塔利娅 42921475, 罗奇卡 65953423, Xyz 威凯特 27632520, fusions 讲述者 4026187 / 编写者 78021082, field 舞蹈会 56725612, spells 开始啦开始啦 19326613 / 故事尾巴 82119326, continuous spell 妖精的传姬 91957038
- 圣月 (Sacred Moon) engine pairs with it: 圣月之皇太子 雷古勒斯 96228804, 恩底弥翁皇国 34041788, 雷古勒斯之矢 60439482, 恩底弥翁的侍女 玻璃 22623509
- Representative build (folder 260228妖精传姬魔女术圣月之皇太子): Fairy Tail fusion core + 圣月 engine + 魔女术 garnish — 恩底弥翁的侍女 杰妮 7656689, 魔女术工匠·万能杰妮 64756282, 魔女术的创造 57916305, 魔女术代理师傅 9603252, 结晶魔术 光之泪 73664385
- Fusion package: 简易融合 1845204, 突然变异 46411259, 超融合 48130397, generic targets 迦楼罗 11765832 / 火焰翼侠 13243124 / 泥龙王 54757758 / 精灵克罗佐 66532962, link 圣魔之少女 阿耳特弥斯 34755994
- Alternate variant (260124妖精传姬灵使融合): 灵使/凭依 (Charmer) shell — 风灵使 薇茵 37744402, 凭依装着-薇茵 31764353, 精灵术的术使 91530236, 超逸融合 44886582, 大轮之灵使 48016074, 五阵魔术师 84433295 — same Fairy Tail core

- **Core Mechanic: 1850 Spellcaster Search Ladder**

- Every Fairy Tail main monster is Level 4 with 1850/1000 stats and Spellcaster race — 1850 ATK is the archetype's glue stat
- 辉夜 86937530: on normal summon search any 1850-ATK Spellcaster from deck; quick effect bounces itself plus one opponent face-up monster unless the opponent sends a same-name card from their deck/extra to grave
- 玛奇莉勒 19144622: special summons itself from hand or grave while a face-up 1850-ATK Spellcaster is on field (checks BOTH players' fields), then locks you to Spellcaster-only summons for the turn
- 玛奇莉勒 19144622: on summon searches a 妖精传姬 Spell/Trap or 妖精的传姬 91957038; third effect pays 500 LP to rename one opponent effect monster to 妖精王子 19144623
- 妖精的传姬 91957038: reveal a hand 1850-ATK Spellcaster (no same-name on your field) to normal summon it — grants an extra normal summon; once per turn reduces damage to 0 while an original-1850 Spellcaster is on your field
- 舞蹈会 56725612 (field): on activation add a Fairy Tail monster or 妖精的传姬 91957038 from deck/banished; Fairy Tails attack directly; once per turn when the opponent special summons, negate and rename one opponent effect monster to 妖精王子 19144623 (needs a Fairy Tail on your field)
- The 妖精王子 rename gimmick: renamed opponent monsters become fusion material for 故事尾巴 82119326, and 编写者 78021082 turns 妖精王子 on field into Spellcaster race

- **One-Card Combo: 辉夜 86937530**

- Starter: 辉夜 in hand, no other cards needed
- Step 1: normal summon 辉夜 → search 玛奇莉勒 19144622 (1850-ATK Spellcaster) from deck
- Step 2: 玛奇莉勒 special summons itself from hand (辉夜 is a face-up 1850 Spellcaster) → search 故事尾巴 82119326 or 舞蹈会 56725612
- End state: 辉夜 + 玛奇莉勒 on field plus one Fairy Tail Spell/Trap in hand; equip 故事尾巴 to 辉夜 for +1000 ATK
- Alt: overlay 辉夜 + 玛奇莉勒 into 威凯特 27632520, detach to mill 妖精传姬 fuel from deck

- **Two-Card Extension: 舞蹈会 56725612 + 辉夜 86937530**

- Activate 舞蹈会 → add 妖精的传姬 91957038 from deck/banished → activate it revealing 辉夜 in hand → extra normal summon 辉夜
- 辉夜 searches 玛奇莉勒 19144622 → 玛奇莉勒 special summons itself → searches 故事尾巴 82119326 or a second 舞蹈会
- End state: 辉夜 + 玛奇莉勒 on field, 舞蹈会 up, 妖精的传姬 up, 故事尾巴 in hand — two searches and an extra summon ahead of the one-card line

- **End Field**

- Standard: 威凯特 27632520 + 舞蹈会 56725612 + 故事尾巴 82119326 equipped + set 妖精的传姬 91957038, with a 妖精传姬 card kept in hand/grave
- 威凯特 27632520: original-LIGHT Fairy Tails you control are unaffected by opponent activated effects; detach up to 2 to mill Fairy Tail cards; grave quick effect re-summons itself by sending a LIGHT Fairy Tail you control to grave
- Fusion end: 讲述者 4026187 (your Fairy Tails cannot be destroyed by battle, quick negate by banishing a Fairy Tail card from hand/grave) or 编写者 78021082 (special summons a Fairy Tail from deck/banished, negates + renames on your non-EARTH Fairy Tail summon)
- With 圣月 engine: 雷古勒斯 96228804 + set 雷古勒斯之矢 60439482 (destroy a monster that battled this turn, or negate a normal/quick-play spell) + 玻璃 22623509 as a 2800 ATK beatstick
- 舞蹈会 56725612 direct attack + 辛德瑞拉 52022648 protection makes the 1850 ladies hard to interact with

- **Extenders**

- 开始啦开始啦 19326613: special summons up to 3 LIGHT Fairy Tails from hand/grave/banished (max 1 per location), then Spellcaster-only lock; self-re-sets if banished or sent from outside the field
- 简易融合 1845204: pay 1000 → fusion-summon 讲述者 4026187 or 编写者 78021082 (both Level 4) with no material — 讲述者's negate or 编写者's summon is live immediately
- 突然变异 46411259: tribute a Level 4 monster → 讲述者 4026187 / 编写者 78021082 / 泥龙王 54757758 from extra deck
- 超融合 48130397: discard 1 → fuse using both fields into 讲述者 / 编写者 / 迦楼罗 11765832 / 火焰翼侠 13243124, cannot be responded to
- 故事尾巴 82119326 grave effect: equip itself to a Fairy Tail then perform an extra normal summon of an 1850-ATK Spellcaster
- 威凯特 27632520 grave effect: quick summon itself by sending a LIGHT Fairy Tail to grave — recursion and removal dodge
- 玛奇莉勒 19144622 grave: re-special summons for a second search
- 雷古勒斯 96228804: special summons from hand revealing another Spellcaster (pay level x 300 LP), then searches 雷古勒斯之矢 60439482 or 恩底弥翁皇国 34041788
- 恩底弥翁皇国 34041788: adds 雷古勒斯 96228804 or 玻璃 22623509 from deck and can special summon a Spellcaster from hand; can destroy 雷古勒斯 from hand/field instead of your card being destroyed
- 玻璃 22623509: special summons from hand in a main phase after a Spell was activated this turn; searches 魔女术 Spell/Trap or 次元魔法; +2300 ATK while 雷古勒斯 96228804 is on field or in grave
- 魔女术: 魔女术的创造 57916305 (add a 魔女术 monster, grave recycle), 万能杰妮 64756282 (tribute + discard a Spell → special summon a 魔女术 from deck), 结晶魔术 光之泪 73664385 (mill a Spellcaster/Spell, or special summon on opponent effect)
- 辛德瑞拉 52022648: while on field neither player can target other monsters with Spell effects; discard a Spell → equip any equip spell from deck (recycles 故事尾巴 82119326, returns to hand at end phase)
- 塔利娅 42921475: flip → special summon from hand; tribute another monster to rewrite an opponent normal Spell/Trap activation into setting one face-up monster face-down
- 罗奇卡 65953423: on normal summon the opponent gains 500 LP and picks one of your top 3 deck cards for you to add — opponent chooses the worst, treat as situational
- 欢聚友伴·茸茸长尾山雀 42141493: discard on empty field → draw per opponent special summon from deck/extra, shuffle hand back over the limit at end phase

- **Halt Points**

- Spellcaster-only locks: 玛奇莉勒 19144622 and 开始啦开始啦 19326613 forbid non-Spellcaster summons for the turn — summon 小夜骑士 29301450 or 欢聚友伴 42141493 BEFORE the lock or skip them
- 威凯特 27632520's protection only covers ORIGINAL-LIGHT Fairy Tails: 威凯特 itself is DARK and not protected; 讲述者 4026187 and 编写者 78021082 are EARTH and not protected
- 讲述者 4026187's board wipe only triggers when fused WITH 妖精王子 19144623 as material — plain fusion or 简易融合 1845204 does not wipe
- 辉夜 86937530's bounce is dodged if the opponent sends a same-name card from deck/extra to grave
- 罗奇卡 65953423 hands card choice to the opponent — never rely on it as a searcher
- 雷古勒斯 96228804 pays the revealed monster's level x 300 LP — revealing a high-level Spellcaster is expensive
- 简易融合 1845204: summoned monster cannot attack and is destroyed at the end phase
- 舞蹈会 56725612 third effect is once per turn and requires a Fairy Tail on your field
- 妖精的传姬 91957038 first effect requires no same-name monster on your field
- 玻璃 22623509 only summons in a turn a Spell Card was activated — activate a Spell first

- **Mirror Match: 妖精传姬 vs 妖精传姬**

- The 1850 search ladder mirrors: whoever resolves 辉夜 86937530 into 玛奇莉勒 19144622 first gets search advantage
- 玛奇莉勒 19144622 summons off the opponent's 1850 Spellcaster too — chain your summon onto their 辉夜
- Rename race: 玛奇莉勒 ③ / 舞蹈会 56725612 ③ / 编写者 78021082 ② rename opponent monsters to 妖精王子 19144623, then 故事尾巴 82119326 fuses those monsters away
- 威凯特 27632520's immunity decides the board: original-LIGHT Fairy Tails (白雪 55623480, 辉夜, 玛奇莉勒) become unaffected by opponent activated effects while it is up
- 讲述者 4026187's quick negate (banish a Fairy Tail card from hand/grave) answers the opponent's 舞蹈会 / 故事尾巴 activations

- **Common Mistakes**

- Using 玛奇莉勒 19144622 or 开始啦开始啦 19326613 and THEN summoning a non-Spellcaster like 小夜骑士 29301450 or 欢聚友伴·茸茸长尾山雀 42141493 — the lock bricks the rest of the turn
- Forgetting 威凯特 27632520 is DARK: it does not protect itself, keep a LIGHT Fairy Tail beside it
- Missing that 讲述者 4026187's negate can banish from hand OR grave — always keep one 妖精传姬 card available as fuel
- Wasting 超融合 48130397 as a beatdown tool — it is unresolvable removal, use it to break the opponent's board
- 简易融合 1845204 only covers Level 5 or lower fusions — 讲述者/编写者 are Level 4 and fine, but 五阵魔术师 84433295 (Level 12) cannot be summoned this way
- 突然变异 46411259 needs an exact level match — tribute a Level 4 for 讲述者 4026187 / 编写者 78021082 / 泥龙王 54757758
- Playing 罗奇卡 65953423 as the main searcher — the opponent picks the card you get
- Activating 玻璃 22623509's summon window after no Spell was activated this turn — always chain a Spell first
- Forgetting 威凯特 27632520's mill is a cost, detach exactly 1 or 2 and send that many Fairy Tail cards from deck to grave
- 故事尾巴 82119326's fusion can only summon Spellcaster fusions — 迦楼罗 11765832 and 火焰翼侠 13243124 are not valid targets for it
- 妖精的传姬 91957038's extra normal summon does not stack with itself — reveal only one 1850 Spellcaster per turn
