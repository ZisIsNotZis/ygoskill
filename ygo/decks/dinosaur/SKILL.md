- **Deck Identity**

- The 恐龙 deck folders hold the classic Dinosaur OTK deck, a generic Dinosaur-TYPE engine, not 幻兽机 (Mecha Phantom Beast), not Dinomist, not a named setcode archetype

- **Core Mechanic: 幻创之混种恐龙 Graveyard Jump**

- 幻创之混种恐龙 38572779 quick effect from hand during the Main Phase sends itself to graveyard and makes your Dinosaur monsters immune to the opponent's activated card effects this turn, verified script e1
- Its graveyard ignition effect announces a level, banishes itself plus that level minus one other Dinos from graveyard, then Special Summons exactly 1 Dino of that level from deck, destroyed at the End Phase, verified script e2
- Level 4 target: 恐龙摔跤手·西斯特玛剑龙 56980148 which searches on Special Summon, level 2 target: 恐龙摔跤手·席拉腔骨龙 75366958 the tuner, level 3 target: 恐龙摔跤手·无限制重爪龙 80831552 or 恐龙摔跤手·卡帕扑翼龙 93507434
- Graveyard fuel comes from 食魂窃蛋龙 44335251 sending a Dino from deck, 手札断杀 74519184, or 小角龙 36042004 and 小翼龙 82946847 destroyed by card effect
- 食魂窃蛋龙 44335251 on summon adds or sends any Dino from deck, its ignition destroys 1 face-up Level 4 or lower Dino anywhere including the opponent's Lost World token, then revives a Dino from graveyard in defense

- **Common Mistakes**

- Activating 幻创之混种恐龙 38572779 with an empty graveyard, the dump does nothing without Dino fuel for the graveyard effect
- Forgetting the Misc Special Summoned monster is destroyed at the End Phase, do not treat it as a lasting body
- 小翼龙 82946847's summoned monster cannot attack that turn, it is setup not an immediate beater
- 暴君恐龙融合 15543940 uses field monsters as material only, make 恐龙摔跤手·大地跑酷冰脊龙 69121954 first, it cannot fuse from the hand
- 恐龙摔跤手·席拉腔骨龙 75366958 cannot be Link material except for Dinowrestler links, save its once-per-duel self Special Summon for the right line
- Attacking in the wrong order under 世界恐龙摔跤 90173539, the attack lock applies after the first attack
- 究极传导恐兽 18940556 destroys your own monster as part of its flip effect, sequence the destruction so a hand or field monster is available
- 进化帝·半鸟龙 74294676 needs two Level 4 Dinos as material, do not burn both materials on the first negation if the summon negation is still live
