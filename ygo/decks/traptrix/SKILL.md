# 虫惑魔 (Traptrix) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- Control trap deck: EARTH Insect/Plant monsters (archetype setcode 0x108a = 4234) that search, set and recycle the 「洞」 (setcode 0x89 = 137) and 「落穴」 (setcode 0x4c = 76) Normal Trap families, played as pure trap control in 260124虫惑 and 250426虫惑
- Main-deck core: 特莱恩 91812341 (trap searcher), 兰卡 82738277 (monster searcher), 普蒂卡 49027020 (searches 虫惑之园 12801833), 蒂奥 45803070 (graveyard trap recursion), 吉娜 28868394, 卡祖拉 28201945 (trap-activation turbo), 基诺 75416738 (hand special summon), 破洞露蒂亚 74577599 (trap monster), plus 阿特拉 55428242 and 莉塞 88078306 in splash builds
- Extra-deck core: 塞拉 73639099 (link-1 engine), 阿蒂普丝 48183890 (link-3 boss), 库拉莉亚 952523 (link-2 trap re-setter), 西托莉丝 1688285, 芙莉西亚 6511113, 阿洛美勒丝 59071624 (rank 4 toolbox)
- Key spells: 虫惑之园 12801833 (extra normal summon plus recursion), 虫惑的引诱 48805472 (draw 2)
- Key traps: 墓穴洞 31548215, 无底的落穴 69599136, 虫惑的落穴 29616929, 奈落的落穴 29401950, 断绝的落穴 89569453, 电网的落穴 84640866, 时空的落穴 2055403, 狂惑的落穴 75294187
- Every main-deck Traptrix is unaffected by the effects of 「洞」 and 「落穴」 Normal Traps, so your trap holes never touch your own monsters and the opponent cannot use trap holes against you

- **Core Mechanic: 塞拉 Trap Recursion Loop**

- 塞拉 73639099 is the whole engine: link-1 summonable from any non-link Traptrix, and a link-summoned 塞拉 is unaffected by all trap card effects
- 塞拉 effect two: whenever any Normal Trap Card is activated, including the opponent's, special summon 1 Traptrix from deck whose name is not face-up on your field, once per turn
- 塞拉 effect three: whenever another of your Traptrix monsters activates an effect, set 1 「洞」 or 「落穴」 Normal Trap from deck, once per turn
- The loop: activate a set 洞/落穴 trap → 塞拉 special summons a Traptrix from deck (蒂奥 45803070 to recover a trap from graveyard, 特莱恩 91812341 to destroy a card) → that monster's effect triggers 塞拉 to set another trap from deck → next turn activate it and repeat
- 卡祖拉 28201945 on field accelerates: each of your 洞/落穴 activations also adds or special summons one more Traptrix from deck, once per turn
- 库拉莉亚 952523 keeps the backrow permanent: after one of your 洞/落穴 traps resolves it is set face-down again instead of going to graveyard, once per turn
- 墓穴洞 31548215 is the premium search target: it negates an opponent monster effect from hand, graveyard or banished and burns 2000, and it works even against trap-immune monsters because it never targets the field
- The engine is a two-turn loop: traps set this turn are live next turn, so turn one is setup and the deck snowballs from turn two

- **One-Card Combo: 特莱恩**

- Starter: 特莱恩 91812341 in hand, no other cards needed
- Step 1: normal summon 特莱恩, add 墓穴洞 31548215 or 无底的落穴 69599136 from deck to hand
- Step 2: set the searched trap in a free spell and trap zone
- Step 3: link 特莱恩 into 塞拉 73639099
- Step 4: pass; end field is 塞拉 plus one set trap, the engine idle
- Turn two onwards: activate the set trap against the opponent's play → 塞拉 effect two special summons 蒂奥 45803070 from deck → 蒂奥 sets one 洞/落穴 trap from graveyard, which is the trap that just resolved → 塞拉 effect three sets a second trap from deck
- End field after the first full loop: 塞拉 plus 蒂奥 plus two set traps, and the loop re-fills the backrow every turn
- The search chain can extend: 兰卡 82738277 searches 特莱恩, and 特莱恩 searches the trap, so 兰卡 alone reaches the same line one step later

- **End Field**

- One-card 特莱恩 line: 塞拉 73639099 plus one set 洞/落穴 trap, with 塞拉's defensive summon ready if the opponent activates any normal trap
- Two-card 破洞露蒂亚 74577599 acceleration: set 露蒂亚, normal summon 特莱恩 91812341 to search 墓穴洞 31548215, link 特莱恩 into 塞拉, then activate the set 露蒂亚 discarding one Normal Trap from hand
- 露蒂亚 becomes a 400/2400 defense monster, its activation triggers 塞拉 effect two → special summon 蒂奥 45803070 → 蒂奥 sets the discarded trap from graveyard → 塞拉 effect three sets another trap from deck
- Result: 塞拉 plus 蒂奥 plus the 露蒂亚 wall plus two set traps, 墓穴洞 31548215 live from next turn
- 虫惑之园 12801833 line: 普蒂卡 49027020 normal summon searches the field spell, the extra normal summon brings 特莱恩 for the trap search, link into 塞拉, then 园 effect three banishes 普蒂卡 to re-special summon 特莱恩 from graveyard for its destroy effect and one more 塞拉 set
- Halt points: 灰流丽 14558127 on the 特莱恩 search, and 无限泡影 10045474 or 效果遮蒙者 97268402 on 塞拉, whose immunity only blocks trap effects

- **Extenders**

- 兰卡 82738277: normal summon searches any Traptrix monster; its quick effect returns one of your face-down spells or traps to hand and can set a card from hand, on either turn, freeing backrow space and dodging destruction of your set cards
- 普蒂卡 49027020: special summon banishes one opponent special-summoned monster, but it returns at the next standby phase, so treat it as temporary removal
- 基诺 75416738: quick effect from hand in either main phase while a Traptrix is face-up, no normal summon needed; after using it only Insect/Plant extra-deck monsters can be special summoned that turn, so finish 访问码语者 86066372 and 闭锁世界的冥神 98127546 plans first
- 吉娜 28868394: send one face-down trap you control to graveyard to special summon itself from hand; banishing it from graveyard, only when your spell and trap zones are empty, sets one 洞/落穴 trap from graveyard
- 莉塞 88078306: tribute itself to set one 洞/落穴 trap from deck and one from graveyard at once, but those set cards are banished if they leave the field
- 阿特拉 55428242: lets you activate 洞/落穴 traps directly from hand and stops your Normal Trap activations and effects from being negated, the only main-deck monster that makes same-turn search into activation work
- 虫惑的引诱 48805472: discard a Level 4 Insect/Plant or a Normal Trap to draw two; its graveyard effect shuffles a banished Level 4 Insect/Plant or banished trap back into the deck
- 沉迷陷溺 22377092: destroy one of your own spells or traps to set another Normal Trap from deck, and with three or more traps in your graveyard that set card can activate the same turn it was set
- 迷宫城的白银姬 81497285: Labrynth splash in the 250426 build, every Normal Trap activation sets a different Normal Trap from deck and she quick-special summons herself from hand, stacking with 塞拉's summon
- Extra-deck toolbox: 阿蒂普丝 48183890 negates up to your Insect/Plant count of opponent face-up cards and can banish a trap from graveyard to destroy one; 芙莉西亚 6511113 effect three sends a 洞/落穴 trap from deck to graveyard and applies its effect reactively; 阿洛美勒丝 59071624 steals opponent monsters that your effects sent to graveyard; 天霆号 阿宙斯 90448279 wipes the field after an Xyz battles; 未来No.0 未来龙皇 霍普 26973555 negates a monster effect and steals the monster; 灾厄之星 提·丰 93039339 locks out monsters with 3000 or more attack; S：P小夜骑士 29301450 for banish removal, 访问码语者 86066372 for the finishing line, 闭锁世界的冥神 98127546 going second with an opponent monster as material

- **Plant-Link Variant: 圣天树/圣种 (Sunavalon)**

- The 落穴圣种之虫惑魔墓穴 build pairs the Traptrix core with the plant link engine: normal summon 圣种之地灵 27520594 into 圣天树之幼精 93896655, then 圣天树之灰树精 44478599 up to 圣天树之大母神 92770064
- The variant boss is 圣天树之大母神 92770064: searches a 圣天树 spell or trap on link summon, cannot be destroyed by opponent effects, and cannot be attacked while it is your only monster
- 圣天树的开花 54340229 negates all opponent face-up monster effects while you control a link-4 or higher Plant, the variant payoff
- Traptrix monsters are Earth Plants and share the link arrows, and 基诺 75416738's Insect/Plant extra-deck lock is compatible with the plant line
- The Traptrix half is still the trap recursion: 塞拉 73639099 and the 洞/落穴 backrow play the same, with the plant link as an additional board

- 无限泡影 10045474 or 效果遮蒙者 97268402 on 塞拉 shuts the engine down: its immunity covers trap effects only, monster effect negation still works

- **Mirror Match: 虫惑魔 vs 虫惑魔**

- The 落穴-set traps are dead in the mirror: both players' Traptrix are immune to 「洞」 and 「落穴」 trap effects, so 无底的落穴 69599136, 奈落的落穴 29401950, 虫惑的落穴 29616929, 断绝的落穴 89569453, 电网的落穴 84640866 and 时空的落穴 2055403 all do nothing
- 墓穴洞 31548215 still works because it negates hand, graveyard and banished monster effects and never touches the field, answering 吉娜 28868394, 破洞露蒂亚 74577599 and 基诺 75416738 plays
- Use generic removal instead: 激流葬 53582587, 天龙雪狱 20899496, 阿蒂普丝 48183890 negation, S：P小夜骑士 29301450, 天霆号 阿宙斯 90448279, and battle over the 800 attack 塞拉 73639099
- 塞拉 effect two triggers on both players' trap activations, so every trap the opponent activates also summons a Traptrix from your deck, expect both sides to snowball in the same turn
- 芙莉西亚 6511113 effect three is the mirror answer for trap holes: the copied trap effect resolves as 芙莉西亚's own monster effect, which trap immunity does not stop, so copy 无底的落穴 69599136 or 虫惑的落穴 29616929 against their monsters
- Remove the opponent 塞拉 first; without it their engine loses the free deck summon and the deck set each turn
- 阿洛美勒丝 59071624 effect three steals their monsters after your effects destroy them, and 普蒂卡 49027020 banishes a special-summoned Traptrix for a turn

- **Common Mistakes**

- Searching a trap with 特莱恩 91812341 and not setting it: traps must be set a turn before activation, except through 阿特拉 55428242, 破洞露蒂亚 74577599 or 沉迷陷溺 22377092
- Forgetting 塞拉 73639099 effect two is once per turn and requires the deck target's name to be absent from your field
- 塞拉 effect three only fires when another Traptrix monster activates an effect, a plain summon or attack does not trigger it, and the trap it sets is face-down and not live until next turn
- 芙莉西亚 6511113 effect three costs both a material and a trap from deck to graveyard, choose the trap reactively, and the sent trap feeds 蒂奥 45803070 and 阿蒂普丝 48183890
- Xyz trap immunity only exists while the Xyz has materials: a material-less 芙莉西亚, 西托莉丝 1688285 or 阿洛美勒丝 59071624 can be hit by trap effects
- 普蒂卡 49027020's banish returns at the next standby phase, do not treat it as permanent removal
- 蒂奥 45803070's set trap is banished at your next end phase, activate it before then
- 吉娜 28868394's graveyard effect needs all your spell and trap zones empty, your own set traps block it
- 基诺 75416738's extra-deck lock lasts the whole turn and locks out the entire generic toolbox including 访问码语者 86066372 and 冥神 98127546, use it only after those plays
- Under 增殖的G 23434538 and 欢聚友伴·茸茸长尾山雀 42141493 every 塞拉 and 卡祖拉 28201945 special summon draws the opponent a card, play one trap and one summon or pass
- 次元吸引者 91800273 is a self-hurting tech in this deck, banishing also kills 蒂奥, 吉娜 and 虫惑的引诱 48805472 recursion, time it against graveyard decks only
- Do not expect 神之密告 78114463 or 无限泡影 10045474 to be searchable, the engine only searches and sets 「洞」 and 「落穴」 traps
