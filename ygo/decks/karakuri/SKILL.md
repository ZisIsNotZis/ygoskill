# 机巧 (Karakuri) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- EARTH Machine synchro deck: every main-deck monster must attack if able and flips to defense when selected as attack target, turning battle steps into position-change triggers
- Tuners: 机巧小町 二二四 24621460 (extra normal summon of a 机巧), 机巧参谋 二四八 66625883 (flips a monster on summon), 机巧蛤蟆 四六二四 75690317 (grave quick flip), 机巧守卫 三一三 70271583, 机巧桶 真九六 92300891
- Non-tuners: 机巧无双 八一八 24150026, 机巧兵 二三六 3846170 (battle-death floats from deck), 机巧忍者 九一九 6276588 (battle-kill floats from grave), 机巧商人 一七七 30230789 (searches any 机巧 card on normal summon), 机巧法师 九七六三 49296203 (special summons itself from hand as a tuner), 机巧忍者 七七四九 50615578 (draws for each defense 机巧), 机巧忍者 三三九 93724592 (flip sends a monster to grave, direct attack)
- Synchro bosses, all Tuner + 1 or more non-Tuner Machine: 机巧将军 无零 23874409 (level 7), 机巧大将军 无零怒 66976526 (level 8), 机巧大权现 无零武 25904894 (level 9)
- Support spells: 借机巧藏 80204957, 借机巧旅笼藏 3693034, 机巧蛤蟆油 11699941, 机巧粉 16708652, 机巧解体新书 85541675, 风云机巧城 22751868, 机巧大暴走 83831356, 紧急行车时间表 25274141
- Support traps: 借机巧整备藏 2924048, 机巧屋敷 33184236, 时限机巧爆弹 79178930
- Near-pure extra package: 幻兽机 曙光女神百头龙 44097050, 幻兽机 猎户座飞狮 72291078, 鲜花女男爵 84815190, 瑚之龙 42566602, 垒球小爱神 21915012; the old 水晶机巧-继承玻纤 50588353 line is forbidden in the active list and 科技属 超图书馆员 90953320 is limited to one

- **Core Mechanic: Position-Change Synchro Engine**

- The engine is the face-up attack/defense flip: every flip of a face-up 机巧 you control triggers the field effects of the synchro bosses and the support cards
- 机巧大将军 无零怒 66976526 draws 1 card once per turn when any face-up 机巧 you control flips between attack and defense, including when it is attack-targeted or flipped by cards
- 机巧大权现 无零武 25904894 banishes 1 card on the opponent field once per turn on the same flip trigger and makes your defense-position monsters indestructible by battle
- 机巧将军 无零 23874409 changes any monster's battle position once per turn as an ignition, a manual trigger source
- 机巧参谋 二四八 66625883 flips any monster on its normal or special summon, 机巧蛤蟆 四六二四 75690317 flips a face-up 机巧 from grave as a quick effect on either player's turn
- 机巧解体新书 85541675 adds a counter per position change up to 2, 机巧蛤蟆油 11699941 gives the equipped monster +500 per position change, 机巧屋敷 33184236 destroys a card per flip
- All three synchro bosses special summon 1 机巧 from deck on their synchro summon, chaining bodies into the next synchro
- Level ladder: 小町 3 + 法师 5 = 无零怒 8; 无零怒 8 + 蛤蟆 1 = 无零武 9; 无零 7 + 桶 2 = 无零武 9; 法师 5 + 无双 4 = 无零武 9
- Flip triggers only count face-up attack/defense flips; flipping a card facedown or face-up from facedown does not trigger 无零怒 or 无零武

- **One-Card Combo: 紧急行车时间表 (going second)**

- 紧急行车时间表 25274141 requires the opponent to control more monsters than you, it is a one-card two-body extender
- Step 1: activate, special summon 机巧小町 二二四 24621460 (level 3 tuner) and 机巧法师 九七六三 49296203 (level 5) from deck in defense with effects negated
- Step 2: synchro 机巧大将军 无零怒 66976526 (3 + 5), its effect special summons 机巧蛤蟆 四六二四 75690317 from deck
- Step 3: synchro 机巧大权现 无零武 25904894 (8 + 1), its effect special summons 机巧参谋 二四八 66625883 from deck
- Step 4: 参谋's mandatory summon flip turns 无零武 itself to defense, triggering 无零武 banish of 1 opponent card
- End field: 无零武 25904894 in defense (3000 attack, battle-indestructible defense) plus 参谋 66625883 plus one banish already applied
- The turn locks attacks to Machine monsters only (紧急行车时间表 25274141 restriction), synchro summons are unaffected
- 借机巧藏 80204957 is the follow-up: add any level 4 or lower 机巧 from deck to hand and flip a face-up 机巧

- **End Field**

- One-card going-second line ends on 无零武 25904894 in defense plus 机巧参谋 二四八 66625883 with one banish used
- Two-card going-first line 小町 24621460 + 无双 八一八 24150026: 无零 23874409 into 无零怒 66976526 into 无零武 25904894, 无零怒 draws once and 无零武 banishes once
- Opponent turn: attack-targeted 机巧 flip to defense, re-triggering 无零怒 66976526 draw and 无零武 25904894 banish
- 机巧蛤蟆 四六二四 75690317 banishes itself from grave as a quick effect to flip any face-up 机巧 again once per turn, a second trigger on the opponent turn
- 时限机巧爆弹 79178930 wipes all face-up opponent monsters when a defense 机巧 is attack-targeted, 借机巧整备藏 2924048 negates and destroys a spell or trap activation while a 机巧 sits in defense
- 风云机巧城 22751868 flips the attack target when your 机巧 attacks and revives a level 4 or higher 机巧 from grave when destroyed
- 无零武 25904894 keeps every defense-position monster safe from battle destruction, the real protection of the field

- **Extenders**

- 借机巧藏 80204957: search any level 4 or lower 机巧 and flip a face-up 机巧, the search plus trigger source
- 借机巧旅笼藏 3693034: flip your 机巧 and negate one opponent effect monster until end of turn; grave quick effect flips any face-up monster while you control a 机巧
- 机巧蛤蟆油 11699941: revive any 机巧 from grave and equip it, +500 attack and defense on each position change
- 机巧粉 16708652: battle-phase spell targeting two attack 机巧, one flips to defense and the other gains that attack value, a battle trick plus a trigger flip
- 机巧解体新书 85541675: up to 2 counters from position changes, send itself to grave to draw that many
- 机巧屋敷 33184236: destroy any 1 card when your face-up 机巧 flips between attack and defense
- 紧急行车时间表 25274141: one-card two-body extender going second, also searches a level 10 Machine from deck when it is sent to grave while set
- 机巧忍者 七七四九 50615578 draws cards equal to your face-up defense 机巧 count when summoned
- 机巧大暴走 83831356: +1000 attack with negated effects on one 机巧 for an attack push

- **Halt Points**

- Ash Blossom 灰流丽 14558127 on 借机巧藏 80204957, on the synchro deck special summons of 无零 23874409 / 无零怒 66976526 / 无零武 25904894, or on 紧急行车时间表 25274141 stops the line
- Negating the synchro monster's deck special summon or the synchro summons themselves kills the chain, the deck has no follow-up plan
- Each flip trigger is once per turn, a second flip in the same turn draws or banishes nothing
- Facedown flips do not count for 无零怒 66976526 and 无零武 25904894, only 机巧解体新书 85541675 also counts facedown flips
- 增殖的G 23434538 taxes every special summon of the chain, stop after 无零怒 66976526 and set backrow
- 原始生命态 尼比鲁 27204311 wipes the board on the fifth special summon, the one-card line reaches six summons
- 紧急行车时间表 25274141 is dead going first, it needs the opponent to have more monsters
- If 机巧参谋 二四八 66625883 summon flip is negated, the engine loses its only free trigger source that turn

- **Mirror Match: 机巧 vs 机巧**

- Position-change triggers only fire on your own 机巧, the scripts check the flipped card's controller, flipping the opponent's monsters fuels nothing for them
- Race to 机巧大权现 无零武 25904894, the first Bureibu wins the banish war because each of your flips removes one of their cards
- Do not attack a face-up defense 机巧 blindly, 时限机巧爆弹 79178930 destroys your whole face-up board; flip their defense monsters to attack first with 参谋 66625883 or 旅笼藏 3693034
- 借机巧整备藏 2924048 turns off their spell and trap activations while any 机巧 is face-up defense, hold a defense monster to answer their backrow
- 无零武 25904894 battle protection decides combat, keep yours in defense and force their attacks into it
- 机巧屋敷 33184236 destroys a card whenever your own 机巧 flips, chain it last to dodge negation and pop their engine pieces

- **Common Mistakes**

- Every main-deck 机巧 must attack if able, monsters left in attack position force attacks into 时限机巧爆弹 79178930 or bad trades
- Keep a face-up defense 机巧 for 借机巧整备藏 2924048, the counter trap is dead without it
- 无零武 25904894 only protects defense-position monsters, never leave it in attack
- 参谋 二四八 66625883 flip on summon is mandatory, when the only legal target is your own defense 无零武 25904894 you are forced to flip it to attack, plan the target in advance
- 机巧法师 九七六三 49296203 locks you into EARTH Machine extra-deck summons for the turn, make generic synchros first or use it as the final piece
- 紧急行车时间表 25274141 negates the summoned monsters' effects and restricts attacks to Machines, treat those bodies as material only
- 机巧粉 16708652 needs two face-up attack 机巧 and only works in the battle phase, do not hold it outside of it
- The 水晶机巧-继承玻纤 50588353 combo line of the old builds is forbidden in the active list, play the pure synchro chain with 无零武 25904894 as the boss
- 科技属 超图书馆员 90953320 and 死者苏生 83764718 are limited to one copy in the active list, do not build around multiples
