# 幻影骑士团 (The Phantom Knights) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- 幻影骑士团 (The Phantom Knights) is a DARK Warrior Xyz deck: Level 3/4 monsters with setcode 0x10db and spells/traps with setcode 0xdb, engine runs out of the graveyard
- Main deck monsters: 破洞鳞甲 25538345, 无声靴 36426778, 破手套 63821877, 沾尘袍 90432163, 污痕胫甲 88544390, 衰亡斗篷 46072770, 厄运钢甲靴 83566725, 脆铠甲 36704180, 裂头盔 99315585

- **Core Mechanic: 墓地堆砌与断碎剑阶梯**

- Every main deck 幻影骑士团 monster pays off from the graveyard, the deck first dumps cards then recycles them
- 无声靴 36426778 banishes itself from the graveyard to add a 幻影骑士团 spell or trap from deck to hand
- 沾尘袍 90432163 banishes itself from the graveyard to add a 幻影骑士团 monster from deck to hand
- 破手套 63821877 banishes itself from the graveyard to send one 幻影骑士团 card from deck to the graveyard
- 破洞鳞甲 25538345 discards 1 card to send one 幻影骑士团 card from deck to the graveyard, and its graveyard effect special summons itself whenever one of your 幻影骑士团 cards is banished from the graveyard
- 衰亡斗篷 46072770 reveals another 幻影骑士团 in hand to special summon itself, then adds a 幻影骑士团 monster from deck to hand when summoned
- 裂头盔 99315585 banishes itself from the graveyard to add one 幻影骑士团 card from the graveyard to hand at the end phase
- 无声靴 36426778 also special summons itself from hand while you control a face-up 幻影骑士团 monster, the standard starter pair with any other 幻影骑士团
- 污痕胫甲 88544390 special summons itself from hand whenever you special summon a face-up 幻影骑士团 monster, and can raise its own level to 4 for Rank 4 plays
- The Xyz ladder: two Level 3 monsters overlay into 断碎剑 62709239 (Rank 3), it detaches 1 to destroy one card you control plus one card the opponent controls, and when it is destroyed it floats into two same-level 幻影骑士团 monsters from the graveyard with level raised by 1
- 断碎剑 62709239 float turns two Level 3 monsters into two Level 4 monsters which overlay into a Rank 4, and two Level 4 monsters into two Level 5s enabling Rank 5 plays
- 破手套 63821877 as Xyz material grants the DARK Xyz monster +1000 ATK when that Xyz is summoned
- 锈蚀月刃斧 26692769 is a Link-2 of 2 DARK monsters: once per turn it sends a 幻影骑士团 monster from deck to the graveyard and then sets a 幻影骑士团 spell or trap directly from deck
- 锈蚀月刃斧 26692769 triggers when a DARK Xyz monster is special summoned to a zone it points to, destroying one card on the field, and it cannot be used as Link material
- The traps 幻影翼 98431356, 幻影雾剑 25542642, 幻影剑 61936647 all banish themselves from the graveyard as a quick effect to special summon one 幻影骑士团 monster from the graveyard, the revived monster is banished instead if it leaves the field
- 破洞鳞甲 25538345 recycles off every trap or monster banish, so banishing 无声靴 36426778 or a trap pays twice

- **One-Card Combo**

- Pure 幻影骑士团 has no true one-card starter, every full line needs a second card or a monster already on the field
- Closest single-card opener: 厄运钢甲靴 83566725 special summons itself while your field is empty and sets one 幻影骑士团 spell or trap from deck, ending on one body plus a set trap with no Xyz
- Baseline two-card combo: any 幻影骑士团 monster plus 无声靴 36426778 in hand
- Step 1: normal summon the first 幻影骑士团, for example 破手套 63821877, and special summon 无声靴 36426778
- Step 2: overlay the two Level 3 monsters into 断碎剑 62709239
- Step 3: activate 断碎剑 62709239, detach 1, targeting 断碎剑 itself and one opponent card, destroying both
- Step 4: 断碎剑 62709239 floats into two 幻影骑士团 monsters from the graveyard made Level 4
- Step 5: overlay the two Level 4 monsters into 暗叛逆超量龙 16195942 or No.86 英豪冠军 击灭枪王 63504681
- Step 6: rank up 暗叛逆超量龙 16195942 with 升阶魔法-幻影骑士团的出击 3298689, the target must have zero materials so resolve its detach-2 effect first, or Xyz summon 袭击队骑士 28781003 and climb it to 弧叛逆超量龙 64276752
- With two Rank 3 Xyz available, a second 断碎剑 62709239 or a trap monster ladder, overlay them into 未来No.0 未来皇 霍普 65305468 then 未来No.0 未来龙皇 霍普 26973555 on top

- **End Field**

- Standard interruption board: 锈蚀月刃斧 26692769 pointing at a DARK Xyz, one set 幻影雾剑 25542642 or 幻影翼 98431356 placed by its deck set, and traps in the graveyard ready to revive

- 天霆号 阿宙斯 90448279 can be Xyz summoned over a face-up Xyz that battled this turn and wipes the field

- **Mirror Match**

- Both players run the same graveyard recursion, whoever resolves 锈蚀月刃斧 26692769 plus set 幻影雾剑 25542642 first controls the pace
- 幻影雾剑 25542642 on the opponent's 无声靴 36426778 or 断碎剑 62709239 stops their ladder, the target cannot attack or use effects and cannot be selected as an attack target
- 墓穴的指名者 24224830 and 抹杀之指名者 65681983 decide the recursion war, save them for the opponent's key graveyard piece
- Stop the opponent's 断碎剑 62709239 float by banishing their graveyard 幻影骑士团 before the destruction or by negating its destroy effect with 无限泡影 10045474 so it cannot destroy itself
- Trap-revived monsters leave the field banished, use yours as Xyz material to keep them and force the opponent to waste recursion
- 天霆号 阿宙斯 90448279 wins the board wipe race, the player whose Xyz battled can stack it first
- 雾鸦爪 9336190 recovers your own banished monsters, both sides banish each other's recursion so the recovery matters

- **Common Mistakes**

- Special summoning 无声靴 36426778 as the first monster, it needs a face-up 幻影骑士团 already on the field, summon a normal or trap monster 幻影骑士团 first
- Activating 阴暗布面甲 98827725 the turn it is set after a trap already reached your graveyard, its same-turn activation requires no traps in the graveyard
- Filling all five spell and trap zones, 锈蚀月刃斧 26692769 sets from deck and trap monsters occupy zones, keep one zone open
- Forgetting 断碎剑 62709239 float restricts to DARK special summons for the rest of the turn, do not plan non-DARK summons such as 转生炎兽 独角兔 60303245 after it
- 断碎剑 62709239 destroy effect needs one of your own cards as target, with an empty field it cannot activate, use itself as the target to trigger the float
- Paying 破洞鳞甲 25538345 discard carelessly, the discard should set up recursion by dumping another 幻影骑士团 card
- 破手套 63821877 ATK gain applies only to the DARK Xyz it was attached to, put it under the monster that will attack instead of under material that will be detached
- Using the graveyard quick effects of 幻影翼 98431356, 幻影雾剑 25542642, 幻影剑 61936647 too early, they are the recovery engine, save one banish for the turn the body is needed
- 升阶魔法-幻影骑士团的出击 3298689 target must have zero materials, detach first
- Overpaying 升阶魔法-幻影之力 88504133 cost, banish exactly as many DARK monsters as the Rank gain, and remember the extra deck is restricted to Xyz summons afterwards
- Forgetting 幻影雾剑 25542642 is a Continuous Trap, it stays on the field vulnerable to backrow removal and destroys itself when its target leaves the field
- 幻影翼 98431356 protection is only once per turn, a second destruction still kills the monster
