# 刻魔 (Fiendsmith) Engine Experience

- **Engine Identity**

- 刻魔 (Fiendsmith) is a splash-in engine of LIGHT Fiend monsters + 刻魔 S/T that builds a small link ladder and a fusion boss 震怒之日 82135803 entirely from the engine, slotted into many 2024-25 decks as a free end-board enabler (variant lists in deck/240427刻魔*, deck/250927*, deck/260228*)
- Core searcher + line: 刻印群魔的刻魔锻冶师 60764609 (the starter), 刻魔的咏圣 98567237 (search spell), 刻魔的镇魂棺 2463794 (link-1), 刻魔的神圣棺 32991300 (link-2 revive-equip), 刻魔的大圣棺 49867899 (link-2 fuse-from-grave), 刻魔 落泪之日 46640168 and 刻魔 震怒之日 82135803 (fusion bosses)
- Support: 刻魔 赫赫君王 11464648 (big fusion), 刻魔的怜歌 26434972 / 刻魔的赞圣 35552985 / 刻魔的乐园 99989863 (trap/spell recursion and 刻魔衍生物 35552986 token)
- The engine is all LIGHT Fiend, so it is fully immune to most DARK/attribute locks and slots into LIGHT Fiend friendly strategies (Yubel 于贝尔, 千年, 荷鲁斯, 拉比艾尔, 蛇眼, dragon links, trap piles)
- 锻冶师 60764609 and 咏圣 98567237 need the machine to be open; a negate-heavy 灰流丽 14558127 or 效果遮蒙者 97268402 on the engine's first action stops the whole line

- **Core Mechanic: Link into Fusion from the Graveyard**

- 锻冶师 60764609 ①: discard itself from hand, add 1 刻魔 S/T from deck (usually 咏圣 98567237), the searcher and the hand-drop are the same card, so one 锻冶师 is both the discard cost and the searcher body
- 咏圣 98567237 ①: add 1 LIGHT Fiend monster from deck to hand then discard 1, pulling 镇魂棺 2463794 or 落泪之日 46640168 into hand for the ladder
- 镇魂棺 2463794 (Link-1, 1 LIGHT Fiend): ① in either Main Phase, release itself to Special Summon 1 刻魔 monster from hand or deck; ② equip a non-link LIGHT Fiend you control with itself as a link-equip +600 ATK
- 神圣棺 32991300 (Link-2, 2+ monsters incl a LIGHT Fiend): ① either turn, target 1 non-link LIGHT Fiend in your GY, Special Summon it and equip this Sacred Coffin as a link-equip; the equipped monster gains 600 ATK per link marker the equipped links point to and deals piercing to Defense monsters
- 大圣棺 49867899 (Link-2, 2 monsters incl LIGHT Fiend): ① in your Main Phase, use your own GY monsters as material and return them to the deck to Fusion Summon a Fiend fusion monster (typically 落泪之日 46640168), effectively a free fusion from the grave; ② equips itself as a link-equip that stops the equipped monster being targeted by opponent effects
- 落泪之日 46640168 (Fusion, 2 LIGHT Fiend): on fusion summon add or Special Summon a LIGHT Fiend from your GY/banished, drops all opponent monsters by 600 ATK, and ③ on being sent to the GY burns 1200 by shuffling another LIGHT Fiend back to deck
- 震怒之日 82135803 (Fusion, 锻冶师 + 2 LIGHT Fiend): ① either turn, negate up to link-marker count face-up cards on the field until the end phase, the mass-negate ender; ② on being sent to the GY sends 1 card on the field to the grave
- The engine is recursive: 落泪之日 ① and the 刻魔 ③ effects recover from the grave, and 咏圣 ② + 怜歌 ② fusion from hand/field/graveyard repeatedly, so the engine plays from the GY as much as on the field

- **One-Card Line: 刻印群魔的刻魔锻冶师 60764609**

- Starter: 锻冶师 60764609 in hand, nothing else required
- Step 1: discard 锻冶师, activate its ① to add 咏圣 98567237 from deck to hand
- Step 2: activate 咏圣, add 镇魂棺 2463794 from deck to hand and discard 1 (prefer discard a dead non-engine card)
- Step 3: normal-summon 镇魂棺, link it into 神圣棺 32991300 (or keep the link-1 and use ② to equip it)
- Step 4: 神圣棺 ① revives 落泪之日 46640168 or 锻冶师 from the GY and equips itself, then the equipped monster attacks for piercing
- Step 5: 咏圣 ② in the GY excludes itself to Fusion Summon using 锻冶师 + the revived LIGHT Fiend into 落泪之日 46640168, 落泪之日 ① searches back a LIGHT Fiend
- Step 6: keep fusing/reviving to 震怒之日 82135803 for the negate ender, or make 神弓 阿波罗萨 4280258 with the spare bodies
- The engine alone ends on 震怒之日 82135803 (2800 ATK mass-negate) plus whatever the host deck adds; in full 千年/Yubel shells it feeds 神弓 阿波罗萨 4280258 and the host plan
- 灰流丽 14558127 on 锻冶师 ① or 咏圣 ① kills the line; 墓穴的指名者 24224830 on 锻冶师 in the grave or on the fusion spell stops the grave recursion

- **End Field (engine contribution)**

- 震怒之日 82135803 (2800 ATK) with up to 2 link-markers' worth of face-up negates each turn, plus the equipped 神圣棺 32991300 boosting a revived attacker
- As a companion: 神弓 阿波罗萨 4280258, 刻魔的镇魂棺 2463794 re-equipped for the +600, and 刻魔 赫赫君王 11464648 if the host supplies the extra fusion/link material
- 怜歌 26434972 face-up or in the grave gives battle protection and a grave-exclusion fusion, and 乐园 99989863 set answers opponent special summons by dumping a 刻魔 monster to the GY
- 赞圣 35552985 makes the 刻魔衍生物 token 35552986 (LIGHT Fiend 1-star 0/0) for one free link/fusion material, and locks you to attacking with Fiends that turn

- **Splash Rules and Restrictions**

- 锻冶师 60764609 and 咏圣 98567237 lock nothing by themselves, but the engine's fusion summon and the mass-negate require LIGHT Fiend material, so keep the host deck's non-Fiend lines resolved before the engine's fusion
- 大圣棺 49867899 and 神圣棺 32991300 Special Summon and equip LIGHT Fiends only; the engine cannot summon anything else, so it is purely a LIGHT Fiend extender
- 赫赫君王 11464648 requires a 刻魔 fusion plus a fusion/link monster and needs the equipped 刻魔 to stay unaffected, a fragile tall option often skipped unless the host supplies link material
- The engine's fusion effects are not once-per-duel, so 落泪之日 46640168 ① and 咏圣 ② can loop to re-fuse every turn; disruption that hits the grave (墓穴的指名者 24224830, D.D. 乌鸦 24508238, 次元吸引者 91800273) is the edge against it
- Against the engine: negate 锻冶师 60764609's ① or 咏圣 ① to deny the search, and banish the grave 锻冶师 or the fusion spell to shut the recursion

- **Common Mistakes**

- Discarding the wrong Fiend for 咏圣 98567237's discard: keep 锻冶师 60764609 or a live 落泪之日 46640168 in grave, not the token
- Linking 镇魂棺 2463794 away without using its ② equip first when the revive matters, the link-1's equip gives a needed +600 and piercing access
- Trying to make 赫赫君王 11464648 without the linked-fusion material: it needs a 刻魔 fusion plus a fusion/link, and it requires the equipped 刻魔 to stay unaffected, most lists skip it
- Over-committing into 增殖的G 23434538: the engine all-in special summons multiple times, so stop at 震怒之日 82135803 or 神弓 阿波罗萨 4280258 and hold the rest
- Forgetting 大圣棺 49867899 returns its grave materials to the deck, so the fusion boss it makes is independent of the normal grave-recovery loop
- Using 震怒之日 82135803's negate on the wrong window: it is a quick effect during either player's Main Phase but the negate lasts only until the end phase, time it against the opponent's play
- 怜歌 26434972 and 咏圣's grave-exclusion fusion and 乐园's grave-dump all conflict with 次元吸引者 91800273-style banish-heavy hate, prioritize the banish counter-side when present
