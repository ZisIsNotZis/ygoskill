# 银河 / 银河眼 (Galaxy-Eyes) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- LIGHT Dragon rank-8 Xyz deck, core engine cards are 银河 (setcode 0x7b) and 光子 (setcode 0x55) monsters, 银河眼 Xyz monsters carry both setcodes plus 0x10
- Reference build: deck/230610银河光子/22804728ea9ef780.ydk, 60 cards, three copies of 银河召唤师 863795, 银河百式 897409, 银河战士 46659709, 银河魔导师 98555327, 银河眼残光龙 62968263, 银河远征 60222582, 光子三角翼飞机 47051709
- Boss ladder: 银河眼光子龙 93717133 into No.62 银河眼光子龙皇 31801517 or CNo.62 超银河眼光子龙皇 48348921, alternate tops are No.107 银河眼时空龙 88177324, 银河眼光波龙 18963306, No.90 银河眼光子卿 8165596
- OTK oriented: CNo.62 48348921 attacks three times, CNo.107 68396121 attacks three times, No.107 88177324 attacks twice, all with high ATK
- Every main-deck monster is Level 4 or Level 8 LIGHT, the ladder is purely two Level 8 monsters into rank 8, plus rank-up overlays into rank 9 and rank 10

- **Core Mechanic: the 银河眼光子龙 Ladder**

- 银河眼光子龙 93717133 special summons itself from hand by tributing two of your 2000+ ATK monsters, which makes it the universal ladder base
- In its battle step it can banish itself and its battle target until end of battle phase, dodging destruction and damage, gaining 500 ATK per overlay of a banished Xyz monster
- 银河召唤师 863795 on normal or special summon revives any 光子 or 银河 monster from grave in defense, and its ignition effect makes another LIGHT monster Level 4, enabling rank 4 plays
- 银河战士 46659709 special summons itself from hand by discarding one LIGHT, then searches any 银河 monster from deck, the main consistency engine
- 光子驱逐者 43147039 special summons itself from hand while a 光子 or 银河 monster is on field, then searches 银河眼光子龙 93717133 from deck
- 银河眼残光龙 62968263 special summons itself from hand while a 银河眼 monster is on field, and when detached as Xyz material it summons or attaches a 银河眼光子龙 93717133 from hand or deck, doubling all your No. Xyz attack in battle phase
- 辉光龙 光子爆龙 16643334 is the rank 4 bridge, on Xyz summon it special summons a 光子 monster from hand, and in the opponent turn it detaches to revive 银河眼光子龙 93717133 from grave or banished zone
- 银河光子龙 85747929 is the second rank 4 bridge, on a LIGHT monster special summon it changes one to Level 4 or Level 8, and it fetches a 光子 or 银河 card to hand or grave by detaching
- The ladder ends by stacking rank 8 Xyz on top of each other: No.62 31801517 into CNo.62 48348921, No.107 88177324 into CNo.107 68396121 via 升阶魔法-速攻混沌 33252803 or direct overlay
- 银河眼光波刃龙 2530830 overlays on a rank 8 银河眼 Xyz to become rank 9, 银河眼极光波龙 28346136 overlays on a 光波龙 monster to become rank 10

- **One-Card Combo Baseline**

- No strict single-card combo exists in pure Galaxy: 银河召唤师 863795 needs a 光子 or 银河 monster already in grave, 光子驱逐者 43147039 needs a 光子 or 银河 monster on field, 银河战士 46659709 needs a LIGHT discard, so every opener is two cards minimum
- Core two-card line: activate 银河百式 897409 dumping 银河眼光子龙 93717133 to grave, then normal summon 银河召唤师 863795 and revive it in defense
- Step 1: 银河召唤师 863795 second effect makes 银河眼光子龙 93717133 Level 4
- Step 2: Xyz both Level 4s into 辉光龙 光子爆龙 16643334, its effect special summons a 光子 monster from hand, ideally 光子驱逐者 43147039
- Step 3: 光子驱逐者 43147039 searches a second 银河眼光子龙 93717133
- Step 4: Xyz 辉光龙 16643334 and 光子驱逐者 43147039 into 银河光子龙 85747929, use its level effect to make 银河光子龙 85747929 Level 8
- Step 5: Xyz Level 8 银河光子龙 85747929 and 银河眼光子龙 93717133 into No.62 银河眼光子龙皇 31801517 with 银河眼光子龙 as material
- Step 6: overlay No.62 31801517 into CNo.62 超银河眼光子龙皇 48348921, or keep No.62 31801517 if the CNo.62 is not needed
- 银河百式 897409 second effect triggers whenever 银河眼光子龙 93717133 is special summoned while it is face up, check the opponent extra deck and banish one monster or steal one No. monster as your own
- 银河百式 897409 first effect is optional, choose what to dump, 银河眼光子龙 93717133 is the standard dump but 光子皇帝 73478096 is a valid alternate dump

- **End Field**

- CNo.62 超银河眼光子龙皇 48348921 with three attacks and monster effect immunity when 银河眼光子龙 93717133 is material, 4000 base attack, plus 100 per material level or rank
- No.62 银河眼光子龙皇 31801517 alternative, 4000 attack, its own damage calc pump of 200 per face-up Xyz rank, and revive on the second standby with doubled attack if destroyed by opponent effect
- 银河百式 897409 face up as extra deck hate, and 破灭之光子流 72044448 in hand to banish any card on field during either turn while a 银河眼 monster is present
- 银河栗子球 46261704 in hand or grave to intercept attacks and replace destruction of 光子 or 银河 monsters
- 光子跳跃者 97639441 in hand to end the opponent battle phase, or 光子轨道 89132148 equipped for 500 attack and battle protection
- Halt point: Ash Blossom on 银河召唤师 863795 revival stops the ladder, Ash on 光子驱逐者 43147039 search loses the second 银河眼光子龙 93717133, 增殖的G punishes the multi-summon line heavily

- **Extenders**

- 银河骑士 35950025 normal summons without tribute while a 光子 or 银河 monster is on field, then revives 银河眼光子龙 93717133 from grave at the cost of 1000 attack
- 银河魔导师 98555327 raises itself to Level 8, or releases itself to search any 银河 card from deck
- 银河远征 60222582 special summons a Level 5 or higher 光子 or 银河 monster from deck while you control one, grabbing 银河眼光子龙 93717133 directly
- 银河天翔 63956833 pays 2000 LP to special summon a 光子 monster from grave and a same-level 银河 monster from deck, both as 2000 attack negated bodies, locking your summons to 光子 and 银河 monsters for the turn
- 超银河 83512285 releases your 2000+ attack monster and a target opponent 2000+ attack monster, then special summons 银河眼光子龙 93717133 from hand, deck, or grave
- 永远的银河 82162616 overlays a 光子 or 银河 Xyz that is four ranks higher onto your Xyz monster, the standard rank 4 to rank 8 jump
- 光子圣域 17418744 makes two 2000 attack Level 4 Photon tokens that serve as 银河眼光子龙 93717133 tribute fodder, locking your summons to LIGHT
- 光荣的编号系 60944809 special summons a No. Xyz monster from grave when your field is empty, then draws one, and in grave attaches a hand card as Xyz material
- 银河修道师 28770951 attaches itself from hand as Xyz material, or on summon shuffles five 光子 or 银河 cards from grave into deck and draws two
- 光子皇帝 73478096 special summons itself from grave when sent there from outside the field, and grants an extra LIGHT normal summon
- 光子轨道 89132148 equips itself from hand or field for 500 attack and battle indestructibility, then sends itself to grave to search any 光子 or 银河 monster
- 银河零式 97617181 revives a 光子 or 银河 monster from grave with equipping, the monster cannot attack or activate but it is destruction-protected and becomes a ladder material

- **Halt Points**

- 增殖的G: stop the line at one Xyz, no benefit in pushing to CNo.62 48348921 and handing three or more draws
- 次元吸引者 91800273 and grave hate hurt hard, the entire engine runs through grave revival of 银河眼光子龙 93717133 and 银河召唤师 863795 targets
- No.62 银河眼光子龙皇 31801517 halves battle damage if it lacks 银河眼光子龙 93717133 as material, always ladder with the 光子龙 material or the OTK fails
- 银河天翔 63956833 and 光子圣域 17418744 both lock summons for the turn, do not plan a non-LIGHT or non-光子银河 play after resolving them
- 超银河 83512285 cannot release 银河眼光子龙 93717133 itself as the cost
- 银河眼光波刃龙 2530830 cannot be used as Xyz material at all, keep it as the final top
- 升阶魔法-速攻混沌 33252803 only works on No. monsters that have a same-number Chaos No. with one rank higher, so No.107 88177324 to CNo.107 68396121 is the valid target, No.62 31801517 to CNo.62 48348921 is done by direct overlay instead
- 异热同心力场 95856586 third effect needs 闪光抽卡 35906693 in the deck, the reference build does not run it so that clause is dead
- 银河召唤师 863795 second effect targets a different LIGHT monster, it cannot change its own level

- **Mirror Match: 银河 vs 银河**

- First No.62 银河眼光子龙皇 31801517 with 银河眼光子龙 93717133 material wins the damage race, race to the CNo.62 48348921 overlay
- No.90 银河眼光子卿 8165596 negates opponent monster effects and destroys them when a 银河 material is detached, the best mirror disruption piece
- 银河百式 897409 second effect strips the mirror opponent extra deck before their ladder can resolve, banish their No.62 31801517 or CNo.62 48348921
- 破灭之光子流 72044448 banishes any field card, removing the opponent 银河眼光子龙 93717133 before it becomes material
- 银河眼光子龙 93717133 battle banish dodges the opponent No.62 31801517 damage calc pump, banish your own 光子龙 and its battle target to avoid the 4000 attack swing
- 银河栗子球 46261704 and 光子跳跃者 97639441 are the defensive edges, they buy the turn the mirror ladder needs

- **Common Mistakes**

- Do not make No.62 银河眼光子龙皇 31801517 without 银河眼光子龙 93717133 material, the halved battle damage and lost revive both come from that material
- Do not use 银河召唤师 863795 level change after the Xyz summon window, change the target to Level 4 before overlaying
- 辉光龙 光子爆龙 16643334 only special summons a 光子 monster from hand, not from grave or deck
- 银河天翔 63956833 bodies are negated 2000 attack monsters, they are material fodder, do not rely on their effects
- 光子驱逐者 43147039 cannot attack the turn it is special summoned
- 光子圣域 17418744 tokens cannot attack and cannot be synchro material, but they are valid Xyz material and 银河眼光子龙 93717133 tribute fodder
- 银河零式 97617181 equipped monster cannot attack or activate effects, it is revival setup, not a beater, and its attack becomes 0 when 银河零式 97617181 leaves the field
- 银河眼极光波龙 28346136 costs two materials to protect your LIGHT monsters, keep enough material for the standby phase recursion
- 永远的银河 82162616 jumps exactly four ranks, a rank 4 goes to rank 8, there is no rank 12 光子 or 银河 target in the archetype
- The 波动龙 110001684 card referenced in deck notes does not exist in the cards.cdb of this YGOPro install, only 波动龙 声子龙 67556500 exists and it is not a floodgate nor present in the pure Galaxy builds, treat that hint as unverified
