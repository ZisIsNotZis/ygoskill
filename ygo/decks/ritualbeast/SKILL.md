- **Core Mechanic: Contact Fusion and the Return Loop**

- Ulti Fusion monsters are special summoned by banishing a Ritual Beast Tamer and a Spiritual Beast from the field as Contact Fusion materials
- Link Ultis are summoned with Ritual Beast monsters and share the same return-to-Extra-Deck effect
- During the End Phase any Ulti monster returns to the Extra Deck and special summons its banished materials in defense position
- This loop reuses the same Tamer and Beast every turn, so card advantage comes from searching and recycling while the materials stay in play
- 圣灵兽骑 雷鹰 48063985 searches any Ritual Beast card once per turn by returning two banished Ritual Beast cards to the graveyard
- 精灵兽 心企太 88123329 mills a same-race Ritual Beast from deck by banishing an Ulti from the Extra Deck, setting up graveyard recursion
- 灵兽的相绊 75457624 is a quick-play that banishes two face-up Ritual Beast monsters to summon an Ulti from the Extra Deck, usable on either turn

- **New Support Engine**

- 灵兽的继圣 22501005 reveals a Ritual Beast in hand to search a Ritual Beast of a different race and discard one card, fixing hands and enabling one-card starts
- 精灵兽使 蕾拉 23623653 can be discarded to perform an additional Normal Summon of a Ritual Beast, protect Ritual Beast cards by banishing herself from field or graveyard, and special summon a Ritual Beast from deck when banished
- 圣灵兽骑 星龙 69718652 protects other Ritual Beast monsters from targeting and can special summon a banished Ritual Beast on either turn
- 圣灵兽骑 风族 96113307 locks both players from tributing for effects, recycles a banished Ritual Beast to hand or Extra Deck for an additional Normal Summon, and banishes an opponent card on their turn

- **One-Card Combo Baseline: 精灵兽 雷鹰 49885567**

- The tutorial labels 雷鹰 as the primary one-card starter, opening into 圣灵兽骑 风族 96113307 plus 圣灵兽骑 星龙 69718652 plus I：P百变莱娜 65741786
- Normal summon 雷鹰 and activate its effect to banish a Ritual Beast card from deck
- Use 灵兽的继圣 22501005 or the banished material to bring a second Ritual Beast monster into play
- Contact into 圣灵兽骑 雷鹰 48063985, search 灵兽的相绊 75457624 or a missing Tamer
- Build the Link-2 圣灵兽骑 山飞隼 58811192 for an extra Normal Summon, then climb into 风族
- End with 星龙 on board for targeting protection and I：P百变莱娜 for an opponent-turn Link climb

- **Classic Two-Card Combo: 灵兽使的长老 14513016 + 精灵兽 雷鹰 49885567**

- Step 1: normal summon 长老, then normal summon 雷鹰 using the extra summon
- Step 2: 雷鹰 effect banishes 精灵兽 心企太 88123329 from deck
- Step 3: contact fuse 长老 and 雷鹰 into 圣灵兽骑 雷鹰 48063985
- Step 4: 圣灵兽骑 雷鹰 effect returns two banished Ritual Beast cards to graveyard to search 灵兽的相绊 75457624
- Step 5: during the End Phase 圣灵兽骑 雷鹰 returns to Extra Deck and special summons 长老 and 雷鹰 in defense
- This loop is the foundation that every extender builds on

- **Playing Under Hate**

- Against Nibiru: the tutorial dedicates a sheet to 防陨石 combos; build 圣灵兽骑 星龙 69718652 or stop summoning before the 5th summon, and use 地火狮 56655675 as a negate body if you can pay the hand cost
- Against Ash Blossom: chain Ash on 雷鹰 search or 圣灵兽骑 雷鹰 search is devastating; save 灵兽的誓还 8321183 or Called by the Grave if possible
- Against Droll & Lock Bird: the deck searches multiple times per turn, so a resolved Droll ends the combo; play compact lines that end in one search when Droll is suspected
- Against Dimensional Fissure / Macro Cosmos: the deck has dedicated 次元吸引者 combos because banishing is already its game plan, but graveyard recursion cards like 蕾拉 become weaker
- Against Infinite Impermanence / Effect Veiler: losing 雷鹰 or 长老 effect before contact fusion stalls the turn

- **Common Mistakes**

- Do not forget each Spiritual Beast and Tamer has a hard once-per-turn special summon limit; you cannot reuse the same copy in the same turn
- Do not search a trap with 圣灵兽骑 雷鹰 if you need an immediate extender; prefer 灵兽的相绊 or a missing Tamer
- 圣灵兽骑 雷鹰 returns to the Extra Deck during every End Phase, so leave zones open for the materials to come back
- 灵兽的相绊 is a quick-play, not a normal spell; hold it for the opponent turn when possible
- Do not overextend into the 5th summon against Nibiru; the 防陨石 sheet shows how to end early with 星龙 or Apollousa
- 圣灵兽骑 地火狮 56655675 needs a Ritual Beast card in hand as cost for its negate, not just any card
- New 精灵兽使 蕾拉 protection only works while she is in the graveyard or on the field, not after she is returned to deck
