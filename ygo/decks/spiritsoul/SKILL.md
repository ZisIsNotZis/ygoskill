# 灵魂鸟 (Spirit Bird) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- TRUE identity of the 灵魂 (Spirit) archetype is 灵魂鸟 (Spirit Bird): a WIND Winged Beast ritual-Spirit family whose whole game is tribute control, field wipe on ritual summon, and End-Phase return-to-hand recursion — not Yosenju 威风妖怪, not Drytron 龙辉巧, not the old 天岩户 stall alone

- **Core Mechanic: Ritual Wipe Plus Return Control**

- 灵魂的降神 73055622 Ritual Summons 灵魂鸟神-姬孔雀 25415052 or 灵魂鸟神-彦孔雀 52900000 from the hand, tributing any monsters from hand or field, or banishing Spirit monsters from the Graveyard as substitute tribute, until total Levels equal or exceed the target (Level 8 bosses, Level 4 影 forms)
- The 影 forms count as the bosses for 灵魂的降神 73055622: 灵魂鸟影-姬孔雀 33325951 is treated as 姬孔雀 and 灵魂鸟影-彦孔雀 60823690 as 彦孔雀 while in hand or on field
- 灵魂鸟神-姬孔雀 25415052 on Ritual Summon shuffles up to 3 of the opponent's Spells/Traps into the owner's Deck, then optionally Special Summons 1 Level 4 or lower Spirit from the Deck ignoring summoning conditions
- 灵魂鸟神-彦孔雀 52900000 on Ritual Summon returns up to 3 of the opponent's monsters to the hand, then optionally Special Summons 1 Level 4 or lower Spirit from the hand
- Both bosses are Spirit-type: on the End Phase of the turn they were Special Summoned they return to the hand and, if they actually returned, Special Summon 2 灵魂鸟衍生物 tokens 25415053/52900001
- The End-Phase return is a one-shot on the summon turn: after that turn a surviving boss stays on the field as a persistent 2500/3000 or 3000/2500 wall
- 灵魂的据所 9553721 gives all your Spirits +500 ATK/DEF and, once per turn when a face-up WIND monster you control returns to your hand, searches 1 Spirit monster or 1 Ritual Spell (灵魂的降神 73055622) — the boss End-Phase return feeds this every turn
- Only WIND monsters trigger 灵魂的据所 9553721, 灵魂的引诱 92394653, 灵魂的圆环 276357 and 星逢的神篱 15306543: the whole 灵魂鸟 family plus 剑道魂 皆传 71614230 are WIND, but 荒魂 16889337 (DARK), 和魂 24701235/幸魂 67972302 (LIGHT) and 天岩户 32181268 (EARTH) are not
- 天岩户 32181268 is the lock piece: while face-up, neither player can activate the effects of non-Spirit monsters, so an all-Spirit board plays freely under it while the opponent's hand traps and monster effects are frozen
- 灵魂的引诱 92394653 makes the opponent return 1 monster they control to the hand whenever a face-up Spirit you control returns to hand, with a 500 LP upkeep per Standby Phase
- 灵魂的圆环 276357 negates an opponent attack and skips their entire Battle Phase by banishing 1 Spirit from your Graveyard, and destroys 1 opponent card when a face-up Spirit returns to your hand
- 灵魂障壁 53239672 prevents all battle damage to you while you control a monster

- **One-Card Combo: 仪式的事前准备**

- Starter: 仪式的事前准备 13048472 alone, needs 2 Spirits already in the Graveyard
- Step 1: activate 仪式的事前准备 13048472, add 灵魂的降神 73055622 from Deck and 灵魂鸟神-姬孔雀 25415052 from Deck or Graveyard (the spell lists both bosses)
- Step 2: activate 灵魂的降神 73055622, banish 2 Spirit monsters from your Graveyard as substitute tribute, Ritual Summon 灵魂鸟神-姬孔雀 25415052
- Step 3: 姬孔雀 trigger shuffles up to 3 of the opponent's Spells/Traps into the Deck, then Special Summons 灵魂鸟-巫鹤 66815913 or 天岩户 32181268 from the Deck
- Step 4: End Phase, 姬孔雀 returns to hand and Special Summons 2 灵魂鸟衍生物 tokens 25415053; if 灵魂的据所 9553721 is face-up it searches 灵魂的降神 73055622 or a Spirit for next turn
- 万手神 95492061 variant: Normal Summon 万手神, add 灵魂的降神 73055622, then Ritual Summon from the hand with any 2 tributes or Graveyard Spirits, provided a boss or 影 form is already in hand

- 灰流丽 14558127 stops 万手神 95492061, 仪式的事前准备 13048472, 荒魂 16889337, 星逢的神篱 15306543 and the 影 searches, so bait it with the least important search first

- Self-halt: 天岩户 32181268 locks your own non-Spirit activations too, so resolve 万手神 95492061, ritual spells and hand traps before flipping it up

- **Mirror Match**

- First ritual summon wins: 灵魂鸟神-彦孔雀 52900000 bounces the opponent's Spirits and 灵魂鸟神-姬孔雀 25415052 shuffles their 灵魂的据所 9553721 and set traps, so whoever resolves 灵魂的降神 73055622 first strips the other's board
- 天岩户 32181268 freezes both sides' non-Spirit cards; the Spirit effects (影-彦孔雀 60823690 search, 巫鹤 66815913 draw) still work, so the mirror is decided by Spirit-only plays
- 灵魂鸟-传鸠 92200612 bounces the opponent's 天岩户 32181268 or boss to unlock your ritual window
- Token race: 2 灵魂鸟衍生物 25415053/52900001 (1500/1500) block 灵魂鸟-巫鹤 66815913 exactly, and 灵魂鸟-忍鸦 39817919 wins battles by discarding a Spirit at damage calculation, so read the opponent's hand before attacking
- 灵魂的据所 9553721 recursion race decides the grind: each End Phase both sides search 灵魂的降神 73055622, so hold the lock piece 天岩户 32181268 until the opponent has used their search

- **Common Mistakes**

- Playing 灵魂的降神 73055622 with no material: remember the tribute is any hand/field monster OR banished Graveyard Spirit, but the Level total must reach 8 for the bosses and only 4 for the 影 forms 33325951/60823690
- Summoning 灵魂鸟影-彦孔雀 60823690 and forgetting its search is a Tribute cost that only works if it was Ritual Summoned; keep one extra Spirit to feed it
- Setting up 天岩户 32181268 before using 万手神 95492061 or hand traps, then finding your own effects locked
- Expecting the bosses to return every End Phase: the return plus 灵魂鸟衍生物 tokens 25415053/52900001 happen only on the turn they were Special Summoned; afterwards they stay on the field
- Using 仪式的事前准备 13048472 to fetch the 影 forms: it only searches monsters code-listed on 灵魂的降神 73055622 (the two Level 8 bosses), so use 仪式的准备 96729612 or 星逢的天河 20417688 for the 影 forms
- Wasting 星逢的神篱 15306543 materials: it only tributes your field Spirits and tokens and summons a WIND ritual monster from the Deck, so count Levels (8 for a boss, 4 for an 影) before sacrificing field presence
- Forgetting 姬孔雀 25415052 and 彦孔雀 52900000 follow-up summons are conditional: the Spirit from Deck or hand appears only if at least one opponent card was actually shuffled or bounced
- Bouncing with 灵魂鸟-传鸠 92200612 without gain: it triggers 灵魂的据所 9553721 and dodges removal, but it also clears your own tribute material, so time it for the End Phase or before a ritual
- Treating 荒魂 16889337, 和魂 24701235, 幸魂 67972302 and 天岩户 32181268 as WIND: they do not trigger 灵魂的据所 9553721 or 星逢的神篱 15306543, only the 灵魂鸟 family and 剑道魂 皆传 71614230 do
- Letting 灵魂火阵 50418970 sit in the Graveyard: at your Draw Phase you may skip the normal draw to return it to hand, and it burns 600 damage when its equipped monster returns to hand
