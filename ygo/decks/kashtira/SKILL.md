# 俱舍怒威族 (Kashtira) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- Main deck monsters, all Level 7 except 莱斯哈特 (Level 4): 芬里尔狼 32909498 (EARTH), 独角兽 68304193 (WIND), 食人魔 94392192 (WATER), 莱斯哈特 31149212 (FIRE), 珠泪哀歌族型俱舍怒威族 4928565 (WATER), 恐吓爪牙族型俱舍怒威族 78534861 (EARTH)
- Extra deck Xyz: 香格里拉茧 73542331 (Rank 7, FIRE, 0 ATK / 3000 DEF), 阿莱斯哈特 48626373 (Rank 7, DARK, 3000 ATK), No.89 电脑兽 系统破坏神 95474755 (Rank 7, DARK, 2800 ATK)
- Spell/trap line: 停泊地 69540484 (continuous spell), 准备 21639276 (continuous trap), 重合 8953369 (quick-play spell), 六世坏-天魔世界 71832012 (field spell), 六世坏他化自在天 34447918 (normal spell), 六世坏根清净 33925864 (trap), 俱舍怒威族的恶修罗星 82286798 (quick-play spell)
- Techs seen in near-pure builds: 星球改造 73628505 searches the field spell, No.89 as extra-deck zone-lock enabler, hand traps 灰流丽 14558127 / 增殖的G 23434538 / 效果遮蒙者 97268402 / 原始生命态 尼比鲁 27204311 / 抹杀之指名者 65681983
- Search economy: 芬里尔狼 searches any 俱舍怒威族 monster, 独角兽 searches any 俱舍怒威族 spell (停泊地, 重合 or 恶修罗星), 食人魔 searches any 俱舍怒威族 trap (准备 or 根清净), 天魔世界 adds a monster on activation, 香格里拉茧 special summons a monster from deck each standby

- **Core Mechanic: Rank 7 Xyz and the Face-Down Banish Zone Lock**

- 芬里尔狼/独角兽/食人魔 all special summon themselves from hand only while you control no monsters at all, so only one of them can jump from hand per turn, the others must be normal summoned or summoned by spells
- 莱斯哈特 special summons from hand while any face-up 俱舍怒威族 is on field, and while summoned this turn can banish a 俱舍怒威族 card from deck as cost to banish the opponent's top 3 deck cards face-down and become Level 7, enabling the Rank 7 Xyz plays
- 香格里拉茧 73542331 is the lock engine: every time the opponent's card is banished face-down while it is face-up, it designates one unused zone (either player's main monster zone, extra monster zone, or spell/trap zone including the field zone slot) and that zone is unusable as long as it stays face-up
- The lock triggers once per banish event regardless of how many cards were banished, has no per-turn limit, and can target either player's zones, so prefer locking the opponent's main monster zones to deny summons
- Face-down banish is the deck's core removal and lock fuel: 芬里尔狼/独角兽/食人魔 ③ effects trigger on their attack declaration or on the opponent's monster effect activation and banish face-down a face-up card, an extra deck card, or a revealed deck card respectively
- 阿莱斯哈特 48626373 attacks from a different axis: cards sent to the graveyard from the deck are banished instead (per this server's script the redirect covers deck-origin cards only, not hand or field cards), and each time any card is banished it can attach one banished card, including the opponent's face-down banished cards, as Xyz material once per chain
- No.89 95474755 amplifies the lock: detach 1 to banish an extra deck card face-down, and once per turn when the opponent's card is banished face-down it banishes that many cards from the top of the opponent's deck face-down, feeding more lock triggers and threatening deck-out
- 天魔世界 71832012 ties the engine together: add a monster on activation, all your monsters gain 100 ATK/DEF per distinct attribute present on the field, and once per turn when your 香格里拉茧 activates an effect you destroy one card on the field

- **One-Card Combo: 芬里尔狼 into 香格里拉茧**

- Starter: 芬里尔狼 32909498 in hand on an empty field, nothing else needed
- Step 1: special summon 芬里尔狼, then use its search to add 莱斯哈特 31149212 from deck to hand
- Step 2: special summon 莱斯哈特 from hand (requires the face-up 芬里尔狼), this locks you to Xyz-only extra deck summons for the turn which is harmless in a pure build
- Step 3: activate 莱斯哈特 ②, banish one 俱舍怒威族 card from deck as cost, banish the opponent's top 3 deck cards face-down, 莱斯哈特 becomes Level 7
- Step 4: Xyz 芬里尔狼 and 莱斯哈特 into 香格里拉茧 73542331 with 2 materials
- End field from one card: 香格里拉茧 with 2 materials, 3 opponent deck cards banished face-down, hand untouched beyond the search
- The next standby phase 香格里拉茧 summons any 俱舍怒威族 from deck, and that effect activation unlocks the 阿莱斯哈特 overlay summon for the turn

- **End Field and OTK**

- Full combo with 芬里尔狼 + 莱斯哈特 + 他化自在天 + 天魔世界: activate 天魔世界 to search 独角兽, summon 芬里尔狼 to search 食人魔, 他化自在天 targeting 芬里尔狼 summons 珠泪哀歌族型俱舍怒威族 from deck in defense, summon 莱斯哈特 and make it Level 7, Xyz 香格里拉茧 from 芬里尔狼 + 莱斯哈特 and No.89 from 独角兽 + 珠泪哀歌族型俱舍怒威族
- End field: 香格里拉茧 + No.89 + 天魔世界 with up to 4 zone locks pending as the opponent's banished cards accumulate, every further face-down banish converts into one more locked zone plus a No.89 deck banish
- Turn 2 extension: 香格里拉茧 standby summon unlocks 阿莱斯哈特, overlay it on the summoned monster (keep 香格里拉茧 alive, its locks vanish if it leaves the field), attach banished cards as materials, then activate 重合 8953369 banishing a 1500 ATK/2100 DEF monster (莱斯哈特's statline) to give 阿莱斯哈特 +1500 ATK for a 4500 attacker
- OTK line: 根清净 33925864 wipes both boards down to one monster each while 香格里拉茧 locks the opponent's zones, then 阿莱斯哈特 boosted to 4500 by 重合 plus 芬里尔狼 or 食人魔 swings for lethal, 天魔世界 adds up to 600 extra ATK to every monster

- **Extender: 六世坏他化自在天 34447918**

- Target one face-up 俱舍怒威族 monster, special summon a 俱舍怒威族 with a different attribute from deck in defense, so 芬里尔狼 (EARTH) can bring 独角兽 (WIND) or 珠泪哀歌族型俱舍怒威族 (WATER) or 莱斯哈特 (FIRE)
- After activation you can only Xyz summon from the extra deck until end of turn, so sequence any non-Xyz extra plays first
- When banished it returns one face-up banished 俱舍怒威族 card to hand, recycling 莱斯哈特 or 芬里尔狼 after 重合 banished them

- **Extender: 六世坏-天魔世界 71832012**

- Field spell searched by 星球改造 73628505, adds a 俱舍怒威族 monster on activation making it a one-card start toward 芬里尔狼, and gives a passive ATK/DEF bonus for every attribute on the field
- Its destroy effect chains to every 香格里拉茧 effect activation, so the standby summon and each zone lock also become a free removal
- This card is not a 俱舍怒威族 card (set code 0), so 独角兽 cannot search it and 他化自在天 cannot recycle it

- **Extender: 俱舍怒威族的停泊地 69540484**

- Continuous spell: once per turn you can normal summon a Level 7 monster without tribute, letting extra 芬里尔狼/独角兽/食人魔 copies hit the field after the hand-summoned one
- Ignition effect special summons a non-Xyz 俱舍怒威族 from your graveyard or face-up banished zone, recycling monsters used as Xyz material or banished by 重合
- When the opponent activates a spell effect while you control a 俱舍怒威族, banish 3 cards from the opponent's graveyard face-down, feeding the zone lock

- **Extender: 俱舍怒威族的准备 21639276**

- Continuous trap with a quick effect that special summons a 俱舍怒威族 from hand or your face-up banished zone on either player's turn, the deck's best grind and interruption extender
- When the opponent activates a trap effect while you control a 俱舍怒威族, look at their hand and banish one card face-down, another lock trigger
- Remember it is a trap: it cannot activate the turn it is set, so 食人魔's trap search cannot produce a same-turn 准备 activation

- **Extender: 珠泪哀歌族型俱舍怒威族 4928565**

- Quick effect usable in either player's main phase: special summon itself and banish a 俱舍怒威族 or 珠泪哀歌族 card from hand or graveyard, a 2300 ATK defensive extender on the opponent's turn
- On summon it mills 3 cards from either player's deck, and when sent to the graveyard by an effect it mills 2 more, but under 阿莱斯哈特 those mills are banished instead so the graveyard triggers never fire and the milled cards instead feed 阿莱斯哈特's material attachment

- **Extender: 六世坏根清净 33925864**

- Trap that resolves while any face-up 俱舍怒威族 Xyz is on the field: both players banish all but one of their own monsters face-down, the strongest board wipe and a burst of zone-lock triggers in one effect
- Each player chooses which single monster they keep, so time it when the opponent is forced to keep a weak monster
- When banished, detach one 俱舍怒威族 Xyz material to hand and optionally special summon it, recycling 莱斯哈特 or 芬里尔狼 from under 香格里拉茧

- **Halt Points**

- 灰流丽 14558127 negates: 芬里尔狼's search, 天魔世界's activation search, 莱斯哈特 ② is NOT negated because it only banishes from deck, 香格里拉茧's standby summon from deck is the most punishing Ash target, 他化自在天's deck summon, and 珠泪哀歌族型俱舍怒威族's mill
- 增殖的G 23434538 draws per special summon: the combo routinely makes 4 to 7 summons, under G stop after 芬里尔狼 + 莱斯哈特 into 香格里拉茧 and hold the extenders
- 效果遮蒙者 97268402 negates 莱斯哈特 ② and breaks the Level 7 line, and 原始生命态 尼比鲁 27204311 clears the whole Xyz board if you pass 5 summons, keep the summon count low when they may hold it
- 抹杀之指名者 65681983 covers your own hand traps, chain it to the opponent's 灰流丽 on 芬里尔狼's search to keep the combo alive
- The deck's removal is all face-down banish, so graveyard hate matters less than hand and extra deck disruption

- **Mirror Match: 俱舍怒威族 vs 俱舍怒威族**

- First player to resolve 香格里拉茧 and start the zone lock usually wins, both sides run the same empty-field hand summons so the opening hand and who draws 他化自在天 or 天魔世界 decides
- 独角兽's extra deck banish and No.89 ① remove the opponent's 香格里拉茧 and 阿莱斯哈特 copies face-down, crippling their lock and overlay engine
- Chain your 芬里尔狼 ③ to the opponent's 芬里尔狼 search and banish it face-down before it can resolve, the mirror is decided by who lets the other's effects resolve
- 阿莱斯哈特 ① also matters here: the opponent's deck mills and deck banishes get banished instead of reaching the graveyard
- Keep 香格里拉茧 alive in the mirror, overlay 阿莱斯哈特 on the standby-summoned monster instead, losing 香格里拉茧 surrenders every locked zone

- **Common Mistakes**

- Do not summon 芬里尔狼 first if you also hold 独角兽 or 食人魔, the empty-field condition means only the first of the three can special summon itself that turn
- Do not search 准备 or 根清净 with 食人魔 mid-combo expecting a same-turn activation, traps cannot activate the turn they are set
- 莱斯哈特 ② requires it to have been normal or special summoned this turn, a 莱斯哈特 revived by 停泊地 or 准备 is a dead Level 4
- 阿莱斯哈特's overlay summon needs 香格里拉茧 to have activated an effect that same turn, it is never available on the turn 香格里拉茧 is first summoned
- An 阿莱斯哈特 overlaid on a monster has only 1 material and cannot pay its 3-material quick banish until ② attaches more, and overlaying it on 香格里拉茧 destroys all your locked zones, so overlay it on the standby-summoned monster instead
- 重合 needs a 1500 ATK/2100 DEF monster to banish for the +1500 buff, without a 莱斯哈特 in hand, field or graveyard the buff cannot resolve
- Do not lock your own zones with 香格里拉茧, the zone selection includes your own field and the lock lasts until 香格里拉茧 leaves the field
- 恶修罗星 82286798 only works with 维萨斯-斯塔弗罗斯特 56099748 and 维舍斯-阿修特罗德 65815684 in the deck, in the pure build without Visas its destruction recovery effect can never activate
- Under 阿莱斯哈特 your own deck mills are banished, so 珠泪哀歌族型俱舍怒威族's graveyard triggers never fire, plan around the banish economy instead of the graveyard
