# 罪宝蛇眼 (Sinful Spoils Snake-Eye) Deck Experience

- **Source Note**

- Based on the Bilibili tutorial BV1NJ4m1t79x by 时夏TSummer, 329k plays, 20-minute introductory guide
- Card IDs and combo lines verified against cards.cdb and tutorial slides

- **Deck Identity**

- Diabellstar engine: 黑魔女 迪亚贝尔斯塔尔 72270339, 原罪宝-蛇眼 89023486, 罪宝狩猎之恶魔 80845034, 叛逆之罪宝-蛇眼 24081957
- Snake-Eye monsters: 蛇眼梣树灵 9674034 (小蓝), 蛇眼桦树灵 12058741 (小粉), 蛇眼橡树灵 45663742 (小绿), 蛇眼炎磷 90241276
- Boss monsters: 蛇眼炎龙 48452496, 蛇眼大炎魔 27260347, 蛇眼原罪龙 58071334
- Spells: 篝火 85106525, 蛇眼神殿 53639887, 蛇眼追赶剧 26700718
- Traps: 睨统之蛇眼龙 74906081
- Generic extra: 赐炎之咎姬 2772337, 转码语者 46947713, 飞溅闪屏法师 59859086, 连接栗子球 41999284, S：P小夜骑士 29301450, I：P百变莱娜 65741786, 召命之神弓-阿波罗萨 4280258

- **Core Mechanic: Face-Up Continuous Spell Embedding**

- 蛇眼炎龙 48452496 places any monster from field or either graveyard face-up as a Continuous Spell in the original owner's Spell/Trap Zone
- During opponent's turn, 炎龙 can special summon a monster placed as a Continuous Spell to your field
- Many Snake-Eye monsters have effects that send two face-up cards (including themselves) to the grave to special summon another Snake-Eye from hand or deck
- 黑魔女 迪亚贝尔斯塔尔 72270339 special summons itself by sending any card from hand or field to grave, then sets any 罪宝 spell/trap from deck
- 原罪宝-蛇眼 89023486 sends a face-up card to grave to special summon a level 1 Fire from hand or deck, or banishes itself from grave to search + recycle

- **One-Card Combo: 黑魔女 迪亚贝尔斯塔尔 72270339**

- Step 1: discard 1 card from hand or field to SS 黑魔女, set 原罪宝-蛇眼 from deck
- Step 2: 原罪宝 send itself to grave, SS 蛇眼梣树灵 9674034 (小蓝) from deck
- Step 3: 小蓝 on summon search 蛇眼炎磷 90241276 or another level 1 Fire
- Step 4: 小蓝 + 炎磷 send themselves to grave, SS 蛇眼炎龙 48452496 from deck
- Step 5: 炎龙 effect place 蛇眼大炎魔 27260347 from deck as Continuous Spell
- Step 6: Link into 赐炎之咎姬 2772337 (Link-3), revive 炎龙 from grave
- Step 7: 炎龙 revive 2 level 1 Fires from grave, Link into 转码语者 46947713
- Step 8: 转码语者 revive 飞溅闪屏法师 59859086 from grave, make I：P百变莱娜 65741786
- Step 9: end board: 咎姬 + 转码语者 + IP, opponent turn IP → S：P小夜骑士 29301450 (banish 2)
- Net advantage: 1 card → full board with 2 interrupts

- **One-Card Combo: 篝火 85106525**

- 篝火 search 小蓝 9674034 from deck
- Normal summon 小蓝, search 炎磷 90241276
- 小蓝 + 炎磷 send themselves, SS 炎龙 48452496
- Same line as above into 咎姬 → 转码 → IP → 小夜
- 篝火 is effectively 3 more copies of 小蓝 in the deck

- **Two-Card Combo: 黑魔女 + 篝火**

- 黑魔女 discard 1, set 原罪宝
- 篝火 search 小蓝
- NS 小蓝, search 炎磷
- 原罪宝 send itself, SS 小粉 12058741 from deck
- 小粉 + 炎磷 send themselves, SS 炎龙
- Extra extender 小粉 gives one more body → 咎姬 + 转码 + IP + 神弓 or 大炎魔
- 大炎魔 27260347 revives itself from S/T zone as a 2800 ATK beater with removal effect

- **Snake-Eye Monster Roles**

- 蛇眼梣树灵 9674034 (小蓝) on summon searches any level 1 Fire, then sends itself + another face-up card to SS a Snake-Eye from deck; primary starter
- 蛇眼桦树灵 12058741 (小粉) special summons from hand if you control a Fire, then sends itself + another face-up card to SS a Snake-Eye from deck during opponent's turn; extender + hand trap
- 蛇眼橡树灵 45663742 (小绿) on summon retrieves a banished or grave level 1 Fire, then sends itself + another face-up card to SS; recycler
- 蛇眼炎磷 90241276 triggers when added to hand outside draw, then searches a Snake-Eye spell/trap; the go-to search target for 小蓝
- 蛇眼炎龙 48452496 is the engine linchpin: embeds monsters as Continuous Spells, revives them on opponent's turn, and floats into 2 level 1 Fires when sent to grave

- **End Board Targets**

- 赐炎之咎姬 2772337: Link-3, revives Fire from grave, floats from grave by destroying a Fire + opponent's monster
- 转码语者 46947713: Link-3, revives Link-3 or lower Cyberse from grave in linked zone, gives targeting protection
- I：P百变莱娜 65741786: Link-2, opponent's turn Link climb into S：P小夜骑士 29301450 for 2 banishes
- 召命之神弓-阿波罗萨 4280258: Link-4, 4-material monster negate
- 蛇眼大炎魔 27260347: 2800 ATK, embeds battling monsters as Continuous Spells, revives self from S/T zone

- **Playing Under Hate**

- Against Ash Blossom: 篝火 search or 小蓝 search is the Ash target; if it resolves, the rest of the combo does not need to search
- Against Nibiru: 咎姬 can be made before the 5th summon; if Nibiru is dropped, 咎姬 floats from grave by destroying a Fire on field + Nibiru token
- Against Infinite Impermanence: 小蓝 on-summon search is the Imperm target; if Impermed, 小蓝 can still send itself + another card to grave for cost
- Against Droll & Lock Bird: 篝火 and 小蓝 both search, so Droll shuts down the turn; end on 炎龙 + 1 set 原罪宝
- Against D.D. Crow or Called by the Grave: targeting 炎龙 in grave stops the float; prioritize making 咎姬 before 炎龙 hits grave

- **Common Mistakes**

- 黑魔女 must discard a card from hand or field to SS; if you have no cards, she cannot be summoned
- 原罪宝 sends itself to grave as cost, so it cannot also be used for 小蓝's self-send cost in the same chain
- 小蓝 + 小粉 send themselves AND another face-up card; if you have no other face-up card, the effect cannot activate
- 转码语者 locks you to Cyberse from Extra Deck for the rest of the turn after using its revival effect; plan the Link order
- 咎姬 locks you to Fire from Extra Deck for the rest of the turn while it is on field; do not make 咎姬 before non-Fire Links
- 炎龙's revive effect on opponent's turn requires the target to be a Continuous Spell monster card; if the opponent removes it, 炎龙 cannot revive
- 大炎魔 revives itself from S/T zone by targeting a Fire in grave; if there is no Fire in grave, it cannot revive
