# @火灵天星 (@Ignister) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- Pure @Ignister build sourced from corpus deck 250426火灵天星, 52 main + 10 extra, newest (2025-04) representative of the archetype, distinct from the sibling cyberse hybrid 260228@火灵天星电子界 covered in the cyberse deck doc
- Cyberse-type toolbox OTK: link climb through 辣辣妖 15808381 into 暗幼童 74567889, then 暗骑士 97383507, then the boss 电子界到临者@火灵天星 11738489
- The pure build drops the ritual engine of the hybrid (电子界贤者 65037172, 代码点火员 05088741, 电子界契约魔女 37458564) and the code talker finish (转码语者 46947713, 访问码语者 86066372), keeping only 水海龙@火灵天星 37061511 plus 艾之仪式 85327820 as a splash
- The 2025 support wave is core: 备份员@火灵天星 30118811, 男巫@火灵天星 3723262, 猞猁连接杀手@火灵天星 61168637, 连接栗栗@火灵天星 66102515, 协心代码语者@火灵天星 39138610, 相连相系-协“艾”- 79015062
- A trap layer (冤家作秀-喜与“艾” 54374642, “艾”宝-搭档 91509824, 生死决斗-节“艾”- 28645123, “艾”眼之影 77421977) replaces the hybrid ritual package, plus burn support 阴云栗仔球@火灵天星 14146794 and 刺刺妖@火灵天星 55762976
- Nearly every effect locks you to Cyberse-type special summons for the turn, so the deck is fully self-contained and must commit before splashing non-Cyberse

- **Core Mechanic: The Ai-Land Ladder**

- 辣辣妖@火灵天星 15808381 on summon adds any 4 star or lower @Ignister from deck, and its grave effect destroys your own battling Cyberse to dodge battle traps
- Link 辣辣妖 into 暗幼童@火灵天星 74567889, a Link 1 over one non-Link @Ignister, whose summon searches the field spell 火灵天星“艾”心乐园岛 59054773
- 艾心乐园岛 59054773 ignites only while your main monster zones are empty, specials any 4 star or lower @Ignister from hand, then locks you to Cyberse summons and to one special per original attribute
- 备份员@火灵天星 30118811 is a tuner that specials itself from hand while an extra-deck Cyberse is on field, then searches a DARK Cyberse and discards 1, feeding the grave
- 男巫@火灵天星 3723262 from hand specials itself plus a DARK Cyberse from grave in defense while an extra-deck Cyberse is on field, then locks you to Cyberse summons
- 郁郁妖@火灵天星 88413677 adds an @Ignister from grave to hand on summon, and as Cyberse link material adds an “艾” spell or trap from grave
- 暗骑士@火灵天星 97383507 is a Link 3 of three differently-named monsters that specials 4 star or lower @Ignister from grave to its zones whenever a monster is special summoned to them, and revives any Cyberse after battle destruction
- 电子界到临者@火灵天星 11738489 is the payoff: a Link 6 of three or more monsters with all-different attributes, base ATK equal to material count times 1000, unaffected by other card effects, with a once-per-turn destroy plus token summon

- **One-Card Combo: 辣辣妖@火灵天星**

- Starter: 辣辣妖@火灵天星 15808381 in hand plus 1 discardable DARK Cyberse (郁郁妖@火灵天星 88413677, 连接栗栗@火灵天星 66102515 or 阴云栗仔球@火灵天星 14146794), because 备份员's search discards as part of resolution and 男巫 needs that DARK Cyberse in grave to revive it
- Step 1: normal summon 辣辣妖, add 备份员@火灵天星 30118811 from deck
- Step 2: link 辣辣妖 into 暗幼童@火灵天星 74567889, add 火灵天星“艾”心乐园岛 59054773
- Step 3: activate 艾心乐园岛, special 备份员 from hand, then 备份员 searches DARK 男巫@火灵天星 3723262 and discards 1 (discard 郁郁妖@火灵天星 88413677, the DARK Level 4 that later becomes 光龙王 material)
- Step 4: activate 男巫 from hand, special itself and the discarded 郁郁妖 from grave in defense, locking you to Cyberse summons for the turn
- Step 5: link 暗幼童, 备份员 and 男巫 into 暗骑士@火灵天星 97383507, three differently-named materials, leaving 郁郁妖 on field
- Step 6: the finish needs a fourth body in hand, so with 连接栗栗@火灵天星 66102515 drawn it quick-specials itself by tributing 郁郁妖, landing in 暗骑士 zone and triggering 暗骑士 to revive 备份员 and 男巫 from grave negated
- Step 7: link 连接栗栗, 备份员 and 男巫 into 炎凤凰@火灵天星 24882256, leaving 暗骑士 as the DARK body and 炎凤凰 as the FIRE body
- Step 8: 电子界到临者@火灵天星 11738489 needs three or more all-different attributes, so the third body is 光龙王@火灵天星 61399402 (Xyz from 男巫 and 郁郁妖 when both are held back) for 3000 ATK, or 风飞马@火灵天星 98506199 (synchro 7 via 抖抖妖@火灵天星 42429678) and 地石人@火灵天星 62111090 (fusion via “艾”慕融合 59332125) as extra distinct attributes for 4000 or more
- Halt point: Ash Blossom 灰流丽 14558127 on 辣辣妖, 暗幼童 or 备份员 search, or 无限泡影 10045474 on 暗骑士, leaves a Link 1 and no field spell

- **Common Mistakes**

- Summoning 电子界到临者@火灵天星 11738489 with duplicate attributes among materials, the summon fails because it needs three or more all-different attributes
- Using 火灵天星“艾”心乐园岛 59054773 while a monster occupies a main monster zone, failing its activation condition, or ignoring its one-special-per-attribute lock
- Summoning 暗骑士@火灵天星 97383507 with fewer than three differently-named materials, or wasting 到临者 materials on duplicate attributes
- Activating 不期而遇-妨“艾”- 6552971 without following through on the revealed 2300 ATK monster, eating 2300 damage at end phase
- Breaking the Cyberse special summon lock by activating 男巫@火灵天星 3723262, 相连相系-协“艾”- 79015062 or “艾”闲者苏生 22933016 after committing non-Cyberse plays
- Forgetting 备份员@火灵天星 30118811 discard is mandatory after the search, and that 男巫 needs both itself and a DARK Cyberse in grave to resolve
- Over-committing against 增殖的G 23434538 instead of stopping at 暗幼童 plus one search, and summoning 协心代码语者@火灵天星 39138610 before the final link because it locks all further summons
- Forgetting 阴云栗仔球@火灵天星 14146794 doubles burn damage, so pair 炎凤凰@火灵天星 24882256 and 刺刺妖@火灵天星 55762976 attacks with it for the OTK
