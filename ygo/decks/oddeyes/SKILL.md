- **Core Mechanic: Pendulum Scale and Double Battle Damage**

- Set a low scale 1 card and a high scale 8 card in the pendulum zones, then pendulum summon every Level 2-7 monster from hand in one action, scale difference plus one monsters at a time
- 四天之龙 异色眼灵摆龙 75787708 pendulum summoned gains 300 ATK per scale difference while both pendulum zones are occupied and doubles its battle damage, scale 1-8 makes it a 4600 ATK double-damage beater
- 异色眼灵摆龙 16178681 doubles battle damage against monsters, its scale effect reduces your own battle damage from pendulum battles, and its end phase scale effect destroys itself to search any 1500 or lower ATK pendulum monster
- 四天之龙 异色眼灵摆龙 75787708 scale effect destroys itself to search any 1500 or lower ATK pendulum monster, but unless you pendulum summon this turn you cannot activate field monster effects, sequence the search before or right after the pendulum summon
- Pendulum monsters destroyed from field or pendulum zone go face-up to the extra deck instead of the grave, every destroyed scale is a future pendulum summon target and the fuel for 异色眼风雷龙 53262004 negate and 异色眼绝零龙 16691074 recursion
- Fusion win: 异色眼风雷龙 53262004 fuses from any 异色眼 monster plus any pendulum monster, bounces one attack position monster on summon, and its quick effect negates and destroys a monster, spell, or trap activation by returning one face-up pendulum from your extra deck to the deck
- Xyz win: 异色眼绝零龙 16691074 is a generic Rank 7 from two Level 7 monsters, detach one material to negate an attack and special summon an 异色眼 from hand or grave, and when an Xyz-summoned 绝零龙 is sent to the grave it special summons an 异色眼 monster from the extra deck
- Synchro win: 异色眼陨火龙 80696379 special summons one pendulum zone card on summon and locks the opponent out of monster effect activations during the battle phase
- Supreme King win: 霸王龙 扎克 13331639 fuses one Dragon fusion, synchro, Xyz, and pendulum monster each, destroys all opponent cards on summon, is untargetable and indestructible by effects, special summons a 霸王眷龙 from deck or extra on battle destroy, and moves to the pendulum zone scale 1 when destroyed, where the opponent cannot activate fusion, synchro, or Xyz monster effects

- **Extender: 超天新龙 异色眼革命龙**

- **Extender: 异色眼幻灵龙**

- **Common Mistakes**

- 四天之龙 异色眼灵摆龙 75787708 scale search and 轨迹之魔术师 22125101 search both lock your monster and pendulum zone effects until you pendulum summon, always pendulum summon right after either search
- 娱乐伙伴 骷髅杂技小丑 40318957 in the pendulum zone restricts your pendulum summons to 娱乐伙伴, 魔术师 pendulum, and 异色眼 monsters only, no stray non-archetype monsters
- Pendulum monsters destroyed from field or pendulum zone go face-up to the extra deck, do not send them to the grave with cost effects and lose the 异色眼风雷龙 53262004 negate fuel and the next turn pendulum summon targets
- 异色眼风雷龙 53262004 negate requires one face-up pendulum in your extra deck as cost, keep destroyed scales available instead of banishing or shuffling them away
- 异色眼绝零龙 16691074 second effect only triggers when an Xyz-summoned copy is sent to the grave, and it cannot summon 异色眼风雷龙 53262004 because 风雷龙 is fusion-only, use the 星霜之魔术师-宙读之魔术士 48171151 quick fusion or 异色眼融合 48144509 for the fusion
- 霸王眷龙 凶饿毒 43387895 must be fusion summoned or contact summoned by releasing two Dark pendulum monsters on your field, and its copy effect replaces its own first effect, pick the copied monster effect deliberately and remember pierce applies to all your monsters
- 娱乐伙伴 异色眼管家 56677752 self-revival destroys one of your own 娱乐伙伴 or 异色眼 cards, sequence it to destroy a card you would otherwise lose, not your only scale

- 异色眼融合 48144509 only fuses Dragon fusion monsters and its extra deck material bonus needs an empty field plus two or more opponent monsters
- 霸王眷龙 异色眼 96733134 needs two 霸王眷龙 monsters on field or face-up extra deck to special summon itself from hand, a dead draw in builds without Supreme King density
- Play under 增殖的G 23434538 and the 欢聚友伴 hand traps with a two or three special summon line into 异色眼风雷龙 53262004 instead of the full pendulum swarm
