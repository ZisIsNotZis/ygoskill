- **Core Mechanic: Banish and Respawn**

- Every Maliss main deck monster has a banish-from-hand effect and a respawn-when-banished effect (pay 300 LP, special summon itself from banish zone, then locked to Link-only from Extra Deck that turn)
- 睡鼠 32061192 banishes a Maliss from deck on activation, pumps ATK, and respawns when banished; it is the primary one-card starter
- 白兔 69272449 sets any Maliss trap from deck on summon (from a different name not in GY), and respawns when banished
- 柴郡猫 96676583 banishes a Maliss card from hand to draw 2, then respawns when banished
- 三月兔 20938824 can special summon itself from hand during either Main Phase by banishing another Maliss card from hand/grave; respawns when banished to recycle a banished Maliss to hand
- 码丽丝梦游地下界 68337209 banishes a Maliss card from hand/deck/grave on activation, creates three-link zones, and locks opponent attacks to Maliss Link monsters

- **Trap Engine**

- 码丽丝<代码>MTP-07 94722358 searches any Maliss monster from deck and can also banish a card from field if a Maliss Link is present
- 码丽丝<代码>GWC-06 20726052 special summons a banished or grave Maliss monster and gains LP equal to its ATK
- 码丽丝<代码>TB-11 57111661 special summons a Maliss from deck, or from Extra Deck if the opponent has 3 or more cards
- All three traps can be activated from hand the turn they are set by banishing a face-up Maliss monster as cost
- 白兔 sets a trap on summon, so any Normal Summon White Rabbit becomes a full board

- **Playing Under Hate**

- Against Ash Blossom: 睡鼠 banishes from deck as cost, so Ash on the banish effect does not stop the cost; the respawn trigger is separate and harder to Ash
- Against Nibiru: the 单鼠 combo ends on 暗流体 or 神弓 before the 5th summon; the 场地 combo can pivot to 防火龙 to bounce and avoid
- Against Artifact Lancea 34267821: the combo switches to 备份员@火灵天星 line, using 红棋勒索 68059897 to search 镜中奇像 93453053 and banish cards from grave instead of from deck
- Against Chaos Hunter 97940434: the combo can still banish from hand and field, and the 混沌猎人 cannot stop Maliss respawn triggers
- Against Droll & Lock Bird: the deck draws 4-5 cards per combo, so Droll is devastating; try to end on just 解码语者 when Droll is suspected

- **Common Mistakes**

- Do not forget the Link-only restriction after respawning: all Maliss monsters that respawn from banish lock you to Link monsters from the Extra Deck for the rest of the turn
- 白兔 sets a trap from deck only if a trap with the same name is not in your graveyard; check graveyard before resolving
- 睡鼠 banishes from deck as cost, so if the deck has no Maliss monsters left, the effect cannot activate
- 码丽丝梦游地下界 ③ effect locks all opponent attacks to Maliss Link monsters, but only while you control a Maliss Link monster
- 暗流体 gains counters equal to the number of Cyberse monster types in your grave; fill the grave before summoning it
- The traps can be activated the turn they are set, but only by banishing a face-up Maliss monster; do not set the trap and then remove the only Maliss monster
- 三月兔's hand-trap special summon can be used during either player's Main Phase, making it a surprise extender during the opponent's turn as well
