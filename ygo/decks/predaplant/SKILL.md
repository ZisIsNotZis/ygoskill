# 捕食植物 (Predaplant) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- DARK Plant Fusion deck built on the 捕食指示物 counter (counter 0x1041) and on fusing monsters from BOTH sides of the field

- **Core Mechanic: 捕食指示物 Counter**

- 捕食指示物 counter 0x1041 is placed on faceup monsters; any monster holding one has its level changed to 1, verified in scripts as EFFECT_CHANGE_LEVEL to 1
- Counter sources that hit both fields: 捕食计划 44536921 (all faceup monsters), 捕食生成 72129804 (reveal 捕食 cards from hand, place that many counters on chosen monsters), 黏菖蒲螳螂 17825378 (when used as fusion material, counters up to your monster count)
- Counter sources on the opponent: 捕蝇草棘龙 52792430 on summon, 苍蝇地狱草 96622984 ignition, 犀角龙 69946549 quick effect, 长叶挖耳草络新妇 44994712 grave effect, 霸王暴龙 凶饿毒辉翼龙 5148778 pendulum effect on opponent special summon
- Counter exploiters: 茅膏菜伞蜥 89181134 fuses using opponent counter monsters treated as DARK, 游走鲸 66309175 releases a counter monster to special summon a 捕食植物 from deck, 露松水螅龙 99913726 releases a counter monster to special summon itself, 腺毛草胡蜂 44932065 revives from grave while any counter monster exists, 露松藤三头龙 79864860 gains attack from counter monsters and revives from grave, 奇美拉大花草 25586143 removes monsters with level at or below its own
- Battle payoffs of the level 1 drop: 捕蝇草棘龙 52792430 and 苍蝇地狱草 96622984 destroy battle targets with level at or below their own level, 鱿鱼茅膏菜 69105797 from hand makes a monster attack all counter monsters

- **Core Mechanic: Both-Fields Fusion Engine**

- 超融合 48130397 discards 1 card, fuses using monsters from either field, and no card can respond to its activation
- 捕食霸种融合 8148322 is a quick-play that fuses from both fields using at least 2 of your own monsters, requiring a faceup 捕食植物 on the field
- 四天之龙 凶饿毒融合龙 27118421 is a walking Super Polymerization: when the opponent activates an effect, fuse DARK monsters from both fields including itself into a DARK fusion
- 茅膏菜伞蜥 89181134 gives an ignition fusion that uses your monsters plus the opponent counter-marked monsters as material
- 超越融合 76647978 pays 2000 LP and fuses ONLY your own 2 monsters, then in grave banishes itself to special summon those 2 materials back negated with 0 ATK and DEF, feeding the fusion loop

- **One-Card Combo: 捕食原生 89176044**

- Activate 捕食原生: send 捕食计划 44536921 or 捕食活动 31643613 from deck to grave, add 超融合 48130397 or 简易融合 1845204 from deck to hand
- This locks you to Dragon and Plant special summons for the turn, so plan the whole line before activating
- Step 2: normal summon 蜂兰蝎 35272499, discard 1 monster as cost to special summon 蛇瓶草眼镜蛇 61677004 from deck
- Step 3: 眼镜蛇 adds a 融合 spell to hand, then fuse 蜂兰蝎 and 眼镜蛇 with 简易融合 or 置换融合 into 凤梨食虫谷精游走鲸 66309175, whose fusion summon searches any 捕食 card from deck or grave
- Step 4: 捕食计划 44536921 in grave destroys a card on the field whenever you fusion summon a DARK monster

- **One-Card Combo: 捕食活动 31643613**

- Activate to special summon 1 捕食植物 monster from hand then add 1 捕食 card from deck, but the turn restricts extra deck summons to Fusion monsters only
- If the special summoned monster is 蜂兰蝎 35272499, chain its discard cost into 眼镜蛇 61677004 into a 融合 spell search for the same turn
- If it is 腺毛草胡蜂 44932065, its grave search grabs another 捕食 plant and its grave revive extends the next turn

- **Extender: 蜂兰蝎 35272499 and 眼镜蛇 61677004**

- 蜂兰蝎 on normal or special summon discards 1 monster as cost to special summon any 捕食植物 from deck except itself
- 眼镜蛇 on special summon by a 捕食植物 effect searches any 融合 setcode spell: 超融合 48130397, 简易融合 1845204, 置换融合 74335036, 超越融合 76647978, 捕食霸种融合 8148322, once per duel
- If 眼镜蛇 search is spent or it is banished, 蜂兰蝎 still fetches 长叶挖耳草络新妇 44994712 or 腺毛草胡蜂 44932065 as the extension body
- 长叶挖耳草络新妇 44994712 is a Tuner that searches any 捕食 card on summon, and in grave banishes itself to place a counter or set 融合 24094653 from grave or banished

- **Extender: 血树龙姬 龙血树鬼 79966218**

- Special summons itself from hand by sending a level 4 or lower Plant from deck to grave, and copies that monster's level
- Its grave effect banishes itself and 1 Plant to special summon a Dragon from hand or grave
- Both effects lock you to Dragon and Plant special summons for the turn; dumping 长叶挖耳草络新妇 44994712 makes it a level 1 Tuner for the synchro line
- Synchro route: 蜂兰蝎 35272499 or 眼镜蛇 61677004 level 3 plus a level 1 Tuner into 羊角麻蝇王 66787942, then up into 黑蔷薇龙 73580471, 黑蔷薇破灭龙 6560411, 幻透翼同调龙 82044279, or 水晶翼同调龙 50954680
- 羊角麻蝇王 66787942 special summons a 捕食植物 or 凶饿毒 monster from grave or extra on synchro summon, and in grave can remove 融合 24094653 to fusion summon when the opponent summons

- **Extender: 超越融合 76647978 Loop**

- Fuse your 2 monsters into 凤梨食虫谷精游走鲸 66309175 with 超越融合, search 捕食活动 31643613 or 捕食计划 44536921 off the fusion summon
- Later banish 超越融合 from grave to bring the 2 materials back negated with 0 ATK and DEF, then re-use them as 捕食惑星 51858200 grave fusion material, 置换融合 74335036 material, or 游走鲸 66309175 release fodder to special summon from deck
- 捕食惑星 51858200 in grave fusion summons using only 捕食植物 monsters from hand and field, and on field searches a 捕食 card whenever a counter monster leaves the field
- 凶饿毒捕食融合龙 39915560 negates a chain by releasing a counter monster, and when destroyed by the opponent revives a DARK monster from grave

- **End Field**

- Modern line: 凤梨食虫谷精游走鲸 66309175 with searched 捕食 card, 犀角龙 69946549 placing a counter and negating that monster's activated effects, 四天之龙 凶饿毒融合龙 27118421 zeroing one monster and holding a quick fusion, 凶饿毒捕食融合龙 39915560 as a second negate
- Backrow and grave resources: grave 捕食原生 89176044 and 捕食惑星 51858200 and 超越融合 76647978, set 捕食花盆 88069597 that revives a level 4 or lower 捕食植物 negated each turn
- Old pendulum variant: 奇美拉大花草 25586143 removal plus 露松藤三头龙 79864860 negating opponent extra deck summons, 强欲毒融合龙 51570882 wiping the field when destroyed, 凶饿毒融合龙 41209827 copying effects and gaining attack on fusion summon

- **Halt Points**

- 灰流丽 14558127 stops 捕食原生 89176044 activation, 长叶挖耳草络新妇 44994712 search, and 蜂兰蝎 35272499 special summon, the last one killing the whole engine because the discard is a cost
- 增殖的G 23434538 punishes the 3 plus special summon lines, play under it by stopping after the first fusion or only resolving 捕食活动 31643613
- 尼比鲁 27204311 lands after the 5th summon, keep a fusion spell set before committing the extra bodies
- 墓穴的指名者 24224830 and 抹杀之指名者 65681983 remove 眼镜蛇 61677004 and 络新妇 44994712 from grave, cutting recursion
- 无限泡影 10045474 on 蜂兰蝎 35272499 or 捕食原生 89176044 leaves the turn with no extension
- Lock checkpoints: 捕食原生 89176044 forbids non Dragon or Plant summons and 捕食活动 31643613 forbids non Fusion extra deck summons, activate them last in the sequence

- **Mirror Match: 捕食植物 vs 捕食植物**

- Every monster is DARK, so 超融合 48130397 and 四天之龙 27118421 steal the opponent fusion board, keep 迦楼罗 11765832 and 凶饿毒融合龙 41209827 in extra as targets
- Counters are shared leverage: the opponent counter monsters can be released by 游走鲸 66309175 and 露松水螅龙 99913726 and fused by 茅膏菜伞蜥 89181134, so do not place counters on the opponent side freely
- The 捕食计划 44536921 and 捕食生成 72129804 counter placement hits all faceup monsters, in the mirror it hands the opponent fusion material, hold them until you can spend the counters the same turn
- Whoever resolves 捕食原生 89176044 into 超融合 48130397 first wins the fusion race, use 墓穴的指名者 24224830 on the opponent 眼镜蛇 61677004 or 络新妇 44994712

- **Common Mistakes**

- 超越融合 76647978 fuses only your own 2 monsters, do not try to use it on the opponent field like 超融合 48130397
- 简易融合 1845204 monster cannot attack and is destroyed in the end phase, use it as fusion material or release fodder before then
- 眼镜蛇 61677004 search is once per duel and only fetches 融合 setcode spells, never use it to grab 捕食原生 89176044
- 蜂兰蝎 35272499 discards a monster as cost and cannot special summon itself, when 眼镜蛇 is unavailable prefer 络新妇 44994712 or 腺毛草胡蜂 44932065
- Activate 捕食原生 89176044 only when the turn stays Dragon and Plant, it blocks generic links like S：P小夜骑士 29301450 and 灾厄之星 提·丰 93039339
- Activate 捕食活动 31643613 only before a Fusion line, it blocks the 羊角麻蝇王 66787942 synchro route for the rest of the turn
- 奇美拉大花草 25586143 removal checks level, the level 1 counter drop lets it remove nearly any monster, and its 融合 spell search happens from grave in the standby phase
- 捕食惑星 51858200 grave fusion allows only 捕食植物 monsters as material, do not fuse generic DARK monsters with it
- 凤梨食虫谷精游走鲸 66309175 releases a monster with a counter or one you control, in the mirror prefer releasing the opponent counter monster
- Do not stack 捕食生成 72129804 counters on your own monsters unless 茅膏菜伞蜥 89181134 or 游走鲸 66309175 consumes them in the same turn
- 瓶子草蚁 53819028 special summons from hand only on a direct attack, do not hold it as a field body, and its search triggers when destroyed by battle or by the opponent effect from the field
