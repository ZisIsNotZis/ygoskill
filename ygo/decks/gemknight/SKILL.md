# 宝石骑士 (Gem-Knight) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- Fusion deck of EARTH monsters, mostly Rock family, carrying both the 宝石 (Gem, setcode 0x47) and 宝石骑士 (Gem-Knight, setcode 0x1047) archetypes
- Core engine: fuse from the DECK with 明亮融合 007394770, recycle the normal monsters 小琉 99645428 / 小璃 81846636, then chain into link 幽晶原核 24484270 and the fusion bosses 辉钻 39512984 / 亮钻 19355597
- No field spell exists in this card pool (宝石骑士之城 is not in cards.cdb), 明亮融合 007394770 is a continuous spell that plays the field-spell role and holds the board together
- Win condition is OTK pressure: 琉璃 47611119 burn, 黄玉 49597193 double attack, 辉钻 39512984 pumped attack, not a floodgate end board
- Deck basis read from near-pure lists: 190914宝石骑士, 191012宝石骑士, 191123宝石骑士, 220423宝石骑士
- Build quirks: 宝石骑士融合 01264319 sits at 3 while 明亮融合 007394770 at 1 to 2, some lists splash 捕食植物 蜂兰蝎 35272499 + 蛇瓶草眼镜蛇 61677004 to search the fusion spell, older lists run the 琉璃 burn FTK with 名推理 58577036 and 手札抹杀 72892473

- **Core Mechanic: Gem Fusion Loop**

- 明亮融合 007394770: continuous spell, on activation fusion summons one 宝石骑士 fusion using monsters from the DECK as material, that fusion has 0 attack and defense and is destroyed if the spell leaves the field, once per turn a spell discard restores original stats until the opponent end phase
- 宝石骑士融合 01264319: fusion from hand and field, grave effect banishes one 宝石骑士 monster to add itself back, so every fusion recycles the spell for another ladder step
- 小璃 81846636: when sent to the graveyard by an effect, adds one normal monster from the grave to hand, the 明亮融合 material dump triggers it and returns 小琉 99645428
- 黑曜 19163116: when sent from the HAND to the grave, special summons a level 4 or lower normal monster from the grave, discard it to revive 小琉 99645428 as link or fusion fodder
- 琉璃 47611119: fusion of 小琉 99645428 plus one 宝石骑士 monster, once per turn sends one 宝石骑士 monster from deck or extra deck to the grave and burns 500 times the number of special summoned monsters on both fields, the mill also stocks the grave for 辉钻 39512984 copy targets
- 拿非琉 51831560: when sent from hand or deck to the grave discards one card and special summons itself, on summon searches a 宝石骑士 card, and once per turn performs an extra normal summon of a 宝石 monster, but its search halves effect damage to the opponent during that main phase
- 幽晶原核 24484270: link 2 of 宝石 monsters, on link summon searches a 宝石骑士 card, pay 1000 life to shuffle grave and banished 宝石 materials into the deck and fusion summon a 宝石骑士 fusion that cannot attack directly that turn
- 虚梦狱原核 88225269: from hand sends a 宝石骑士 normal or 宝石骑士融合 01264319 from deck to grave as cost and special summons itself in defense, grave quick effect while a 宝石骑士 fusion is on field banishes itself plus two 宝石骑士 cards from grave to negate an opponent effect and gives all 宝石骑士 monsters plus 1000 attack

- **One-Card Combo: 明亮融合**

- Starter: 明亮融合 007394770 in hand, no other cards needed
- Step 1: activate it and fusion summon 琉璃 47611119 using 小琉 99645428 and 小璃 81846636 from the deck, both hit the grave as materials
- Step 2: 小璃 81846636 trigger adds 小琉 99645428 from grave back to hand
- Step 3: 琉璃 47611119 effect sends 拿非琉 51831560 from deck to grave and burns 500 times the special summoned monster count
- Step 4: 拿非琉 51831560 chain effect discards one card and special summons itself, resolve the burn before its search effect to avoid the damage halving
- Step 5: 拿非琉 51831560 summon effect searches 宝石骑士融合 01264319, then its extra normal summon brings 小琉 99645428 from hand
- Step 6: link 琉璃 47611119 plus 拿非琉 51831560 into 幽晶原核 24484270, its link summon searches 虚梦狱原核 88225269 or a fusion spell
- Step 7: 幽晶原核 24484270 pays 1000 life, shuffles the grave 宝石 materials into the deck and fusion summons 辉钻 39512984 or 亮钻 19355597
- Halt point: 灰流丽 14558127 on the 明亮融合 007394770 activation stops the whole line, 灰流丽 14558127 on 琉璃 47611119 or 拿非琉 51831560 stops extension

- **End Field**

- 幽晶原核 24484270 link plus 辉钻 39512984 with attack boosted by 100 per 宝石 monster in grave, able to banish a level 7 or lower 宝石骑士 fusion from grave and copy its name and effect for the turn
- Copy targets matter: 黄玉 49597193 for double attack, 蓝玉 13108445 for a bounce on leaving the field, 橙黄晶 67985943 for the battle lock that stops opponent effects during the damage step
- Ladder option: 亮钻 19355597 sends a face-up 宝石骑士 monster on field to the grave and special summons any 宝石骑士 fusion from the extra deck ignoring summon conditions, one per turn, so it converts 琉璃 47611119 or 辉钻 39512984 into 辉钻-色散 25342956
- 辉钻-色散 25342956: quick effect in a main phase sends itself to the grave and special summons up to three non-Rock 宝石 monsters from extra deck or grave ignoring conditions with one copy per name, after it resolves only fusion summons are allowed from the extra deck that turn, grave effect revives it when a 宝石骑士 fusion is destroyed by battle
- 虚梦狱原核 88225269 in the grave stands ready as a one-shot omni negate, and 玫钻 55610199 if fused protects 宝石骑士 monsters from effect destruction on the opponent turn
- 明亮融合 007394770 stays on the field, discard a spell before battle to give the fused monster back its real attack

- **Halt Points**

- 灰流丽 14558127 on 明亮融合 007394770 or 宝石骑士融合 01264319 kills the fusion-from-deck engine
- 墓穴的指名者 24224830 and 抹杀之指名者 65681983 on 小璃 81846636, 拿非琉 51831560 or 黑曜 19163116 cut the recursion and the extra summons
- 增殖的G 23434538 is dangerous because every combo step special summons, the full ladder hands over many draws
- Grave dependence is total, any mass banishment such as 次元吸引者 91800273 or 次元的裂缝 81674782 stops 小璃 81846636 recycling, 黑曜 19163116 revival, 辉钻 39512984 attack gain and the 虚梦狱原核 88225269 negate
- 明亮融合 007394770 destruction takes its fusion with it, play removal protection before activating it or keep a spare fusion spell
- 幽晶原核 24484270 costs 1000 life per use and 琉璃 47611119 burn is effect damage that gets halved after 拿非琉 51831560 search or 色散 24220368 mode two resolves
- Self locks constrain sequencing: 吸光融合 71422989 restricts special summons to 宝石骑士 for the turn, 分散融合 40597694 and 石英 35622739 restrict extra deck summons to 宝石骑士, so run generic plays first
- 亮钻 19355597 and 琉璃 47611119 each allow only one special summon per turn

- **Common Mistakes**

- Forgetting the 明亮融合 007394770 fusion is 0 attack until a spell is discarded, always restore stats before the battle phase
- Activating 明亮融合 007394770 when 小琉 99645428 or 小璃 81846636 are no longer in the deck, the deck materials come from the deck so 粗人预料 00911883 and 救援兔 85138716 must be sequenced after the fusion spell
- Using 琉璃 47611119 burn or 分散融合 40597694 fusions after 拿非琉 51831560 search or 色散 24220368 mode two, the effect damage is halved, burn first then search
- 吸光融合 71422989 banishes its materials so 小璃 81846636 and 黑曜 19163116 grave triggers do not fire, prefer it for fusions that do not need those triggers
- 分散融合 40597694 only fuses non-Rock 宝石骑士 targets, it cannot make 琉璃 47611119, 仿钻 008692301, 亮钻 19355597 or 辉钻 39512984
- 琉璃 47611119 specifically needs 小琉 99645428 as material, keep at least one 小琉 99645428 available and never banish both without a plan
- 黑曜 19163116 triggers only from the hand, discarding it as 拿非琉 51831560 cost only works when a level 4 or lower normal monster is already in the grave
- 虚梦狱原核 88225269 negate banishes itself, it is a one-shot per copy, do not waste it on minor effects when 亮钻 19355597 or 辉钻-色散 25342956 are incoming
- 石英 35622739 requires the opponent to control a monster, it is a going second starter not a first turn play
- 亮钻 19355597 needs a face-up 宝石骑士 monster on the field to feed its ladder effect, keep 琉璃 47611119 alive instead of using it as link material first
