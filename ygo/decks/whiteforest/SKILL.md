# 白森林 (White Forest) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- LIGHT Spellcaster tuner engine from the 罪宝/Diabellstar lore: discard spells and traps to special summon, then chain-synchro climb, because every 白森林 synchro is itself a Tuner Synchro
- Main deck monsters: 莉泽特 61980241, 阿斯忒瑞亚 25592142, 濡血雅 24779554, 森厄维 98385955, 圣徒 莉泽特 38648860, all Level 2 or 4 LIGHT Spellcaster or Illusion
- Synchro ladder: 幻妖 5800323 (Level 4 Tuner Synchro), 魔狼 森厄狼 41924516 and 魔性 濡血蝠 77313225 (Level 6 Tuner Synchro), 妖魔 迪亚贝尔 14307929 (Level 8, "Diabell, Queen of the White Forest")
- Field spell 白森林的魔女 4398189 searches a 白森林 monster, gives battle protection, and turns a 白森林 monster into a tuner for a turn
- Spells and traps that re-set themselves from the GY when sent as cost: 灾祸 62995268, 传说 99289828, 罪宝 3496543, 禁止入内 35778533, 恶魔 93723936, 罪宝的低语 62173132
- 罪宝/迪亚贝尔 splash: 白魔女 迪亚贝尔泽 60145298, 断罪之迪亚贝尔斯塔尔 23151193, 恶魔迪亚贝尔的诅咒 64998567, 恶魔迪亚贝尔的友爱 78293584
- Finish bosses: 鲜花女男爵 84815190, 红龙 63436931, 蛇眼断罪龙 79415624, 宇宙耀变龙 21123811, with 同调鸣动 88901994 as the 红龙 support
- Naming note: the prompt's 露雪/阿斯特 are this server's 濡血雅 24779554 (TCG Rucia) and 阿斯忒瑞亚 25592142 (TCG Astellar); no 坎夫兰特 or 圣祭司 card exists in this card pool, the closest are 妖魔 迪亚贝尔 14307929 (the Queen) and 圣徒 莉泽特 38648860

- **Core Mechanic: pay S/T, summon, synchro-climb**

- 莉泽特 61980241 ①: from hand, send 1 S/T from hand/field to GY as cost, special summon itself, then add any other 白森林 monster from deck; ② in GY it returns to hand during the opponent's turn whenever a face-up LIGHT Spellcaster Tuner is special summoned on your field
- 阿斯忒瑞亚 25592142 ①: on field, same S/T cost, special summon 1 LIGHT Spellcaster Tuner from deck (濡血雅 or 森厄维); ② in GY it revives itself whenever a S/T is sent to your GY as cost for a monster effect
- 濡血雅 24779554 and 森厄维 98385955 are Level 4 tuners; 莉泽特 and 阿斯忒瑞亚 are Level 2 non-tuners, and 魔女 4398189 ③ turns any 白森林 monster into a tuner for the turn
- The discarded 白森林 S/T re-set themselves from GY, so every cost is recycled fuel and the engine never runs out of fodder
- 幻妖 5800323: ① on summon place 2 白森林 monsters from GY as face-up Continuous Spells in the S/T zones; ② during the opponent's Main Phase it can synchro summon using itself — the deck's main turn-2 interrupt
- 妖魔 迪亚贝尔 14307929: ① when synchro summoned using a Synchro Tuner material, add 1 S/T from GY; ② when the opponent activates an effect, send 1 S/T from hand/field to GY to special summon 1 Level 7 or lower Synchro Tuner from Extra/GY/banished — the loop engine that recycles 幻妖 every opponent turn

- **One-Card Combo: 莉泽特 61980241**

- Step 1: 莉泽特 ①, cost send 1 S/T (prefer 传说/罪宝/灾祸/恶魔, they re-set from GY) → summon itself, search 阿斯忒瑞亚 25592142
- Step 2: 阿斯忒瑞亚 ①, cost send 1 S/T → summon 濡血雅 24779554 (Level 4 tuner) from deck
- Step 3: synchro 濡血雅 (4) + 莉泽特 (2) = 幻妖 5800323 (Level 4 Tuner Synchro); skip 幻妖 ① here — placing 濡血雅 into a S/T zone removes it from the GY and kills the recycle
- Step 4: synchro 幻妖 (4, tuner) + 阿斯忒瑞亚 (2) = 魔性 濡血蝠 77313225 (Level 6 Tuner Synchro)
- Step 5: 魔性 濡血蝠 ①, cost send 1 S/T → add 1 白森林 card or LIGHT Spellcaster from deck (search 传说 99289828 or the trap 灾祸 62995268); the cost also triggers 阿斯忒瑞亚 ② to summon itself back
- Step 6: 濡血雅 ③ from GY (quick): return 幻妖 (in GY) to the Extra Deck, then special summon 濡血雅 (Level 4 tuner, negated)
- Step 7: synchro 濡血雅 (4, tuner) + 阿斯忒瑞亚 (2) = 魔狼 森厄狼 41924516 (Level 6 Tuner Synchro)

- **End Field**

- One-card: 魔性 濡血蝠 77313225 + 魔狼 森厄狼 41924516 — both are Spellcaster synchros so each gives the other +500 ATK, 魔性 濡血蝠 ③ makes both immune to effect destruction, 魔狼 森厄狼 ① flips the opponent's field face-down on summon and grants double piercing; plus 白森林的魔女 4398189 field and a set 灾祸 62995268
- Recursion for next turn: 莉泽特 61980241 ②, 濡血雅 24779554 ③, 森厄维 98385955 ② (recycle a 白森林 synchro to summon themselves), 幻妖 5800323 back in the Extra Deck for the 妖魔 迪亚贝尔 loop, 阿斯忒瑞亚 25592142 reviving on the next S/T cost
- Two-card upgrade: add 妖魔 迪亚贝尔 14307929 (Queen), made as 魔狼 森厄狼 (6, tuner) + 阿斯忒瑞亚 (2) or 幻妖 (4, tuner) + two Level 2; then on the opponent's turn ② sends 1 S/T → summon 幻妖 → 幻妖 ② synchros 4+8=12 into 红龙 63436931 or 蛇眼断罪龙 79415624 during their Main Phase
- 王 finish: 红龙 63436931 ① searches 同调鸣动 88901994 (revives 幻妖, protects 红龙); 红龙 ② targets any face-up Level 7+ synchro on the field and summons a same-level Dragon Synchro — with a Level 12 蛇眼断罪龙 79415624 on field, or an opponent's Level 12, it becomes 宇宙耀变龙 21123811
- 鲜花女男爵 84815190 (omni-negate and destroy) needs a second card: 方程式同调士 50091196 (2, tuner) + 妖魔 迪亚贝尔 (8) = 10, or 魔女 4398189 ③ turning a Level 2 into a tuner to pair with an 8
- Honesty note: 宇宙耀变龙 cannot be hard-synchroed in this build (no Level 2/4/6 non-tuner Synchros to reach 12 with a Tuner Synchro plus 2 non-tuners); it only enters via 红龙 ②

- 森厄维 98385955: ① on normal/special summon add any 白森林 spell/trap from deck; ② GY quick: same recycle trick as 濡血雅 ③
- 圣徒 莉泽特 38648860: ① reveal it in hand to summon any 白森林 or 蓟花 monster from hand; ② on field, fusion summon a 蓟花 Fusion from hand/field; ③ when used as synchro material, add any 罪宝 card from deck (断罪之迪亚贝尔斯塔尔 23151193, 罪宝的低语 62173132, 恶魔迪亚贝尔的诅咒 64998567)
- 白魔女 迪亚贝尔泽 60145298: ① same S/T cost to summon itself from hand; ② on summon mill 1 罪宝 or 迪亚贝尔 card from deck, milling 恶魔迪亚贝尔的诅咒 64998567 triggers its ② to add 恶魔迪亚贝尔的友爱 78293584; ③ with 2+ 迪亚贝尔 monsters your monsters cannot be destroyed by effects

- 白森林的罪宝 3496543: ① while you control an Illusion/Spellcaster/Fiend, summon one from hand or fusion summon any Fusion from hand/field (米德拉什 94977269, 七贤巨鲲魔 50907446, 迦楼罗 11765832, 蛇眼原罪龙 58071334); ② re-sets itself from GY

- **Halt Points**

- 灰流丽 14558127 on 莉泽特 61980241 ① (cost is paid but the summon and search both fail), on 阿斯忒瑞亚 25592142 ①, on 传说 99289828, and on 魔女 4398189's search
- 效果遮蒙者 97268402 or 无限泡影 10045474 on 阿斯忒瑞亚 ① (stops the deck tuner) or on 幻妖 5800323 (stops its quick-synchro ②)
- 墓穴的指名者 24224830 on 濡血雅 24779554 or 幻妖 5800323 in GY kills the recycle loop, on 莉泽特 61980241 kills the add-back
- 增殖的G 23434538: the combo special summons 5+ times; under G stop after 魔狼 森厄狼 41924516 and pass on the field plus backrow

- 原始生命态 尼比鲁: the deck easily exceeds 5 summons; hold 幻妖 5800323 in the Extra Deck and 濡血雅 24779554 in GY so the board can rebuild after the token clears the field

- **Mirror Match**

- The first player to resolve 妖魔 迪亚贝尔 14307929 ② wins the opponent-turn synchro war, so bait with 灾祸 62995268 or 恶魔 93723936 before committing it
- 魔狼 森厄狼 41924516's field flip is the mirror breaker: face-down synchros cannot be targeted by the 濡血雅 ③/森厄维 ② recycle, by 红龙 ②, or by 妖魔 迪亚贝尔 ① — flip their ladder before they climb again
- 白森林的魔女 4398189 ② battle protection applies once per 白森林 monster per turn — count it before attacking
- Whoever keeps 幻妖 5800323 accessible (Extra Deck, not banished) controls the grind game

- **Common Mistakes**

- Do not activate 幻妖 5800323 ① when 濡血雅 24779554 is still needed in the GY — placing it as a Continuous Spell moves it out of the GY and breaks the recycle line
- Do not search with 白森林的魔女 4398189 ① on a turn you plan the Shaddoll fusions: the search forbids DARK monsters from the Extra Deck for the rest of the turn (米德拉什 94977269 and 七贤巨鲲魔 50907446 are both DARK)
- 灾祸 62995268 is a Trap: it cannot activate the turn it re-sets itself from GY — set it one turn ahead or hold it in hand
- 濡血雅 ③ and 森厄维 ② summon themselves with effects NEGATED — the revived tuner is synchro material only, do not expect its search or draw
- 妖魔 迪亚贝尔 14307929 ② only triggers on the opponent's activation, not your own chain, and the follow-up search can be Ashed — sequence accordingly
- 阿斯忒瑞亚 25592142 ② revives only when a S/T is sent to your GY as COST of a monster effect — not for spell activations or synchro material sends
- 断罪之迪亚贝尔斯塔尔 23151193 ② needs a second 迪亚贝尔 monster on field for the 幻妖 summon — keep 白魔女 迪亚贝尔泽 60145298 or 妖魔 迪亚贝尔 alive
- 红龙 63436931 ② returns itself to the Extra Deck and needs a face-up Level 7+ synchro on the field as target; without a Level 12 (蛇眼断罪龙 79415624 or an opponent's) it cannot become 宇宙耀变龙 21123811
- 同调鸣动 88901994 locks you to Synchro-only Extra Deck summons for the rest of the turn — resolve the fusion lines first, then revive 幻妖 and climb
- Keep S/T fodder available: 莉泽特, 阿斯忒瑞亚 and 魔性 濡血蝠 each consume 1 S/T as cost, so open with 2+ spells/traps in hand or be ready to discard the field spell 4398189 as a cost (it does not re-set itself from GY, unlike 传说/罪宝/灾祸)
