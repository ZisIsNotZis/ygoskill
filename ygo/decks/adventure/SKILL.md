# 勇者 (Adventure) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- The 勇者 (Adventure/Brave) engine is a splash package found in hundreds of decks, one card 阿拉弥赛亚之仪 3285551 makes a free 2000/2000 token plus the continuous spell 命运之旅路 39568067 from deck
- Classic package takes 5-8 main deck slots: 2-3 阿拉弥赛亚之仪 3285551, 1-2 圣殿的水遣 30680659, 1 命运之旅路 39568067, 1 流离的狮鹫骑手 2563463, 1 骑龙 驮龙 38745520
- Newer 2024 Aramatya package runs 3 禁咒 阿拉马提亚 34690953, 3 忒塞亚圣灵器 54092240, 3 外法之骑士 42198835, 3 遗迹的魔矿战士 66078354, plus 1 雷霆放电 27623932 and 1 复活气息 64018647
- 勇者衍生物 3285552 is a Level 4 EARTH Fairy 2000/2000 non-effect token created directly by the spell scripts, never a main deck card; 34690954 is the alias token made by 禁咒 阿拉马提亚

- **Core Mechanic: One Discard, Token Plus Two Disruptions**

- 阿拉弥赛亚之仪 3285551 (Normal Spell, once per turn): if you control no 勇者衍生物, Special Summon 1 token 3285552, then optionally place 1 命运之旅路 39568067 from deck face-up
- The price: the turn 阿拉弥赛亚之仪 is activated you cannot activate the field effects of monsters that were not Special Summoned, so the engine runs last in the turn
- 命运之旅路 39568067 (Continuous Spell): effect one searches any monster whose text lists 勇者衍生物 then discards 1 card, effect two on any monster summon adds an Equip Spell that lists the token and equips it to a token you control, effect three stops an equipped monster being destroyed by battle once per turn
- 流离的狮鹫骑手 2563463 is the negate: quick effect Special Summons itself from hand in either Main Phase while you control no monsters or a token, then once per turn while a token is on your field it shuffles itself into the deck to negate and destroy any effect activation
- 骑龙 驮龙 38745520 (Equip Spell) is the disruption: while equipped to a non-effect monster like the token it returns 1 opponent card to hand once per turn, and re-equips itself from the graveyard to a token
- Net trade: 1 card from hand plus 1 discard produces token plus 命运之旅路 plus 狮鹫骑手 plus 驮龙, four cards worth of field from the deck

- **One-Card Combo: 阿拉弥赛亚之仪**

- Starter: 阿拉弥赛亚之仪 3285551 in hand, no other cards needed
- Step 1: activate 阿拉弥赛亚之仪, Special Summon 勇者衍生物 3285552, place 命运之旅路 39568067 face-up from deck
- Step 2: activate 命运之旅路 effect one, add 流离的狮鹫骑手 2563463 from deck to hand, then discard 1 card
- Step 3: activate 流离的狮鹫骑手 effect one from hand in your Main Phase, Special Summon it because the token is on field
- Step 4: 命运之旅路 effect two triggers on that Special Summon, add 骑龙 驮龙 38745520 from deck and equip it to the token
- Step 5: end with the equipped token plus 流离的狮鹫骑手 plus 命运之旅路 face-up

- **End Field One-Card**

- 勇者衍生物 3285552 at 2000/2000 equipped with 骑龙 驮龙 38745520: bounces one opponent card per turn and survives battle destruction once per turn through 命运之旅路 39568067
- 流离的狮鹫骑手 2563463 at 2000/2800: one negate-and-destroy per turn on any card or effect, then shuffles back into the deck
- 命运之旅路 39568067 face-up continuous spell ready to search again next turn
- Halt point: negating the 阿拉弥赛亚之仪 activation or destroying 命运之旅路 before effect one leaves only a naked token

- **Extender: 圣殿的水遣 30680659**

- Level 3 Spellcaster that Special Summons itself from hand while a token is on field
- Effect two banishes itself from hand or graveyard to add 阿拉弥赛亚之仪 3285551 back, acting as extra copies of the starter and as recovery
- Effect three places a Field Spell that lists the token from deck, 暗黑神殿 扎拉拉姆 64230128 or 迷幻花之森 91228233

- **Extender: 禁咒 阿拉马提亚 34690953**

- Continuous Spell whose effect one Special Summons a token to your or the opponent's field in Main Phase then discards 1 card
- The turn effect one is used you can only Special Summon the token or monsters listing it, so no Extra Deck summons after it
- Effect two Special Summons a token to either field when your monster is destroyed by battle or effect
- The either-field choice gives the opponent a token, only useful when your zone is full or the plan demands it

- **Extender: 2024 Aramatya Support**

- 忒塞亚圣灵器 54092240: Special Summons itself with a token on field, then shows a hand card listing the token to add any non-Normal Spell that lists the token, locking your Special Summons for the turn
- 遗迹的魔矿战士 66078354: Special Summons itself with a token on field, then at the end of a battle involving your token-listing monster sets a token-listing Trap from deck
- 雷霆放电 27623932: with a token on field, destroys all opponent monsters whose ATK is at or below your equipped monster, then re-equips a token-listing Equip Spell from hand or graveyard
- 复活气息 64018647: with a token on field, revives up to two monsters from hand or graveyard at most one of each name, optionally equipping a token-listing Equip Spell, revived monsters leave the field banished
- 外法之骑士 42198835: Special Summons itself in either Main Phase, then gives control of itself to the opponent to return up to two opponent cards to hand, only while a token is on your field
- Alternate Equip Spells searchable by 命运之旅路 39568067: 光之圣剑 丹内尔 65952776 raises equipped monster ATK per token-listing monster you control, 星空蝶 92341815 lowers opponent monster ATK, both re-equip to a token from the graveyard

- **Halt Points**

- Ash Blossom on 阿拉弥赛亚之仪 3285551 leaves an empty field, the whole engine is gone for the turn
- Negating 命运之旅路 39568067 effect one stops the 流离的狮鹫骑手 2563463 search but the token stays, so the Equip trigger on your next summon still works
- Destroying 命运之旅路 after it is placed keeps the token but costs the search and the battle protection
- 命运之旅路 effect two fires on any monster summon including the opponent's, so an unplanned summon can consume it
- The 流离的狮鹫骑手 negate needs a face-up token at activation, so removing the token first, such as Tributing it for a Kaiju or bouncing it, blanks the negate

- **Mirror Match: 勇者 vs 勇者**

- The negates trade one for one, whoever activates first loses their 流离的狮鹫骑手 2563463 to the opponent's negate, so bait with 命运之旅路 39568067 effect one or the Equip trigger first
- 骑龙 驮龙 38745520 bounce targets the opponent's face-up 命运之旅路 or their equipped token to break the line
- 外法之骑士 42198835 control swap bounces two opponent cards but hands over a 2000/2000 body the opponent then uses to enable their own token-listing monsters
- Never place a token on the opponent's field with 禁咒 阿拉马提亚 34690953 in the mirror, it lets their 圣殿的水遣 30680659 and 忒塞亚圣灵器 54092240 Special Summon themselves for free

- **Common Mistakes**

- Activating 阿拉弥赛亚之仪 3285551 before Normal Summon plays locks the on-field effects of non-Special Summoned monsters for the turn, run the engine last in Main Phase 1
- Using 禁咒 阿拉马提亚 34690953 before Extra Deck plays locks out all non-token Special Summons for the turn
- Forgetting 命运之旅路 39568067 effect one discards a card and pitching a card the deck needs instead of a dead draw
- Equipping 骑龙 驮龙 38745520 to an effect monster makes its bounce effect unusable, it only works on the non-effect token
- Removing the token before activating the 流离的狮鹫骑手 2563463 negate, the negate needs the token face-up at activation
- Expecting two negates in one turn, the 流离的狮鹫骑手 negate has its own once-per-turn count separate from its Special Summon
- Putting 勇者衍生物 3285552 in the main deck, the scripts create the token directly and no deck copy exists
- Leaving the token unguarded, it is a 2000/2000 non-effect monster with no self-protection and the negate and bounce package dies with it
