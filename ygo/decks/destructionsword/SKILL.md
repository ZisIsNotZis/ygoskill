# 破坏之剑士 (Destruction Sword) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- Anti-Dragon control: the deck's floodgate (龙破坏的剑士-破坏之剑士 86240887) only locks Dragon monsters, so the whole plan is to make the opponent's board Dragon, then lock it
- Monster core: 破坏之剑士 78193831 (2600 ATK, +500 per opponent field/grave Dragon; alternate artworks 78193832/3/4 are the same card), 使破坏剑的高手-破坏之剑士 03428069 (name is 破坏之剑士 while on field or in grave)
- Tuner and engine: 破坏剑士的伴龙 49823708 (Level 1 Dragon Tuner, searches 破坏剑 on normal summon, tributes itself for 破坏之剑士, grave revive)
- Extra deck: 破戒蛮龙-破坏龙 11790356 (Synchro 8: all opponent monsters become Dragon), 龙破坏的剑士-破坏之剑士 86240887 (Fusion boss), 破坏剑士的守护绊龙 47158777 (Link-2)
- Spells/traps: 破坏剑士融合 41940225 (Quick-Play fusion using either side of the field), 龙破坏之证 06390406 (adds 破坏之剑士 from deck/grave), 破坏剑的追忆 32104431, 破坏剑士的摇篮 33280639, 破坏剑一闪 15155568, 破坏剑士的宿命 78348934
- Equip-monsters that are also main deck monsters: 破坏剑-魔法破坏之剑 2602411, 破坏剑-武器破坏之剑 38601126, 破坏剑-龙破坏之剑 76218313
- Archetype setcodes: 破坏剑 cards are setcode 0xd6 (searchable by 伴龙, millable by 守护绊龙/摇篮), 破坏之剑士 family is 0xd7 (破坏之剑士 78193831, 剑士高手 03428069, fusion 86240887); 剑士高手 carries both 0xd6 and 0xd7
- Hybrid option in the reference builds: 超魔导剑士-黑魔导剑士 98502113 (Dark Paladin, 黑魔术师 46986414 + 破坏之剑士 78193831) is summonable by 破坏剑士融合 and satisfies 一闪's condition
- Pure reference list 210612破坏之剑士: every archetype card at 3-of plus 3 神之宣告, 3 无限泡影, 3 增殖的G, 1 轮回独断 45064756, 1 DNA改造手术 74701381, 1 封印之黄金柜 75500286; no hand traps beyond G, no 指名者

- **Core Mechanic: The Dragon Lock**

- 破戒蛮龙-破坏龙 11790356 continuously turns every opponent face-up monster into Dragon type, so the fusion floodgate applies to the whole opponent board
- 龙破坏的剑士-破坏之剑士 86240887 (破坏之剑士 78193831 + any Dragon, fusion only): opponent Dragon monsters are forced face-up defense, opponent cannot activate Dragon monster effects, cannot direct attack, piercing, +1000 ATK/DEF per opponent face-up or grave Dragon
- The ATK gains stack: 破坏之剑士 78193831 gains +500 per opponent field/grave Dragon, the fusion gains +1000, so grave Dragon counts decide the damage race
- Equip locks ride on a 破坏之剑士 monster: 破坏剑-龙破坏之剑 76218313 blocks opponent extra deck special summons while equipped, 破坏剑-武器破坏之剑 38601126 blocks face-up S/T effect activation, 破坏剑-魔法破坏之剑 2602411 blocks grave monster effect activation
- 破戒蛮龙 11790356 quick effect on the opponent's turn equips a 破坏剑 monster from your grave to your 破坏之剑士, recycling any equip lock
- Grave-command engine: discard a 破坏剑 card to pay for 伴龙 49823708 grave revive, 追忆 32104431 activation, 融合 41940225 recycle, 宿命 78348934 recycle; banish from grave for 一闪 15155568, 追忆's grave fusion, and 摇篮 33280639 protection — the hand is fuel
- The deck is a midrange grind deck, not a combo deck: turns 1-2 set the lock, then grind with grave recursion and equips

- **One-Card Combo: 破坏剑士的摇篮 33280639**

- Set 摇篮 (normal trap) and pass; it is the pure one-card setup needing only itself in hand
- Step 1: activate 摇篮, cost sends 1 破坏剑 card (prefer 破坏剑-龙破坏之剑 76218313 for the extra deck lock) and 1 破坏之剑士 78193831 from deck to grave
- Step 2: special summon 破戒蛮龙-破坏龙 11790356 from the extra deck; it is destroyed at the opponent's end phase, so the lock window is exactly one turn cycle
- Step 3: 破戒蛮龙 ignition revives 破坏之剑士 78193831 from grave (its revive is off while you already control a face-up 破坏之剑士 family monster)
- Step 4: with 破坏剑士融合 41940225 in hand (or searched by 伴龙), fuse 破坏之剑士 + any Dragon (破戒蛮龙 itself works) into 龙破坏的剑士-破坏之剑士 86240887 for the full lock
- The milled 破坏剑 card choice matters: 龙破坏之剑 76218313 = extra deck lock fuel, 破坏剑的追忆 32104431 = grave fusion fuel, 魔法破坏之剑 2602411 = grave lock fuel
- Two-card line: 伴龙 49823708 + 破坏之剑士/剑士高手 — normal summon 伴龙, search 破坏剑士融合 41940225, tribute 伴龙 for 破坏之剑士 from hand, fuse with a Dragon from hand or the opponent's field

- **End Field**

- Full lock: 破戒蛮龙-破坏龙 11790356 + 龙破坏的剑士-破坏之剑士 86240887 on board, opponent's monsters all Dragon, flipped defense, effects dead, fusion at 2800+1000 per Dragon
- Optional equip layer on the 破坏之剑士 monster: 破坏剑-龙破坏之剑 76218313 (extra deck lock), 破坏剑-武器破坏之剑 38601126 (S/T lock), 破坏剑-魔法破坏之剑 2602411 (grave lock)
- Set backrow: 破坏剑一闪 15155568 (banishes the whole opponent field, needs a fusion that lists 破坏之剑士 present), 破坏剑的追忆 32104431 (deck 破坏之剑士 summon, grave fusion), 神之宣告
- 破坏剑士的守护绊龙 47158777 on field adds a burn line: at end of battle phase with no opponent monsters, deal damage equal to a 破坏之剑士 that did not attack
- 破坏剑士的宿命 78348934 in hand is a damage-step battle trick: banish up to 3 same-race opponent grave monsters, +500 ATK/DEF each for a 破坏剑 or 破坏之剑士 monster

- **Extenders**

- 破坏剑士的伴龙 49823708 grave: discard 1 破坏剑 card, special summon itself while you control 破坏之剑士; note ② and ③ share one use per turn
- 破坏剑的追忆 32104431 grave: banish itself as a quick effect, fusion summon 龙破坏的剑士-破坏之剑士 86240887 using materials banished from your grave (轮回独断 45064756 turns any grave monster into a Dragon for this)
- 破坏剑士融合 41940225 grave: discard 1 card, add itself back to hand
- 破坏剑士的摇篮 33280639 grave: banish itself, your 破坏剑 cards cannot be destroyed by battle or effect this turn
- 破坏剑一闪 15155568 grave: banish itself to negate and destroy an effect targeting your face-up 破坏之剑士 monster
- 破坏剑士的宿命 78348934 grave: discard 1 破坏剑 card, add itself back to hand
- 破坏剑-魔法破坏之剑 2602411 equipped: send itself to grave to add any 破坏剑 monster from grave to hand
- 破坏剑士的守护绊龙 47158777: link summon (any 2 monsters) mills 1 破坏剑 from deck then optionally special summons a 破坏之剑士 family monster from hand — 剑士高手 03428069 is a valid target, use it to bridge into the fusion
- 破坏剑-龙破坏之剑 76218313 equipped: special summons itself from the S/T zone
- 龙破坏之证 06390406: add 破坏之剑士 78193831 from deck or grave to hand, fuel for 伴龙's tribute effect
- 破戒蛮龙 11790356 ignition: if you control no face-up 破坏之剑士, special summon 破坏之剑士 78193831 from grave

- **Halt Points**

- 伴龙 49823708 normal summon search is the deck's first action — Ash, Droll, or effect negation there stops the engine; its tribute and grave effects are separate windows
- 摇篮 33280639 activation is the one-card setup — negate the trap (神之宣告/红指) and the turn's plan collapses; the cost already went to grave but the 破戒蛮龙 never lands
- 追忆 32104431 activation and its grave fusion are chainable quick effects — save interruption for the grave fusion rather than the deck summon
- 破戒蛮龙 11790356 has no protection: remove it (or 摇篮's summon) and the opponent's monsters stop being Dragons, turning off the fusion floodgate
- The lock is not live on turn 1: the deck opens on set traps and maybe 破戒蛮龙, so turns 1-2 are the window to stop it
- 摇篮's 破戒蛮龙 11790356 self-destructs at the opponent's end phase — if you cannot fuse the same turn it dies, leaving no lock
- 增殖的G: lines special summon 1-3 times, so the deck can play under G but never extend greedily; the fusion spell using opponent field monsters costs the opponent nothing extra
- 神之宣告 and 无限泡影 are the only generic interruptions in the pure list — the fusion 86240887 and the synchro 11790356 both die to 无限泡影's monster negate

- **Mirror Match: 破坏之剑士 vs 破坏之剑士**

- The lock only hits Dragon monsters and 破戒蛮龙 11790356 makes BOTH sides' boards Dragons, so the mirror is decided by who resolves their lock first and who has the bigger Dragon count in field plus grave
- 破坏剑-龙破坏之剑 76218313 equipped is the mirror-killer: it blocks the opponent's extra deck, so their fusion 86240887 can never be summoned
- 破坏剑-魔法破坏之剑 2602411 equipped shuts the opponent's 伴龙 49823708 grave revive (monster effect from grave), but does not stop trap grave plays like 追忆 32104431 or 摇篮 33280639
- Whoever controls the fusion 86240887 first flips the opponent's monsters (all Dragons under 破戒蛮龙 11790356) to defense and blocks their activated monster effects — 伴龙 49823708 and 剑士高手 03428069 go dead, but continuous ATK gains like 破坏之剑士 78193831's still apply
- 破坏剑一闪 15155568 wins races: banishing the entire opponent board (all their monsters are Dragons) is usually lethal or game-ending
- 轮回独断 45064756 feeds both sides: declaring Dragon pumps your fusion and enables your 追忆 32104431 grave fusion, but also buffs the opponent's 破坏之剑士 and their fusion
- Watch 剑士高手 03428069's destroy-the-race effect: it equips destroyed monsters and can nuke every same-race monster the opponent controls, including your Dragons

- **Common Mistakes**

- 剑士高手 03428069 is 破坏之剑士 only while on field or in grave — fusing it from the hand as the 破坏之剑士 half fails, put it on the field first
- Forgetting 破坏剑士融合 41940225 can use the OPPONENT's field monsters as material — stealing a Dragon (or any monster under DNA改造手术 74701381) is the classic play
- 破戒蛮龙 11790356's revive effect requires no face-up 破坏之剑士 on your field — summon order matters, revive before summoning the second 破坏之剑士
- Do not fuse away the 破戒蛮龙 11790356 with 破坏剑士融合 41940225 if you still need the Dragon-type conversion; keep the synchro, fuse with the 破坏之剑士 and another Dragon instead
- 破坏之剑士 78193831 only gains ATK, it has no protection — never leave it alone without the lock or an equip
- 追忆 32104431's grave fusion BANISHES materials from grave while 破坏剑士融合 41940225 sends from hand/field to grave — do not mix up which zone feeds which
- 伴龙 49823708 searches on normal summon only, and its tribute and grave revive share one use per turn — plan the 破坏剑 discard carefully
- Do not normal summon the equip-monsters (破坏剑-魔法破坏之剑 2602411, 破坏剑-武器破坏之剑 38601126, 破坏剑-龙破坏之剑 76218313) as beaters; 400-1600 ATK bodies are the locks, equip them from hand
- 破坏剑一闪 15155568 needs a fusion that lists 破坏之剑士 on your field — with only the synchro out it cannot wipe
- The fusion 86240887 and Dark Paladin 98502113 are fusion-summon-only — no monster reborn recursion
- 轮回独断 45064756 changes BOTH graveyards to the declared race; DNA改造手术 74701381 changes BOTH sides' face-up monsters, so your own monsters also become Dragons and enemy Dragon-hate hits you
- The deck files store fusions/synchros/link in one flat list — build must move 龙破坏的剑士-破坏之剑士 86240887, 破戒蛮龙-破坏龙 11790356, and 破坏剑士的守护绊龙 47158777 to the extra deck
- 卡通破坏之剑士 61190918 is a Toon card, not part of the 破坏之剑士 engine — do not confuse it with the archetype core
