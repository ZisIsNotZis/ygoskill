- **Core Mechanic: 圣剑递归**

- Equipping a 圣剑 card flips the dormant monsters live: 莫德雷德 59057152, 加拉哈德 13391185 and 鲍斯 47120245 are LIGHT Normal monsters until equipped, then become effect monsters with Level +1 and DARK attribute
- Equip triggers: 伊文 88923963 summons a 圣骑士衍生物 88923964 token, 崔斯坦 53550467 destroys one face-up card; the token is a LIGHT Normal monster so 高文 19680539 can special summon itself from hand for free
- The recursion: equipped 莫德雷德 59057152 alone on field special summons a 圣骑士 from deck and destroys one equip you control, and the destroyed 圣剑's ③ effect re-equips itself to the freshly summoned 圣骑士 Warrior, refunding the destroy cost
- The five re-equipping 圣剑, each once per turn: 加拉廷 14745409 (+1000 ATK decaying 200 per standby), 阿隆戴特 83438826 (pop one set card, -500 ATK), 石中剑 23562407 (+500 ATK and +500 LP per turn), 克拉伦特 41359411 (pay 500 for direct attack), 天命之圣剑 7452945 (one destruction protection per turn)
- Re-equip targets only your 圣骑士 Warriors and every 圣剑 is a one-per-field unique, so the engine needs multiple different names
- The 圣剑 flush: 贝德维尔 30575681 dumps one 圣剑 from deck on summon, 鲍斯 47120245 mills three 圣剑 with the opponent picking one, 伊索德 59934749 sends any number of different-named equips from deck as cost, all loading the grave for Xyz equips and 断钢湖中剑 46008667
- 断钢湖中剑 46008667 banishes itself from the grave, not the turn it died, to overlay a different 圣骑士 Xyz on top of a 圣骑士 Xyz with materials carrying over, the Xyz ladder
- 圣剑引导的未来 87800375 excavates one card per face-up 圣剑 you control, adds one and reorders the rest, and its grave effect, not the turn it died, special summons a 圣骑士 from deck whose name is not on your field or grave
- 圆桌的圣骑士 55742055 at your end phase counts distinct 圣骑士 names in your field plus grave: 3+ mills a 圣骑士 card, 6+ summons a 圣骑士 from hand and equips a 圣剑 from hand, 9+ recycles a 圣骑士 from grave, exactly 12 draws one card
- 圣骑士与圣剑的巨城 15635751 banishes itself until your standby to place 圆桌的圣骑士 55742055 from deck, grave or hand, then adds a 圣剑 card or summons an 阿托利斯 monster; it also protects a 圣骑士 card once per turn by destroying an equipped card instead, which feeds the 圣剑 ③ recursion
- 桂妮薇儿 19748583 is a monster that counts as a 圣剑 card and equips herself from hand or grave to a 圣骑士 for +300 ATK, arming 莫德雷德 59057152 with no 圣剑 in hand
- 圣骑士与圣剑的巨城 15635751 carries both setcodes so it counts for the Round Table name count and for 圣剑 searches, while 圣剑引导的未来 87800375 is 圣剑-only
- The locks: 伊文 88923963, 圣骑士三兄弟 57690191 and 梅林 03580032 ① restrict to 圣骑士-only summons until end phase, 康尼厄斯 42472002 as material restricts extra deck summons to 圣骑士, and 天魔伏圣剑 23249029 restricts summons to Warriors

- **One-Card Combo**

- No true one-card full combo, every full line needs a second card, matching the mid-range grind identity
- Closest single openers: 梅林 03580032 normal summoned then tributed summons any 圣骑士 from deck but extends no further alone; 圣杯的继承 51412776 adds any 圣骑士 monster or 圣剑 card from deck or grave and sets up the two-card line; 巨城 15635751 places 圆桌的圣骑士 55742055 and adds a 圣剑, starting the grind
- Baseline two-card combo: 莫德雷德 59057152 plus one 圣剑, sourced from hand, from 桂妮薇儿 19748583, or tutored from deck by discarding 布拉达曼特 91479482
- Step 1: normal summon 莫德雷德 59057152 and equip 圣剑 加拉廷 14745409

- **Mirror Match**

- Never destroy the opponent's 圣剑 equips, the ③ re-equip refunds them, kill the equipped monster first with 莫德雷德 59057152 as the priority target
- The Xyz ladder via 断钢湖中剑 46008667 is the strongest play in the mirror, keep 墓穴的指名者 24224830 or grave banishment for it
- The player who resolves 莫德雷德 59057152 plus 圣剑 first wins the resource race, each activation generates a monster and a re-equip
- 崔斯坦 53550467 protection makes monsters under 1800 ATK immune to targeting and 断钢湖中剑 46008667 makes its holder untargetable, so mirror removal needs effects that do not target or battle
- 圆桌的圣骑士 55742055 only counts your own cards, racing the opponent to more distinct 圣骑士 names decides the long game

- **Common Mistakes**

- Do not special summon before resolving 莫德雷德 59057152 ②, it requires being the only monster you control and needs an equipped 圣剑 to become an effect monster
- Do not break your own locks: 伊文 88923963, 圣骑士三兄弟 57690191 and 梅林 03580032 ① restrict to 圣骑士-only summons, 康尼厄斯 42472002 as material restricts extra deck summons to 圣骑士, 天魔伏圣剑 23249029 restricts to Warriors
- 天魔伏圣剑 23249029 destroys the monster it was previously equipped to, sequence it last or on a monster already committed to material
- 圣剑引导的未来 87800375 and 断钢湖中剑 46008667 grave effects cannot be used the turn they reached the grave
- 圣剑引导的未来 87800375 grave summon requires the name to be absent from your field and grave, and 伊索德 59934749 ② needs a Warrior whose level exactly equals the number of different-named equips sent
- 伊索德 59934749 ① added Warrior is locked from summoning and activation this turn, special summon the body with ② instead
- 圆桌的圣骑士 55742055 draw effect triggers only at exactly 12 distinct 圣骑士 names, and all end phase effects are optional per threshold
- 圣骑士与圣剑的巨城 15635751 summon branch needs 圣骑士 阿托利斯 92125819 in the main deck, this build runs none and only uses the 圣剑-add branch
