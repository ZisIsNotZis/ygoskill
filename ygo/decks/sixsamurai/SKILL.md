# 六武众 (Six Samurai) Deck Experience

- **Deck Identity**

- EARTH Warrior swarm-synchro deck built on four linked families: 六武众 0x103d monsters, 影六武众 0x903d monsters, 六武式 0x203d spells, 紫炎 0x20 effect monsters
- Main tuners: 六武众的指南番 16968936, 六武众的破戒僧 80570228, 六武众的影武者 1498130, 影六武众-风魔 71207871
- Main bodies: 真六武众-阴鬼 2511717, 真六武众-辉斩 49721904, 六武众的师范 83039729, 六武众的隐退者 61737116, 影六武众-木猿 6579928, 影六武众-玄蕃 7291576, 六武众的真影 15327215, 影六武众-初芽 44686185
- Extra deck: 真六武众-紫炎 29981921, 真魔六武众-紫炎 34235530, 真魔六武众-辉斩 42209438, 真魔六武众-缘 70634245, 六武众的军大将 74752631, 影六武众-理伴 33964637, plus generics 鲜花女男爵 84815190, 召命之神弓-阿波罗萨 4280258, 闭锁世界的冥神 98127546, 自然兽 33198837, 失X-剑士 招来者 4423206
- Key spells and traps: 紫炎的狼烟 54031490, 六武之门 27970830, 紫炎的道场 47436247, 六武众的团结 72345736, 六武式袭双阵 28273805, 六武众的荒行 27821104, 六武众之理 27178262, 六武众的影忍术 79968632, 六武众推参！ 46874015, 六武式真传天魔六段冲 90939874, 六尺琼勾玉 41458579
- Attribute diversity is a resource: monsters span EARTH, WIND, FIRE, DARK, LIGHT, and mechanics key off it (木猿 6579928 search, 理伴 33964637 fusion, 真影 15327215 stat copy)
- 2025 repo builds: pure control build runs 3x 指南番 16968936, 破戒僧 80570228, 师范 83039729, 真辉斩 49721904, 真影 15327215, 木猿 6579928 with classic traps; engine build adds 六武之门 27970830, 道场 47436247 x3, 团结 72345736, 军大将 74752631 x2, 狼烟 54031490 x3, 袭双阵 28273805 x2, 荒行 27821104, 影忍术 79968632, 理伴 33964637, 增援 32807846 and a Speedroid package for 失X-剑士 招来者 4423206 and 方程式运动员 电光赛道名将 33158448

- **Core Mechanic: Bushido Counter (武士道指示物) Engine**

- 六武之门 27970830: every 六武众 summon places 2 counters; remove 2 for +500 ATK to a 六武众/紫炎 effect monster, 4 to add a 六武众 from deck or GY, 6 to special a 紫炎 effect monster from GY
- 紫炎的道场 47436247: 1 counter per summon; send it to GY to special a 六武众/紫炎 effect monster of level equal to or below its counters from deck
- 六武众的团结 72345736: 1 counter per summon up to 2; send it to GY to draw that many cards
- 六武众的军大将 74752631: Link-2 of 2 Warriors including 1 六武众; on Link summon discard 1 card to search any card that places Bushido counters; gains 1 counter per 六武众 summoned in its zone and 100 ATK per counter
- 六武式风雷斩 23212990: trap that removes 1 counter to destroy a monster or bounce a card
- 六武式真传天魔六段冲 90939874: trap field wipe needing 3 different 六武众 Synchros, reduced by 1 required type per 6 counters; if destroyed or banished by an opponent effect, specials a 六武众/紫炎 effect monster from deck or extra
- There is no token engine: the counter loop above is the actual resource engine, and 名工 虎铁 73431236 is an unrelated flip monster that is not played

- **Core Mechanic: Attribute Diversity Swarm**

- 影六武众-木猿 6579928: on special summon, search a 六武众 whose attribute is not on your field
- 六武众的真影 15327215: specials itself from hand when any 影六武众 is summoned; once per turn banishes a level 4 or lower 六武众 from GY as cost to copy its level, attribute, ATK, and DEF
- 影六武众-理伴 33964637: contact fusion from 3 六武众 of different attributes on field; once per turn banishes a 六武众 card to banish a card on the field
- Every 影六武众 (木猿 6579928, 玄蕃 7291576, 风魔 71207871, 初芽 44686185, 理伴 33964637) has the same destroy-replace: when exactly 1 of your 六武众 would be destroyed by an effect, banish this card from GY instead
- 影六武众-风魔 71207871: when destroyed by battle or effect, specials any 六武众 from deck as a replacement body
- 影六武众-玄蕃 7291576: on normal summon adds a banished 六武众 to hand, feeding 真影 15327215 copies and 影忍术 79968632 costs

- **One-Card Combo (Nearest Equivalent)**

- Six Samurai has no true one-card starter: every line needs a body plus an extender or tuner, making it a two-card combo deck
- The single-card opener is 紫炎的狼烟 54031490, which searches any level 3 or lower 六武众: 指南番 16968936, 破戒僧 80570228, 风魔 71207871, 阴鬼 2511717, or 影武者 1498130
- Standard opening, cards 狼烟 54031490 plus 真六武众-阴鬼 2511717 (增援 32807846 can substitute for 狼烟):
- Step 1: activate 狼烟, add 六武众的指南番 16968936 to hand
- Step 2: normal summon 阴鬼, its effect specials 指南番 from hand
- Step 3: synchro 指南番 plus 阴鬼 into 真六武众-紫炎 29981921; 指南番 as material gives the synchro an effect weakening all opponent monsters by 500 ATK, and 指南番 in GY searches 六武式袭双阵 28273805
- Step 4: 真六武众-辉斩 49721904 specials itself from hand because a 六武众 is on field
- Step 5: activate 袭双阵, special 六武众的影武者 1498130 from hand, and because 2 or more 六武众 are on field also flip an opponent monster face-down
- Step 6: synchro 影武者 plus 真辉斩 into 真魔六武众-紫炎 34235530, whose summon search adds 大将军 紫炎 63176202 or 六武之门 27970830
- Step 7: 大将军 紫炎 specials itself from hand while 2 or more 六武众 are on field

- **End Field**

- The 紫炎 lock: 真六武众-紫炎 29981921 (negates 1 spell or trap per turn, destroy-replace) plus 真魔六武众-紫炎 34235530 (negates 1 monster effect per turn, destroy-replace) plus 大将军 紫炎 63176202 (opponent may activate only 1 spell or trap per turn)
- 六武之门 27970830 or 紫炎的道场 47436247 face-up for counter recursion, and 袭双阵 28273805 in GY for its add-a-六武众 effect
- Alternative: link 2 bodies into 六武众的军大将 74752631 to search 道场 47436247, 团结 72345736, or 六武之门 27970830; the Speedroid package (贝陀螺集合体 81275020 plus 竹蜻蜓电子人 53932291) instead makes 方程式运动员 电光赛道名将 33158448 (spell/trap negate) or 失X-剑士 招来者 4423206, and 自然兽 33198837 comes from an EARTH tuner like 指南番 16968936 plus an EARTH body like 隐退者 61737116
- Counter-board: set 六武式真传天魔六段冲 90939874 behind 3 different 六武众 Synchros (真紫炎 29981921, 真魔紫炎 34235530, 真魔辉斩 42209438, 真魔缘 70634245) or 6 counters for a full field wipe

- **Extenders**

- 六武众的师范 83039729: free special from hand while any 六武众 is on field; when destroyed by an opponent effect, adds a 六武众 from GY
- 六武众的隐退者 61737116: free special from hand when the opponent controls a monster and you control none, a going-second starter
- 六武众的破戒僧 80570228: free special from hand once per turn; sent from field to GY searches a 六武众 quick-play spell (袭双阵 28273805, 荒行 27821104, 之理 27178262, 影忍术 79968632); as material for a 六武众 synchro, opponent monsters lose 1 level
- 六武众的指南番 16968936: free special from hand once per turn; sent from field to GY searches a 六武式 card (袭双阵 28273805, 三段冲 81426505, 风雷斩 23212990, 真传天魔六段冲 90939874)
- 六武式袭双阵 28273805: quick-play that specials a 2000-or-less ATK 六武众 from hand or GY, and flips an opponent monster face-down when 2 or more 六武众 are on field; in GY, banishes itself to add a 六武众 from GY
- 六武众的荒行 27821104: quick-play targeting a 六武众, specials a different-code 六武众 with the same ATK from deck, such as 阴鬼 2511717 at 200 into 指南番 16968936 or 玄蕃 7291576 at 500 into 破戒僧 80570228; the target is destroyed at end phase
- 六武众之理 27178262: quick-play that sends 1 face-up 六武众 to GY to special a 六武众 from GY, cycling GY triggers
- 六武众的影忍术 79968632: quick-play that sends 1 monster you control to GY to special a banished 六武众, partnering with 真影 15327215 copies and 玄蕃 7291576 recovery
- 六武众推参！ 46874015: trap that specials a 六武众 from GY, destroyed at end phase
- 影六武众-初芽 44686185: banishes 2 六武众 from GY or field to special another 六武众 from GY
- 真魔六武众-缘 70634245: on synchro, banishes any number of 六武众 from GY to bounce that many opponent monsters; when sent to GY, specials a banished 六武众
- 真魔六武众-辉斩 42209438: gives your 六武众 600 ATK and DEF in battle phase; during the opponent main phase banishes a 六武式 from GY to destroy 1 card; revives itself from GY while 2 or more 六武众 are on field
- 失X-剑士 招来者 4423206: rank 3 that detaches to special an EARTH level 4 Warrior, such as 真辉斩 49721904 or 木猿 6579928, from deck, destroyed at end phase

- **Halt Points**

- 灰流丽 14558127 on 狼烟 54031490, the 指南番 16968936 GY search, the 真魔紫炎 34235530 search, or the 军大将 74752631 search ends the line
- 墓穴的指名者 24224830 or 抹杀之指名者 65681983 on 指南番 16968936 or 破戒僧 80570228 removes the 六武式 or quick-play search
- 无限泡影 10045474 or 效果遮蒙者 on 真六武众-阴鬼 2511717 stops the swarm and strands the tuner in hand
- 尼比鲁 lands after the fifth summon: the opening line summons 6 times (阴鬼 2511717, 指南番 16968936, 真紫炎 29981921, 真辉斩 49721904, 影武者 1498130, 真魔紫炎 34235530)
- Under 增殖的G 23434538, stop at 真紫炎 29981921 only or make 真魔紫炎 34235530 and pass, keeping special summons to 2 or 3
- 真魔紫炎 34235530 negates once per turn, so a third monster effect in the same chain resolves; 真紫炎 29981921 covers spells and traps only
- 次元吸引者 or 大宇宙 cuts the GY engine: 指南番 16968936 search, 袭双阵 28273805, 玄蕃 7291576, 推参 46874015, and 影忍术 79968632 all need the GY
- 紫炎的道场 47436247 only fires from accumulated counters: if your summons are negated there are no counters and no deck special

- **Mirror Match: 六武众 vs 六武众**

- The first resolved 真魔六武众-紫炎 34235530 negate wins the monster-effect war; 真六武众-紫炎 29981921 negates the opponent 袭双阵 28273805 and 荒行 27821104 activations
- 大将军 紫炎 63176202 lock decides the mirror: whoever resolves it first forces the opponent to 1 spell or trap activation per turn, which 真紫炎 29981921 can then negate
- Prefer bounce or flip-down over destruction: the 影六武众 destroy-replace banishes itself from GY instead, so destroy removal like 真魔辉斩 42209438 or 三段冲 81426505 whiffs; use 真魔缘 70634245 bounce, 袭双阵 28273805 flip-down, or 风雷斩 23212990 bounce mode
- A mass destroy such as 真传天魔六段冲 90939874 destroys every monster at once, which does not trigger the exactly-one destroy-replace
- 指南番 16968936 material effect at minus 500 ATK and 破戒僧 80570228 material effect at minus 1 level stack on each 六武众 synchro and decide mirror battles, for example 师范 83039729 at 2100 against 真紫炎 29981921 reduced to 2000
- Watch attribute gates: 木猿 6579928 cannot search an attribute already on your field, and 真影 15327215 stat copies shift which searches are legal mid-combo
- 六尺琼勾玉 41458579 negates a destroy effect aimed at your 六武众, answering the mirror 真魔辉斩 42209438 destroy and the 天魔六段冲 90939874 wipe

- **Common Mistakes**

- 指南番 16968936 GY search only gets 六武式 0x203d cards (袭双阵 28273805, 三段冲 81426505, 风雷斩 23212990, 真传天魔六段冲 90939874), never 狼烟 54031490, 六武之门 27970830, or 团结 72345736
- 破戒僧 80570228 GY search only gets 六武众 quick-play spells (袭双阵 28273805, 荒行 27821104, 之理 27178262, 影忍术 79968632)
- 军大将 74752631 searches only cards that place Bushido counters (六武之门 27970830, 道场 47436247, 团结 72345736); 风雷斩 23212990 removes counters and is not searchable by it
- 真紫炎 29981921 negates spells and traps only while 真魔紫炎 34235530 negates monster effects only; firing the wrong one wastes the once-per-turn
- 真魔辉斩 42209438 destroy is a quick effect only during the opponent main phase; its GY revive needs 2 or more face-up 六武众
- 真魔缘 70634245 banishes any number of 六武众 from GY as cost, so over-banishing starves 袭双阵 28273805, 玄蕃 7291576, and 推参 46874015; its own GY trigger revives a banished 六武众
- 影六武众 destroy-replace triggers only when exactly 1 六武众 would be destroyed by an effect and the 影六武众 is already in GY; a board wipe like 真传天魔六段冲 90939874 or 雷击 bypasses it
- 荒行 27821104 and 推参 46874015 destroy their specialed monster at end phase, so spend them as synchro material first
- 六武之门 27970830 gains counters only while face-up: activate it before the swarm or earlier summons are wasted
- 真影 15327215 cannot copy an identical monster, meaning the same level, attribute, ATK, and DEF, and only comes out on a 影六武众 summon, not a plain 六武众 one
- 阴鬼 2511717 specials only from hand and its 1500 ATK bonus needs another 六武众, so use it as synchro fodder rather than a beater
- 一族的团结 86780027 turns off when any non-Warrior reaches your GY, so 灰流丽 14558127 and 增殖的G 23434538 as Zombie and Insect break the pure beatdown build
- 六武众的先导 78792195 is legacy tribute removal not played in modern builds; the cards 六武众的对手 and 六武众的决心 do not exist in this database and the Link monster is 六武众的军大将 74752631
