# 龙骑兵团 (Dragunity) Deck Experience

- **Core Mechanic: Equip-Dragon Engine**

- The engine equips Dragon Tuners onto Winged Beasts as cards in the spell/trap zone, then re-summons them as monsters to generate extra bodies at every step
- 元老院 92868896: discard 1 龙骑兵团 card to equip 1 龙骑兵团 Tuner from the deck to itself, after resolving you may only Special Summon Dragon monsters from the extra deck this turn, and its second effect substitutes an equipped 龙骑兵团 card for any of your 龙骑兵团 cards that would be destroyed
- 方阵龙 59755122: while equipped, once per turn in your main phase it special summons itself, and its once-per-turn resets when it leaves the field, so re-equipping it repeatedly keeps the tuner loop alive
- 士兵 81661951: special summons 1 face-up 龙骑兵团 card from your spell/trap zone, turning an equipped dragon back into a monster
- Synchro material rules: 雷枪龙骑士 21249921, 赤枪龙骑士 34116027, 三叉龙骑士 80159717 and 长枪龙骑士 25682811 need a Dragon Tuner plus a Winged Beast non-tuner, while 祭杖龙骑士 12496261, 碧枪龙骑士 36556781, 战枪龙骑士 34528176, 屠枪龙骑士 48891960 and 毒枪龙骑士 88234821 accept any non-tuner
- On summon the knights re-equip dragons from the grave to keep looping: 雷枪龙骑士 21249921 equips 1 Level 3 or lower 龙骑兵团 dragon, 长枪龙骑士 25682811 equips any number of 龙骑兵团 dragons and gains 300 attack per equipped 龙骑兵团 card, 祭杖龙骑士 12496261 equips 1 龙骑兵团 monster and equips itself to a 龙骑兵团 monster for 1000 attack and defense if it later goes to the grave
- 双始龙 26577155 is the glue: discard it to search 龙之溪谷 62265044, and it revives itself from the grave while you control any 龙骑兵团 monster, but it is banished when it leaves the field, it locks you to Dragon extra-deck summons that turn, and it can only be synchro material for a 龙骑兵团 summon
- 长柄大刀龙 29253591 counts as Level 4 while used as synchro material, enabling Level 8 长枪龙骑士 25682811 from a Level 4 Winged Beast, and it also special summons itself while equipped

- **One-Card Combo: 军事官**

- Starter: normal summon 军事官 81962318 with no other cards needed, every step below verified against the scripts
- Step 1: 军事官 81962318 summon effect sends 弯形大刀龙 8039562 from the deck to the grave
- Step 2: 弯形大刀龙 8039562 banishes itself from the grave to equip 方阵龙 59755122 from the deck to 军事官
- Step 3: 方阵龙 59755122 special summons itself, leaving 军事官 and 方阵龙 on the field
- Step 4: link summon 始枪龙骑士 11969228 with both as Dragon/Winged Beast materials, its effect adds 龙之溪谷 62265044 or any 龙骑兵团 spell/trap from the deck to hand
- Result of the single card: 始枪龙骑士 11969228 plus 龙之溪谷 62265044, with 方阵龙 59755122 in the grave, the engine is live but a second card is needed to extend this turn
- Standard extension: 龙之溪谷 62265044 discards a card to add 双始龙 26577155 or 司令官 89172051 from deck to hand, 双始龙 revives itself from the grave and 司令官 special summons itself while 龙之溪谷 is face-up
- From a Level 4 Winged Beast plus 方阵龙 59755122, synchro 赤枪龙骑士 34116027 to search any Level 4 or lower Dragon/Winged Beast and discard one, typically adding the next tuner or body
- Ladder upward: 双始龙 26577155 as tuner plus 赤枪龙骑士 34116027 as non-tuner Synchro makes 战枪龙骑士 34528176 at Level 8, then 方阵龙 59755122 plus 战枪龙骑士 34528176 makes 屠枪龙骑士 48891960 or 毒枪龙骑士 88234821 at Level 10
- Alternative Level 8: 长柄大刀龙 29253591 as Level 4 plus a Level 4 Winged Beast makes 长枪龙骑士 25682811, which re-equips every 龙骑兵团 dragon in the grave, and each equipped dragon that can summon itself re-enters the ladder

- **End Field**

- 支配者-乔治 70522875 (Link-4, needs a Tuner among its materials) special summons a Level 5 or higher 龙骑兵团 from grave or extra deck on link summon and again whenever the opponent summons, negates a spell/trap activation by sending an equipped card to the grave, and while it carries a face-up 龙骑兵团 equip the opponent cannot activate monster effects from the grave
- 毒枪龙骑士 88234821 (Level 10): quick-negates a monster effect by banishing 1 龙骑兵团 from your grave, banishes any monster it destroys in battle, and if destroyed by the opponent it destroys all their spells and traps

- 雷枪龙骑士 21249921 doubled to 3800 attack and 龙骑兵团征兵 52962804 attack immunity are the reliable ways through 持枪神 71645463 battle protection

- **Common Mistakes**

- Do not plan non-Dragon extra-deck plays after 元老院 92868896, 双始龙 26577155 or 疾风之龙骑兵团 89450409 resolve, all three lock the extra deck to Dragon summons this turn
- 士兵 81661951 needs a face-up 龙骑兵团 card in the spell/trap zone, equip first and unequip second, never activate it as an opener
- Do not summon 方阵龙 59755122 before a synchro is ready, the loop is equip to Winged Beast, summon 方阵龙, synchro, re-equip 方阵龙 from the grave, summon again
- 赤枪龙骑士 34116027 searches then discards one card, keep a Dragon/Winged Beast in hand to discard or 双始龙 26577155 to fuel the grave
- 战枪龙骑士 34528176 negates a number of face-up cards equal to the monster cards in your spell/trap zone, with no equips equipped it cannot negate anything
- The monster summoned by 始枪龙骑士 11969228 second effect is negated and cannot be link material, do not plan to climb with it
- 支配者-乔治 70522875 negation costs an equipped card, keep it equipped or the negation stops working
- 屠枪龙骑士 48891960 and 毒枪龙骑士 88234821 costs banish 龙骑兵团 cards from your own grave, check the grave before activating
- 双始龙 26577155 revived from the grave is banished if it leaves the field, do not tribute or release it casually
- 指挥官 28183605 equips from the grave only, it needs a dragon in the grave first, while 首席百夫长 18060565 and 元老院 92868896 equip from the deck
- 弯形大刀龙 8039562 first effect needs a 龙骑兵团 monster already on the field as its equip target, it is never the first move
- 疾风之龙骑兵团 89450409 requires your field completely empty and the opponent to control a monster, sequence it before any summon
- 天球之圣刻印 24361622 bounce works only on the opponent turn and only while it is in an Extra Monster Zone, keep it in the Extra Monster Zone to keep the out available
