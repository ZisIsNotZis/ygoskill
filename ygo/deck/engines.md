# Combo Engines

Definition: mini-axes of 2 to 8 cards that can be slotted into multiple decks to add specific functionality. Join criteria: ydkshow mean greater than or equal to 0.5 and q75 greater than or equal to 1 in target deck consensus, otherwise the engine is a personal preference not a recognized engine.

- **Dinosaur Engine**

- Core cards: 化石調査 times 2, 幻創の混種恐竜, 食魂のバクレツ竜, 小型角竜, 小型翼竜
- Function: mill to special summon to search to expand
- Applications: Scrap, Rate of Change,混沌
- Cards needed: 5 to 7

- **Superheavy Samurai Engine**

- Core cards: 牛若丸, モーコウ
- Function: no Spell/Trap in graveyard enables defence position special summon and search
- Applications: Scrap, Self-Playing, Rate of Change
- Constraint: graveyard must contain zero Spells and Traps
- Cards needed: 2 to 3

- **Doumyouji Engine**

- Core cards: 鍛冶師, 落涙, 詠聖, 大圣棺, 鎮魂棺
- Function: equip to destroy to special summon from graveyard
- Applications: Self-Playing,混沌
- Dependency: LIGHT Fiend monsters
- Cards needed: 3 to 5

- **Gold Cabinet Engine**

- Core cards: 光の黄金柜, 未来への沈黙, 黒魔術の少女
- Function: delayed search then special summon and ATK increase
- Applications: Self-Playing, Dark Magician
- Constraint: requires 2 turn wait
- Cards needed: 3 to 4

- **Sinbao Engine**

- Core cards: 黒魔女ディアベル, 罪宝の詐欺
- Function: set Sinbao card plus special summon from hand plus recycle
- Applications: Purrely, Rescue ACE
- Dependency: Thistle field name
- Cards needed: 3 to 5

- **Albaz Engine**

- Core cards: 赫界竜, サロンイーター, マーグレット, ドルイド, バルド
- Function: banish LIGHT or DARK to special summon from hand
- Applications: Labrynth, Rate of Change
- Cards needed: 4 to 6

- **Dandan Engine**

- Core cards: 速攻回転, 曳光龍
- Function: special summon Dandan then search then expand
- Applications: Labrynth, Labrynth
- Constraint: can only special summon DARK monsters the turn activated
- Cards needed: 2 to 4

- **Train Engine**

- Core cards: 特別行车, 青い旅人, 回転調車, グスタフ
- Function: search to special summon to burn damage
- Applications: Self-Playing, Machine
- Dependency: Rank 10 Machine monster
- Cards needed: 3 to 5

- **White Forest Engine**

- Core cards: リゼット, 森厄狼, 濡血蝠, 魔女
- Function: send Spell/Trap to hand to draw or search plus Synchro
- Applications: Sinbao, Rate of Change
- Dependency: LIGHT Spellcaster monsters
- Cards needed: 3 to 5

- **Star Relic Engine**

- Core cards: 星冠, 星杖
- Function: defence position special summon then negate then search
- Applications: Self-Playing聖楽
- Dependency: Link monster in connected zone
- Cards needed: 2 to 3

- **K9 Engine**

- Core cards: 飯綱, 霜妖, 呪, 野狼, K9事件

- Applications: K9, Rate of Change

- **Genex Engine**

- **Shaddoll Engine**

- Core cards: 神数神託, 神数の絆, 皮莉佳
