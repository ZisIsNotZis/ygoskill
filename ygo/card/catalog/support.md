# Generic Support Cards

Definition: cards for your own resource management including search, mill, draw, or special summon. Generic means no specific series restriction or restriction is wide enough for multiple archetypes. Excludes opponent interference and archetype-specific support.

- **Search Cards**

- 増援 32807846 lim1, search Level 4 or lower Warrior from deck to hand, Warrior decks, limited to 1, typical count 1-2
- 星球改造 73628505 lim1, search Field Spell from deck to hand, Field Spell reliant decks, limited to 1, typical count 1
- 龙之灵庙 41620959, mill 1 Dragon from deck then optionally mill 1 Normal Dragon, Dragon decks, typical count 1
- 封印の黄金柜 75500286 lim1, banish 1 card from deck face-up add to hand in 2 turns, universal delayed search, limited to 1, can be disrupted while banished, typical count 1-2
- 小世界現象 89558743, reveal 1 card from hand reveal matching attribute/race/ATK monster from deck add that monster to hand, bridges between unrelated series, needs bridge card in hand, typical count 0-1
- 三戦の号 35269904, if opponent activated a effect this turn search 1 Normal Spell or Trap, Normal Spell/Trap reliant decks, requires opponent effect activation, typical count 1-2
- 闇の誘惑 1475311 lim1, draw 2 then banish 1 DARK from hand or send entire hand to grave, DARK attribute decks, limited to 1, risks hand loss without DARK, typical count 1-2
