# Extra-Deck Summon Geometries

- Scope: patterns distilled from 110+ deck docs under ygo/decks plus the generic-extra catalog; anchor codes verified in cards.cdb; read the per-deck doc before relying on a specific ladder because each archetype adds its own summon locks

- **Rank/Level Ladder: Every Rung Summons the Next Tier**

- Pattern: a fixed extra deck chain where each tier's summon effect produces the material or body for the next tier, so the main deck only needs to produce the base
- 机巧 (Karakuri): 无零 23874409 → 无零怒 66976526 → 无零武 25904894, each synchro summon specials a 机巧 tuner from deck (3+5=8, 8+1=9) and the bosses fire on every position flip
- 魔妖 (Mayakashi): synchro tiers 3/5/7/9/11, 妲姬 42542842 revives from GY on each extra-deck 魔妖 summon to become the next tier's tuner; destroyed tiers cascade back from the GY, and 雪女 66870733 → 垂冰之魔妖-雪女 36114945 → 零冰之魔妖-雪女 2645637 top the ladder with leftover bodies
- 废品/星尘 (Junk/Stardust): 废品增速者 77075360 dumps a tuner swarm one-per-level → 加速同调星尘龙 30983281 → 星尘龙 44508094 → 红龙 63436931, whose effect two tags into 流天救世星龙 40939228 / 救世星龙 7841112
- 相剑 (Swordsoul): fixed token math, non-tuner + Level 4 Wyrm token = Level 8 赤霄 69248256, Level 6 龙渊 93490856 + token = Level 10 承影 96633955 or 鲜花女男爵 84815190
- Implication: interrupt the base, not the boss — 灰流丽 14558127 or 无限泡影 10045474 on the first tuner/token-maker or the first synchro's deck special summon ends the ladder at its first rung

- **Overlay-On-Top Xyz: Material Pile Transfer**

- Pattern: an Xyz (or Link) valid as the next Xyz's material carries its whole material pile upward, and material count is the payoff resource
- 十二兽 (Zoodiac): any 十二兽 Xyz overlays onto a face-up 十二兽 with one material 狗环 41375811 → 虎炮 11510448 → 龙枪 48905153; 蛇笞 31755044 attaches itself from hand, then 天霆号 阿宙斯 90448279 or 未来No.0 未来皇 65305468 → 未来龙皇 26973555 finish the pile
- 天霆号 阿宙斯 90448279 is the generic top: overlays once per turn onto any Xyz that battled, inherits the pile, wipes the field with two detaches (spright 54498517, galaxyeyes, constellar, gunkansuship, exosister, orcust all use it)
- 未来No.0 未来皇 65305468 → 未来龙皇 26973555: indestructible, one negate-plus-control-steal per material (utopia, zoodiac, gunkansuship)
- 卫星闪灵 (Spright): Link-2 迅妖龙炮 72329844 is valid Xyz material for 巨大喷流 54498517, whose ATK doubles when a link monster is material
- 自奏圣乐 (Orcust): 丁吉尔苏 93854893 overlays directly onto the link 伽拉忒亚 30741503 and sends 1 opponent card
- Implication: the underlay is the value — remove the base Xyz before battle or negate the attack to deny the 阿宙斯 overlay, and treat every attached material as a future negate or wipe charge

- **Fixed Tuner + Body Math (Level Fixers and Tokens)**

- Pattern: the deck forces one fixed level sum with a token tuner, a level-copying tuner, or a level-changing effect; the extra deck only needs that one sum
- 冰结界 (Ice Barrier): 镜魔师 9396662 tributes and summons 3 tokens while raising its own level, then 5+2+4 = 还零龙 三叉龙 70980824; 霜精 70703416 copies a milled monster's level to fix the 9 for 三叉龙 52687916
- 龙星 (Yang Zing): 源龙星 65536818 (Level 5 tuner) + 椒图 25935625 (2) = 邪龙星 43202238, + 水龙星-赑屃 2095764 (2) = 幻龙星 19048328; 辉龙星 83755611 needs a non-tuner Wyrm material
- 异色眼 (Odd-Eyes): 娱乐伙伴 异色眼同调士 82224646 makes any 异色眼 monster a Level 1 tuner for 陨火龙 80696379
- Implication: the level-fixer or token-maker is the hinge — 效果遮蒙者 97268402 / 无限泡影 10045474 on it kills every sum; the summon locks the fixer carries (token forbids Xyz/Link, WATER-only, synchro-only) are the deck's own halt points, so non-archetype plays must come before the fixer

- **1-Material / 2-for-1 Fusion from Deck or Grave**

- Pattern: a fusion spell (or monster effect) uses monsters from the deck or both graveyards as material, so one activation equals one boss plus a chain of grave triggers
- 影依 (Shaddoll): 影依融合 44394295 fuses from the deck when the opponent controls an extra-deck monster; 影依的伪典 21011044 fuses from field + GY as an opponent-turn quick effect
- 宝石骑士 (Gem-Knight): 明亮融合 07394770 (continuous spell) fuses from the deck; 幽晶原核 24484270 (Link-2) shuffles GY + banished 宝石 into the deck and fuses again
- 恐啡肽狂龙 (Dinomorphia): 激昂 78420796 fuses during the opponent's Main Phase from exactly 1 Deck + 1 Extra Deck material, so the fielded 镰刀龙 92133240 stays as an extra body
- 召唤师 (Invoked): 召唤魔术-「杯」 76334960 fuses 1 of your monsters + 1 from the deck; 召唤魔术 74063034 may use both players' GYs for 召唤兽
- 月光 (Lunalight): 月光融合 87931906 adds exactly 1 deck/extra material when the opponent has an extra-deck monster; 多层融合 58570206 uses up to the opponent's monster count of extra deck monsters as material
- 英雄 (HERO): 融合命运 52947044 fuses only from hand + deck and the fusion is destroyed next end phase
- Implication: the spell activation is the choke point — 灰流丽 14558127 on the fusion spell (it special summons from the deck) ends the whole line including the grave triggers; for monster-based fusers (幽晶原核 24484270, Tearlaments), GY banishment is the answer instead

- **Contact / Field-Only Fusion vs Fusion Spell**

- Pattern: contact fusion is an inherent summon with no spell to negate, so the counterplay is removing the field or GY materials
- 剑斗兽 (Gladiator Beast): pure contact fusion by returning field monsters to the deck (凯撒 48156348), plus the battle-phase spell 团结的剑斗兽 66290900 that shuffles hand/field/GY materials
- 电子龙 (Cyber Dragon): 嵌合要塞龙 79229522 contact-fuses 电子龙 plus Machine monsters from either field (eats opponent Machines); 机械驱动之夜 84797028 makes every monster a Machine
- 半龙女仆 (Dragonmaid): 贴身龙女 48658295 contact-fuses by banishing 1 matching Dragonmaid from field + 1 from GY (same attribute, different level) and is fusion-only, so 更衣 40110009 cannot make it
- 霸王眷龙 凶饿毒 43387895 contact-releases two DARK pendulums, while 异色眼风雷龙 53262004 needs the spell 异色眼融合 48144509 (hand + field)
- Implication: never hand-trap a contact fusion — interrupt the materials on the field/GY or remove the boss before it tags or recurs

- **Attribute-Pair Fusion and the Attribute Substitute**

- Pattern: fixed material formula of 1 named engine monster plus 1 monster of a listed attribute; the attribute axis is the toolbox
- 召唤师 (Invoked): 阿莱斯特 86120751 + one attribute monster into the eight 召唤兽 (梅尔卡巴 75286621 LIGHT negate, 卡利古拉 13529466 floodgate); 暴走魔法阵 47679935 makes your fusion-summon effects unnegatable
- 影依 (Shaddoll): 1 影依 + 1 attribute (拿非利 20366274 light, 米德拉什 94977269 dark), and 影依的原核 4904633 substitutes any attribute — the glue that makes every fusion live
- 半龙女仆 (Dragonmaid): 贴身龙女 48658295 needs same attribute plus different level, which 盛情 78231355 mills to order
- Implication: the attribute substitute (原核 4904633) and the engine searcher (阿莱斯特 86120751) are the real engines; 神之宣告 41420027 on the substitute or removing it from GY cuts the whole fusion axis

- **Boss-Brick → Bigger Boss (Ladder within the Extra Deck)**

- Pattern: an extra deck monster upgrades into a named higher boss by overlay, tag-out, or by serving as the bigger boss's material
- 恐啡肽狂龙 (Dinomorphia): 狂飙霸王龙 92798873 = 钉状龙女王 48832775 + any Dinomorphia; 宝石骑士 (Gem-Knight): 亮钻 19355597 converts any face-up 宝石骑士 into any fusion ignoring conditions
- 银河 (Galaxy-Eyes): No.62 银河眼光子龙皇 31801517 → CNo.62 超银河眼光子龙皇 48348921 (three attacks, needs 银河眼光子龙 93717133 material); rank 9/10 overlays 光波刃龙 2530830 and 极光波龙 28346136
- 霍普 (Utopia): 霍普 84013237 → 霍普·翻倍 62517849 → 电光皇 56832966 (5000 ATK no-activation lock); 超升阶魔法-乌托邦之力 67517351 as the rank-up spell
- 半龙女仆 (Dragonmaid): 耀光龙女 24799107 returns to the extra deck and special summons 龙女管家 41232647 when it negates
- Implication: upgrades usually require the specific under-boss or named material (No.62 with 光子龙, Rexterm with 钉状龙女王), so removing the under-boss blocks the top; these are the boss-brick fixers that keep a small boss never dead

- **Link-Rating Climb with a Recurring Same-Name Clause**

- Pattern: a field spell or monster grants a link summon using a single same-name link as material; the re-linked monster's effect is the per-turn engine
- 转生炎兽 (Salamangreat): 圣域 1295111 re-links 日光狼 87871125 with only itself as material, turning on its ② S/T recursion; 灯火美洲豹 56003780 re-summons itself from GY into the 日光狼 zone each turn to trigger ① card gain
- 码语者 (Code Talker): 转码语者 46947713 revives a Link-3-or-lower Cyberse link from GY, so 码语者 53413628 → 转码语者 → 访问码语者 86066372 (with 更新干扰员 88093706 as the double-attack material) rebuilds every turn
- 邪恶★双子 (Live☆Twin): alternating Link-2 loop — 姬丝基勒 9205573 revives 璃拉, 璃拉 36609518 revives 姬丝基勒, each ② applying a Fiend-only extra deck lock
- 自奏圣乐 (Orcust): 伽拉忒亚 30741503 → 伽拉忒亚i 48835607 (needs an Orcust link as material) under 通天塔 90351981 turns the whole GY loop into quick effects
- Implication: hit the recursion piece, not the boss — banish the GY body (灯火美洲豹 56003780, 犰狳蜥 52277807) with 墓穴的指名者 24224830 / 屋敷童 73642296, or destroy the enabling field spell (圣域 1295111, 通天塔 90351981, 艾心乐园岛 59054773)

- **Banish-N-to-Summon-Rating-N (No Field Materials)**

- Pattern: an ignition effect on a main monster banishes N monsters from the GY and special summons a Link of rating N from the extra deck directly, with no field materials
- 铁兽战线 (Tri-Brigade): every main 铁兽 (纳贝尔 14816857, 姬特 56196385, 克拉斯 50810455, 弗拉克杜尔 87209160) banishes Beast/Beast-Warrior/Winged-Beast from GY to summon 蓝辉熊 47163170 (2) or 施莱格 99726621 (4); 铁兽的抗战 40975243 re-links from the banished pool on the opponent turn
- Implication: the GY is the field — 次元吸引者 91800273 and GY banishment kill the engine outright, and "Link Summoned" triggers never fire from this route (use the engine's own type-only link material lock as the counterplay window)

- **Attribute-Diversity Link Boss**

- Pattern: the boss's material requirement is all-different attributes, so the deck spends its climb collecting attribute coverage
- @火灵天星 (@Ignister): 电子界到临者 11738489 (Link-6, 3+ all-different attributes, ATK = materials × 1000, unaffected by other effects); 艾心乐园岛 59054773 enforces one special summon per attribute to shape the pool, and 暗骑士 97383507 revives bodies into its zones
- Implication: the summon fails on duplicate attributes — negating 暗骑士 or the field spell stalls the collection before the boss

- **The Generic Tunnel Links (Bridging Any Deck)**

- Pattern: near-material-free links and overlay tops that convert leftover bodies into a generic boss, usable by any deck that reached the right body shape
- Link-2 tunnels: S：P小夜骑士 29301450 (2 effect monsters, quick negate-and-banish), I：P百变莱娜 65741786 (opponent-turn link into 双穹之骑士 阿斯特拉姆 21887175 / 闭锁世界的冥神 98127546 / 访问码语者 86066372), and Link-1 exits 转生炎兽 独角兔 60303245 (normal-summoned ≤1000 ATK) and 连接栗子球 41999284 (any Level 1)
- Link-4 finishers: 访问码语者 86066372 (2+ effect monsters, +1000 ATK per chosen material's rating, no response to its effects), 双穹之骑士 阿斯特拉姆 21887175, 闭锁世界的冥神 98127546 (may use opponent monsters as material)
- Xyz tops: 天霆号 阿宙斯 90448279 and 灾厄之星 提·丰 93039339 overlay onto any Xyz
- Synchro tunnels: 鲜花女男爵 84815190 (Synchro 10 negate), 黑蔷薇龙 73580471, 星尘龙 44508094, 虹光之宣告者 79606837, 水晶翼同调龙 50954680, 冰结界之龙 三叉龙 52687916
- Fusion tunnel: 超融合 48130397 with 凶饿毒融合龙 41209827 steals DARK boards mid-chain
- Implication: these are what to slot when a deck lacks its own ladder (one normal summon → S：P or I：P, any Level 1 → 连接栗子球, any Xyz that attacked → 阿宙斯), and what to interrupt in opponents — deny the second body or negate the I：P activation

- **Spell-Is-the-Boss (One-Card Boss Engines)**

- Pattern: one spell or field spell produces the whole board, and the boss is the spell's effect rather than a summoned line
- 源数 (Numeron): 源数网络 41418852 copies 源数直系 77402960 to special summon four Rank 1 gates with zero materials, and its effect two lets the material-less gates activate detach effects without detaching
- 魔术师 (Pendulum Magician) / 异色眼 (Odd-Eyes): 灵摆呼唤 53208660 sets two scales from deck, then one pendulum summon is the swarm; 四天之龙 异色眼灵摆龙 75787708 is the double-damage beater
- 机壳 (Qliphort): scale 1 + scale 9 tribute into the Apoqliphort bosses 40061558 / 27279764 behind the pend-lock
- Implication: destroy or negate the spell or field spell (幽鬼兔 59438930, 双龙卷 43898403) before the Battle Phase; these engines have no follow-up if the spell is answered

- **GY-Triggered Fusion (Pile Engines)**

- Pattern: monsters fuse from the GY when sent there by an effect, so the mill chain is the summon chain with no fusion spell needed
- 珠泪哀歌族 (Tearlaments): 塞壬人鱼 572850, 小美人鱼 37961969, 梅洛人鱼 74078255 fusion summon from hand/field/GY when sent by an effect; 雷诺哈特 73956664 mills the trigger monster and 水仙女人鱼 92731385 mills 5 to restart the pile
- 影依 (Shaddoll): flip monsters' grave effects chain off the deck-fusion materials (影依融合 44394295)
- Implication: 深渊的潜伏者 21044178 shuts all GY triggers, 墓穴的指名者 24224830 / D.D.乌鸦 24508238 remove the trigger monster, 次元吸引者 91800273 blanks the engine; Ash the first mill, not the boss

- **Evaluating an Extra Deck**

- A real ladder exists if: an extra deck monster's summon condition names other extra deck monsters (伽拉忒亚i 48835607, 巨大喷流 54498517, 圣母颂歌 59242457, 电子界到临者 11738489, 阿宙斯 90448279, 未来龙皇 26973555), if a card summons an extra deck monster ignoring conditions (亮钻 19355597, 红龙 63436931, 未来皇 65305468, 丁吉尔苏 93854893, 铁兽的抗战 40975243), or if the main deck has a level-fixer or tuner swarm pointed at one sum (相剑 token, 镜魔师 9396662, 机巧蛤蟆 75690317, 魔妖妲姬 42542842)
- A deck has only generic one-offs if: every extra deck monster is a 2-material box with no cross-references, no overlay or tag-upgrade, and no GY recursion of materials — the deck converts bodies one-for-one and a single interruption ends the board
- Material-pile payoffs (阿宙斯 wipe, 到临者 ATK, 未来龙皇 negate count, 嵌合超载龙 64599569 attacks per material) signal the ladder is real: the pile itself is the resource, so material and GY protection matter more than the boss's effect
- When slotting: a deck without a ladder takes the generic tunnel (I：P 65741786 → 双穹 21887175 / 冥神 98127546, S：P 29301450, 阿宙斯 90448279, 鲜花女男爵 84815190); a deck with a ladder needs its under-bosses and recursion pieces (2× each link in Live☆Twin, one 龙女管家 41232647 per 耀光 24799107, 伽拉忒亚 30741503 for the 丁吉尔苏 overlay) more than extra generic tops
- Interruption recap across all docs: 灰流丽 14558127 on the first search or deck-send, 墓穴的指名者 24224830 / 屋敷童 73642296 on GY recursion, 无限泡影 10045474 / 效果遮蒙者 97268402 on the engine hub or level-fixer, 增殖的G 23434538 on swarm ladders (the doc baseline stops at 2-3 summons), 尼比鲁 27204311 on the 5th summon, and 次元吸引者 91800273 for any GY-geometry deck
