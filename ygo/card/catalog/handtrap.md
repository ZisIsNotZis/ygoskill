# Hand Trap Cards

Definition: activates from HAND during OPPONENT'S TURN to INTERFERE. All three required: from hand, opponent turn, interference. Excludes self-summon only, search only, or field-dependent hand activations.

- **Universal Hand Traps**

- 灰流丽 14558127 lim2, 3炎不死, hand discard negate search/special summon/mill, counters search and mill heavy decks, limited to 2 copies, vulnerable to pointer cards, useless against pure draw decks, typical count 3
- 増殖するG 23434538 lim1, 2地昆虫, hand send to grave grants draw each time opponent special summons, counters expand heavy decks, limited to 1 copy, weak against low special summon decks, typical count 2-3
- 小丑与锁鸟 94145021 lim1, 1风魔导师, hand send to grave prevents both players from searching rest of turn, counters search heavy decks like Swordsoul or Labrynth, limited to 1 copy, affects both players equally, useless against non-search decks, typical count 1
- 无限泡影 10045474, normal trap, negate 1 face-up monster effect on field, can activate from hand during opponent turn if your field has no cards, dual use as hand trap or set trap, requires empty field for hand activation, typical count 1-3
- 效果遮蒙者 97268402, 1光魔导师, hand send to grave negate monster effect, only during opponent main phase not quick effects, typical count 1-2
- 幽鬼うさぎ 59438930, 3光念动力, hand send to grave destroy the card whose effect activated, destroys but does not negate, effective against field spells and continuous cards, typical count 1-2
- 屋敷童 73642296, 3不死地, hand discard negate graveyard effect activation, only graveyard effects not field effects, effective against graveyard reliant decks, typical count 1-2
- D.D.クロウ 24508238, 1暗鳥獣, hand discard banish 1 card from opponent graveyard, banishes but does not negate, may need 2 copies for key targets, typical count 1-2
- 浮幽さくら 62015408, 3不死暗, hand discard banish from opponent extra deck, only extra deck monsters, effective against extra deck reliant strategies, typical count 1
- 原始生命态ニビル 27204311, 11光岩石, hand special summon yourself and clear opponent field after opponent special summons 5 or more times, useless if opponent special summons fewer than 5 times, can accidentally trigger on your own turn, typical count 1
- 次元吸引者 91800273 lim1, 6暗魔導師, hand send to grave makes next turn sends go to banish instead, limited to 1 copy, affects your own plays too, conflicts with mill and graveyard strategies, typical count 1
- 朱光の宣告者 17266660 lim1, 2光天使, hand plus an angel from hand negate effect, limited to 1 copy, requires angel in hand, typical count 1
